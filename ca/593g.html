§ 593g

Possessing tree-spiking materials

Up to 1 year Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 15 · Malicious Injuries to Railroad Bridges, Highways, Bridges, and Telegraphs

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every person who, with the intent to use it in a violation of Section 593a, possesses any iron, steel, ceramic, or other substance sufficiently hard to injure saws or wood manufacturing or processing equipment, shall be punished by imprisonment in the county jail not to exceed one year.

This section shall only become operative if Senate Bill 1176 of the 1987–88 Regular Session of the Legislature is enacted and becomes effective on or before January 1, 1988.

History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1414, Sec. 1. Note: SB 1176 was enacted as Stats. 1987, Ch. 1132.

Simplified explanation

Possessing iron, steel, ceramic, or other hard substances that can damage saws or wood processing equipment, intending to use them to spike trees or logs in violation of Section 593a, is punishable by up to one year in county jail.
