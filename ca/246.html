§ 246

Shooting at an inhabited dwelling or vehicle

Up to 7 years Weapons & Terrorism Ch. 9 · Assault and Battery

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Any person who shall and discharge a firearm at an inhabited dwelling house, occupied building, occupied motor vehicle, occupied aircraft, inhabited housecar, as defined in Section 362 of the Vehicle Code, or inhabited camper, as defined in Section 243 of the Vehicle Code, is guilty of a , and upon conviction shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for three, five, or seven years, or by imprisonment in the county jail for a term of not less than six months and not exceeding one year.

As used in this section, “inhabited” means currently being used for dwelling purposes, whether occupied or not.

History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 911, Sec. 1. Effective September 15, 1988.

Simplified explanation

Maliciously and willfully discharging a firearm at an inhabited dwelling, occupied building, vehicle, or aircraft is a felony punishable by three, five, or seven years in state prison.
