§ 539

False certification of community service

See penalty details Courts & Justice Title 13, Ch. 8 · False Personation and Cheats

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every person who, with the intent to defraud, certifies that a person ordered by the court to participate in community service as a condition of probation has completed the number of hours of community service prescribed in the court order and the participant has not completed the prescribed number of hours, is guilty of a .

History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 371, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.

Simplified explanation

Certifying, with intent to defraud, that someone ordered to do community service as a probation condition finished the required hours when they did not is a misdemeanor; no term is stated here.
