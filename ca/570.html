§ 570

Punishment for unlawful subleasing of vehicles

Up to 1 year+ Fraud & Money Title 13, Ch. 12.7 · Unlawful Subleasing of Motor Vehicles

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

An act of unlawful subleasing of a motor vehicle, as defined in Section 571, shall be punishable by imprisonment in a county jail for not more than one year, or by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170, or by a fine of not more than ten thousand dollars ($10,000), or by both that fine and imprisonment.

History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 397. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68.
