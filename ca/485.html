§ 485

Keeping lost property

See penalty details+ Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 5 · Larceny

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

One who finds lost property under circumstances which give him knowledge of or means of inquiry as to the true owner, and who appropriates such property to his own use, or to the use of another person not entitled thereto, without first making reasonable and just efforts to find the owner and to restore the property to him, is guilty of theft.

History: Amended by Stats. 1927, Ch. 619.

1 crime in this section

  1. Finds lost property and appropriates such property to his own use, or to the use of another person not entitled thereto

    Ways to commit
    • to his own use · to the use of another person not entitled thereto
    Conditions
    • The property is found under circumstances which give him knowledge of or means of inquiry as to the true owner
    • Without first making reasonable and just efforts to find the owner and to restore the property to him
    Penalty
    • Guilty of theft (no punishment is stated in this section)

Simplified explanation

A person who finds lost property while knowing or able to learn who owns it, and keeps it without first making reasonable efforts to find and return it to the owner, is guilty of theft.
