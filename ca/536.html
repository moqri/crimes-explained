§ 536

False accounts by commission merchants

Up to 6 months Fraud & Money Title 13, Ch. 8 · False Personation and Cheats

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every commission merchant, broker, agent, factor, or consignee, who shall and make, or cause to be made, to the principal or consignor of such commission merchant, agent, broker, factor, or consignee, a false statement as to the price obtained for any property consigned or entrusted for sale, or as to the quality or quantity of any property so consigned or entrusted, or as to any expenditures made in connection therewith, shall be deemed guilty of a , and on conviction thereof, shall be punished by fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000) and not less than two hundred dollars ($200), or by imprisonment in the county jail not exceeding six months and not less than 10 days, or by both such fine and imprisonment.

History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 299. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.

Simplified explanation

A commission merchant, broker, agent, factor, or consignee who willfully and corruptly gives the owner a false statement about the sale price, quality, quantity, or expenses of consigned property is guilty of a misdemeanor, up to six months in county jail.
