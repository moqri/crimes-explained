§ 466.6

Ignition key making records

See penalty details Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 3 · Burglarious and Larcenous Instruments and Deadly Weapons

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Any person who makes a key capable of operating the ignition of a motor vehicle or personal property registered under the Vehicle Code for another by any method other than by the duplication of an existing key, whether or not for compensation, shall obtain the name, address, telephone number, if any, date of birth, and driver’s license number or identification number of the person requesting or purchasing the key; and the registration or identification number, license number, year, make, model, color, and vehicle identification number of the vehicle or personal property registered under the Vehicle Code for which the key is to be made. Such information, together with the date the key was made and the signature of the person for whom the key was made, shall be set forth on a work order. A copy of each such work order shall be retained for two years, shall include the name and permit number of the locksmith performing the service, and shall be open to inspection by any peace officer or by the Bureau of Collection and Investigative Services during business hours or submitted to the bureau upon request.

Any person who violates any provision of this subdivision is guilty of a .

(b) The provisions of this section shall include, but are not limited to, the making of a key from key codes or impressions.

(c) Nothing contained in this section shall be construed to prohibit the duplication of any key for a motor vehicle from another key.

History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1135, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1993.

1 crime in this section

  1. (a) Makes a key capable of operating the ignition of a motor vehicle or personal property registered under the Vehicle Code for another by any method other than by the duplication of an existing key, and violates any provision of this subdivision

    Conditions
    • The person fails to obtain the requester's name, address, telephone number, date of birth, and driver's license or identification number and the vehicle's registration or identification number, license number, year, make, model, color, and vehicle identification number; to set this out with the date and the requester's signature on a work order including the locksmith's name and permit number; or to retain a copy for two years open to inspection by any peace officer or the Bureau of Collection and Investigative Services
    • Whether or not for compensation; includes making a key from key codes or impressions
    Exceptions
    • Duplication of any key for a motor vehicle from another key
    Penalty
    • A misdemeanor (no term is stated in this section)

Simplified explanation

A person who makes a vehicle ignition key for someone else other than by duplicating an existing key must record the customer's and vehicle's details on a work order kept for two years; violating this is a misdemeanor.
