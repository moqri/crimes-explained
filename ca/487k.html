§ 487k

Theft of agricultural equipment

See penalty details+ Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 5 · Larceny

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person who steals, takes, or carries away tractors, all-terrain vehicles, or other agricultural equipment, or any portion thereof, used in the acquisition or production of food for public consumption, which are of a value exceeding nine hundred fifty dollars ($950), is guilty of grand theft.

History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 119, Sec. 1. (SB 224) Effective January 1, 2020.

Stealing tractors, all-terrain vehicles, or other agricultural equipment used to produce food for the public, worth more than $950, is grand theft.
