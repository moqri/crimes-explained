§ 499b

Joyriding on a bicycle or vessel

Up to 1 year Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 5 · Larceny

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Any person who shall, without the permission of the owner thereof, take any bicycle for the purpose of temporarily using or operating the same, is guilty of a , and shall be punishable by a fine not exceeding four hundred dollars ($400), or by imprisonment in a county jail not exceeding three months, or by both that fine and imprisonment.

(b) Any person who shall, without the permission of the owner thereof, take any vessel for the purpose of temporarily using or operating the same, is guilty of a , and shall be punishable by a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000), or by imprisonment in a county jail not exceeding one year, or by both that fine and imprisonment.

History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 391, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.

Simplified explanation

Taking someone's bicycle or vessel without permission to use it temporarily is a misdemeanor, punishable by up to three months in county jail for a bicycle or up to one year for a vessel.
