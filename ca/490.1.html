§ 490.1

Petty theft of $50 or less as infraction

Fine only+ Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 5 · Larceny

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Petty theft, where the value of the money, labor, real or personal property taken is of a value which does not exceed fifty dollars ($50), may be charged as a or an infraction, at the discretion of the prosecutor, provided that the person charged with the offense has no other theft or theft-related conviction.

(b) Any offense charged as an infraction under this section shall be subject to the provisions of subdivision (d) of Section 17 and Sections 19.6 and 19.7.

A violation which is an infraction under this section is punishable by a fine not exceeding two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 638, Sec. 2.

    Conditions

    Penalty
    • May be charged as a misdemeanor or an infraction, at the discretion of the prosecutor (no misdemeanor term is stated in this section)
    • If charged as an infraction, provided that the person charged has no other theft or theft-related conviction: A fine not exceeding two hundred fifty dollars ($250)

Simplified explanation

Petty theft of $50 or less may be charged as a misdemeanor or, if the person has no other theft conviction, as an infraction punishable by a fine up to $250.
