§ 452.1

Unlawful fire sentence enhancements

Up to 3 years+ Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 1 · Arson

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Notwithstanding any other law, any person who is convicted of a violation of Section 452 shall be punished by a one-, two-, or three-year enhancement for each of the following circumstances that is found to be true:

(1) The defendant has been previously convicted of a violation of Section 451 or 452.

(2) A firefighter, peace officer, or other emergency personnel suffered as a result of the offense. The additional term provided by this subdivision shall be imposed whenever applicable, including any instance in which there is a violation of subdivision (a) of Section 452.

(3) The defendant proximately caused to more than one victim in any single violation of Section 452. The additional term provided by this subdivision shall be imposed whenever applicable, including any instance in which there is a violation of subdivision (a) of Section 452.

(4) The defendant proximately caused multiple structures to burn in any single violation of Section 452.

(b) The additional term specified in subdivision (a) of Section 452.1 shall not be imposed unless the existence of any fact required under this section shall be alleged in the accusatory pleading and either admitted by the defendant in open court or found to be true by the trier of fact.

History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 421, Sec. 4. Effective September 7, 1994.

1 crime in this section

  1. (a) Is convicted of a felony violation of Section 452

    Conditions
    • Any of the following is found to be true (one enhancement for each): the defendant has been previously convicted of a felony violation of Section 451 or 452; a firefighter, peace officer, or other emergency personnel suffered great bodily injury as a result of the offense; the defendant proximately caused great bodily injury to more than one victim in any single violation of Section 452; the defendant proximately caused multiple structures to burn in any single violation of Section 452
    Penalty
    • A one-, two-, or three-year enhancement for each circumstance found to be true
    Other consequences
    • The additional term shall not be imposed unless the facts are alleged in the accusatory pleading and either admitted by the defendant in open court or found to be true by the trier of fact

Simplified explanation

A person convicted of felony unlawfully causing a fire under Section 452 gets an extra one, two, or three years for each listed circumstance, such as a prior arson conviction or injured firefighters.
