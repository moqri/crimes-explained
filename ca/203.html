§ 203

Mayhem defined

See penalty details Violence & Threats Ch. 2 · Mayhem

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every person who unlawfully and deprives a human being of a member of his body, or disables, disfigures, or renders it useless, or cuts or disables the tongue, or puts out an eye, or slits the nose, ear, or lip, is guilty of mayhem.

History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1360, Sec. 106.

1 crime in this section

  1. Deprives a human being of a member of his body, or disables, disfigures, or renders it useless, or cuts or disables the tongue, or puts out an eye, or slits the nose, ear, or lip

    Ways to commit
    • Deprives a human being of a member of his body · Disables, disfigures, or renders it useless · Cuts or disables the tongue · Puts out an eye · Slits the nose, ear, or lip
    Intent
    • Unlawfully and maliciously
    Penalty
    • No penalty is stated in this section

Simplified explanation

Mayhem is unlawfully and maliciously depriving a person of a body member or disabling, disfiguring, or rendering it useless; this section states no penalty.
