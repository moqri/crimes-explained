§ 558.1

Punishment for Scripps lands trespass

Up to 29 days Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 12 · Unlawful Interference With Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every person who violates any of the provisions of Section 558 is guilty of a and upon conviction thereof shall be punished by a fine of not more than six hundred dollars ($600) or by imprisonment for not more than 30 days, or by both such fine and imprisonment.

History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 304. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.

1 crime in this section

  1. Violates any of the provisions of Section 558

    Penalty
    • Guilty of a misdemeanor and shall be punished by a fine of not more than six hundred dollars ($600) or by imprisonment for not more than 30 days, or by both such fine and imprisonment

Simplified explanation

Violating Section 558, which bars unauthorized entry onto the Scripps Institution of Oceanography lands, is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine up to $600, up to 30 days' imprisonment, or both.
