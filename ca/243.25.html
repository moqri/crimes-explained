§ 243.25

Battery on an elder or dependent adult

Up to 1 year Violence & Threats Ch. 9 · Assault and Battery

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

When a battery is committed against the person of an elder or a dependent adult as defined in Section 368, with knowledge that he or she is an elder or a dependent adult, the offense shall be punishable by a fine not to exceed two thousand dollars ($2,000), or by imprisonment in a county jail not to exceed one year, or by both that fine and imprisonment.

History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 369, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.

1 crime in this section

  1. Commits a battery against the person of an elder or a dependent adult

    Knowledge
    • With knowledge that he or she is an elder or a dependent adult
    Penalty
    • A fine not to exceed two thousand dollars ($2,000), or imprisonment in a county jail not to exceed one year, or both that fine and imprisonment

Simplified explanation

Battery on an elder or dependent adult, knowing of that status, is punishable by a fine up to $2,000, county jail up to one year, or both.
