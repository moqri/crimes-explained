§ 204

Punishment for mayhem

Up to 8 years Violence & Threats Title 8, Ch. 2 · Mayhem

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Mayhem is punishable by imprisonment in the state prison for two, four, or eight years.

History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1424, Sec. 1.
