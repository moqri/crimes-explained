§ 451

Arson

Up to 9 years Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 1 · Arson

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of arson when he or she and sets fire to or burns or causes to be burned or who aids, counsels, or procures the burning of, any structure, forest land, or property.

(a) Arson that causes is a punishable by imprisonment in the state prison for five, seven, or nine years.

(b) Arson that causes an inhabited structure or inhabited property to burn is a punishable by imprisonment in the state prison for three, five, or eight years.

(c) Arson of a structure or forest land is a punishable by imprisonment in the state prison for two, four, or six years.

(d) Arson of property is a punishable by imprisonment in the state prison for 16 months, two, or three years. For purposes of this paragraph, arson of property does not include one burning or causing to be burned his or her own personal property unless there is an intent to defraud or there is injury to another person or another person’s structure, forest land, or property.

(e) In the case of any person convicted of violating this section while confined in a state prison, prison road camp, prison forestry camp, or other prison camp or prison farm, or while confined in a county jail while serving a term of imprisonment for a or conviction, any sentence imposed shall be consecutive to the sentence for which the person was then confined.

History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 421, Sec. 1. Effective September 7, 1994.

1 crime in this section

  1. Sets fire to or burns or causes to be burned or aids, counsels, or procures the burning of, any structure, forest land, or property

    Ways to commit
    • sets fire to · burns · causes to be burned · aids, counsels, or procures the burning of
    Mental state
    • Willfully and maliciously
    Exceptions
    • Arson of property does not include one burning or causing to be burned his or her own personal property unless there is an intent to defraud or there is injury to another person or another person's structure, forest land, or property
    Penalty
    • Arson of property: a felony punishable by imprisonment in the state prison for 16 months, two, or three years
    • If the arson is of a structure or forest land: A felony punishable by imprisonment in the state prison for two, four, or six years
    • If the arson causes an inhabited structure or inhabited property to burn: A felony punishable by imprisonment in the state prison for three, five, or eight years
    • If the arson causes great bodily injury: A felony punishable by imprisonment in the state prison for five, seven, or nine years
    Other consequences
    • If convicted while confined in a state prison, prison camp or farm, or in a county jail serving a felony or misdemeanor term, the sentence shall be consecutive to the sentence then being served

Simplified explanation

Arson is willfully and maliciously setting fire to or burning any structure, forest land, or property, or helping to do so; it is a felony with up to nine years in state prison when it causes great bodily injury.
