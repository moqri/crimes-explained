§ 532e

Construction fund kickbacks

See penalty details Fraud & Money Title 13, Ch. 8 · False Personation and Cheats

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Any person who receives money for the purpose of obtaining or paying for services, labor, materials or equipment incident to constructing improvements on real property and rebates any part of the money to or on behalf of anyone contracting with such person, for provision of the services, labor, materials or equipment for which the money was given, shall be guilty of a ; provided, however, that normal trade discount for prompt payment shall not be considered a violation of this section.

History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1145.

1 crime in this section

  1. Rebates any part of the money to or on behalf of anyone contracting with such person, for provision of the services, labor, materials or equipment for which the money was given

    Mental state
    • Willfully
    Exceptions

    Penalty
    • Guilty of a misdemeanor (no term is stated in this section)

Simplified explanation

A person who receives money to pay for construction work on real property and willfully rebates part of it to anyone contracting to provide that work is guilty of a misdemeanor.
