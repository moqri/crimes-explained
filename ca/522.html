§ 522

Obtaining a signature by extortion

See penalty details+ Fraud & Money Title 13, Ch. 7 · Extortion

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every person who, by any extortionate means, obtains from another his signature to any paper or instrument, whereby, if such signature were freely given, any property would be transferred, or any debt, demand, charge, or right of action created, is punishable in the same manner as if the actual delivery of such debt, demand, charge, or right of action were obtained.

History: Enacted 1872.

1 crime in this section

  1. Obtains from another his signature to any paper or instrument by any extortionate means

    Conditions
    • If such signature were freely given, any property would be transferred, or any debt, demand, charge, or right of action created
    Penalty
    • Punishable in the same manner as if the actual delivery of such debt, demand, charge, or right of action were obtained (no punishment is stated in this section)

Simplified explanation

Using extortionate means to get someone's signature on a paper that would transfer property or create a debt is punished as if the debt or right itself were obtained; no term is stated here.
