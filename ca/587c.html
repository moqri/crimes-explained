§ 587c

Railroad fare evasion

Up to 6 months Fraud & Money Title 13, Ch. 15 · Malicious Injuries to Railroad Bridges, Highways, Bridges, and Telegraphs

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every person who fraudulently evades, or to evade the payment of his fare, while traveling upon any railroad, shall be deemed guilty of a , and upon conviction thereof, shall be punished by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars, or imprisonment not exceeding six months, or by both such fine and imprisonment.

History: Added by Stats. 1909, Ch. 345.

Simplified explanation

Fraudulently evading, or attempting to evade, paying one's fare while traveling on a railroad is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine up to $500, up to six months' imprisonment, or both.
