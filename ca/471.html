§ 471

Forging record entries

See penalty details+ Fraud & Money Title 13, Ch. 4 · Forgery and Counterfeiting

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every person who, with intent to defraud another, makes, forges, or alters any entry in any book of records, or any instrument purporting to be any record or return specified in Section 470, is guilty of forgery.

History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 787, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2003.

Simplified explanation

Making, forging, or altering an entry in a book of records, or a record or return named in Section 470, with intent to defraud is forgery; the punishment is set in Section 473.
