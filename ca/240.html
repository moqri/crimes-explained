§ 240

Assault defined

See penalty details Violence & Threats Ch. 9 · Assault and Battery

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

An assault is an unlawful attempt, coupled with a present ability, to commit a violent injury on the person of another.

History: Enacted 1872.

1 crime in this section

  1. Makes an unlawful attempt, coupled with a present ability, to commit a violent injury on the person of another

    Conditions
    • The person has a present ability to commit the violent injury
    Penalty
    • Set in Section 241

Simplified explanation

An assault is an unlawful attempt, coupled with a present ability, to commit a violent injury on another person; the section sets no punishment itself.
