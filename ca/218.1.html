§ 218.1

Obstructing railroad track with gross negligence

Up to 4 years+ Transportation & Aviation Title 8, Ch. 5 · Attempts to Kill

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Any person who unlawfully and with gross negligence places or causes to be placed any obstruction upon or near the track of any railroad that proximately results in either the damaging or derailing of any passenger, freight, or other train, or injures a rail passenger or employee, shall be punished by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170 for two, three, or four years, or by imprisonment in a county jail for not more than one year, or by a fine not to exceed two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500), or by both that imprisonment and fine.

History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 286. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68.

Simplified explanation

Unlawfully and with gross negligence placing an obstruction on a railroad track that damages or derails a train or injures a passenger or employee is punishable in county jail, under Section 1170(h), or by fine.
