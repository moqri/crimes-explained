§ 478

Punishment for counterfeiting

Up to 4 years+ Fraud & Money Title 13, Ch. 4 · Forgery and Counterfeiting

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Counterfeiting is punishable by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170 for two, three or four years.

History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 362. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68.

1 crime in this section

  1. Commits counterfeiting

Simplified explanation

Counterfeiting is punishable by two, three, or four years of imprisonment under subdivision (h) of Section 1170.
