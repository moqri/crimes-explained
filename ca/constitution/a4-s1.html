Art. IV, § 1

Legislative power; initiative and referendum

Power The Legislature Art. IV · Legislative

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The legislative power of this State is vested in the California Legislature which consists of the Senate and Assembly, but the people reserve to themselves the powers of initiative and referendum.

History: Sec. 1 added Nov. 8, 1966, by Prop. 1-a. Res.Ch. 139, 1966 1st Ex. Sess.
