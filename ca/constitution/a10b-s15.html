Art. X B, § 15

Other wildlife closures not preempted

Structure Water & Natural Resources Art. X B · Marine Resources Protection Act of 1990

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

This article does not preempt or supersede any other closures to protect any other wildlife, including sea otters, whales, and shorebirds.

History: Sec. 15 added Nov. 6, 1990, by Prop. 132. Initiative measure.
