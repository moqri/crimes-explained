Art. I, § 26

Constitution is mandatory and prohibitory

Structure Founding & Purposes Art. I · Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The provisions of this Constitution are mandatory and prohibitory, unless by express words they are declared to be otherwise.

History: Sec. 26 renumbered from Sec. 28 on June 8, 1976, by Prop. 14. Res.Ch. 5, 1976.
