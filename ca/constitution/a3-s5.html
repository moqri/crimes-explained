Art. III, § 5

Suits against the State

Structure The Courts Art. III · State of California

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Suits may be brought against the State in such manner and in such courts as shall be directed by law.

History: Sec. 5 added Nov. 7, 1972, by Prop. 6. Res.Ch. 120, 1972.
