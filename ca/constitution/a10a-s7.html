Art. X A, § 7

State agencies must honor water protections

Duty Water & Natural Resources Art. X A · Water Resources Development

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

State agencies shall exercise their authorized powers in a manner consistent with the protections provided by this article.

History: Sec. 7 added Nov. 4, 1980, by Prop. 8. Res.Ch. 49, 1980. No force or effect.
