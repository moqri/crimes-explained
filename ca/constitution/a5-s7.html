Art. V, § 7

Governor as commander in chief of the militia

Power The Governor & Executive Art. V · Executive

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The Governor is commander in chief of a militia that shall be provided by statute. The Governor may call it forth to execute the law.

History: Sec. 7 amended Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 11. Res.Ch. 96, 1974.
