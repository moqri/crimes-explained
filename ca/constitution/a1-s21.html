Art. I, § 21

Separate property

Structure Individual Rights Art. I · Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Property owned before marriage or acquired during marriage by gift, will, or inheritance is separate property.

History: Sec. 21 renumbered from Sec. 8 (of Art. 20) on Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 7. Res.Ch. 90, 1974.
