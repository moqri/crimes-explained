Art. XVI, § 7

Money drawn only by appropriation and warrant

Limit Taxes, Money & Commerce Art. XVI · Public Finance

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Money may be drawn from the Treasury only through an appropriation made by law and upon a Controller’s duly drawn warrant.

History: Sec. 7 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 8. Res.Ch. 70, 1974.

Simplified explanation

Money may be drawn from the Treasury only through an appropriation made by law and upon a warrant duly drawn by the Controller.
