Art. V, § 6

Reorganizing executive functions

Power The Governor & Executive Art. V · Executive

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Authority may be provided by statute for the Governor to assign and reorganize functions among executive officers and agencies and their employees, other than elective officers and agencies administered by elective officers.

History: Sec. 6 added Nov. 8, 1966, by Prop. 1-a. Res.Ch. 139, 1966 1st Ex. Sess.

Simplified explanation

A statute may give the Governor authority to assign and reorganize functions among executive officers and agencies and their employees, other than elective officers and agencies they administer.
