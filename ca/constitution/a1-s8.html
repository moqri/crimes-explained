Art. I, § 8

No job disqualification for sex, race, creed

Limit Equality & Citizenship Art. I · Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

A person may not be disqualified from entering or pursuing a business, profession, vocation, or employment because of sex, race, creed, color, or national or ethnic origin.

History: Sec. 8 renumbered from Sec. 18 (of Art. 20) on Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 7. Res.Ch. 90, 1974.
