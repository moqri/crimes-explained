Art. XI, § 12

Claims against counties and cities

Power Local Government Art. XI · Local Government

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The Legislature may prescribe procedure for presentation, consideration, and enforcement of claims against counties, cities, their officers, agents, or employees.

History: Sec. 12 added June 2, 1970, by Prop. 2. Res.Ch. 331, 1969.
