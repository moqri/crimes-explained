Art. II, § 17

Recall of Governor or Secretary of State

Duty Voting & Elections Art. II · Voting, Initiative and Referendum, and Recall

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

If recall of the Governor or Secretary of State is initiated, the recall duties of that office shall be performed by the Lieutenant Governor or Controller, respectively.

History: Sec. 17 added June 8, 1976, by Prop. 14. Res.Ch. 5, 1976.
