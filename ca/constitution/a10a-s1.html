Art. X A, § 1

Purpose of water protections

Structure Water & Natural Resources Art. X A · Water Resources Development

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The people of the State hereby provide the following guarantees and protections in this article for water rights, water quality, and fish and wildlife resources.

History: Sec. 1 added Nov. 4, 1980, by Prop. 8. Res.Ch. 49, 1980. No force or effect.
