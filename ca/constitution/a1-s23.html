Art. I, § 23

Grand juries

Duty Criminal Justice Art. I · Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

One or more grand juries shall be drawn and summoned at least once a year in each county.

History: Sec. 23 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 7. Res.Ch. 90, 1974.
