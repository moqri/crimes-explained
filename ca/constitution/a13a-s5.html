Art. XIII A, § 5

When the tax limitation article takes effect

Structure Taxes, Money & Commerce Art. XIII A · Tax Limitation

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

This article shall take effect for the tax year beginning on July 1 following the passage of this Amendment, except Section 3 which shall become effective upon the passage of this article.

History: Sec. 5 added June 6, 1978, by Prop. 13. Initiative measure.

Simplified explanation

This article takes effect for the tax year beginning on July 1 following its passage, except Section 3, which becomes effective upon the passage of the article.
