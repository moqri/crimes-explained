Art. V, § 1

Executive power vested in the Governor

Power The Governor & Executive Art. V · Executive

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The supreme executive power of this State is vested in the Governor. The Governor shall see that the law is faithfully executed.

History: Sec. 1 amended Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 11. Res.Ch. 96, 1974.
