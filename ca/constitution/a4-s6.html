Art. IV, § 6

Senate and Assembly districts

Structure Voting & Elections Art. IV · Legislative

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

For the purpose of choosing members of the Legislature, the State shall be divided into 40 Senatorial and 80 Assembly districts to be called Senatorial and Assembly Districts. Each Senatorial district shall choose one Senator and each Assembly district shall choose one member of the Assembly.

History: Sec. 6 added June 3, 1980, by Prop. 6. Res.Ch. 78, 1978.

Simplified explanation

For choosing members of the Legislature, the State is divided into 40 Senatorial and 80 Assembly districts; each Senatorial district chooses one Senator and each Assembly district one member of the Assembly.
