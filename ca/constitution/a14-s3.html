Art. XIV, § 3

Mechanics' and laborers' liens

Right Labor Art. XIV · Labor Relations

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Mechanics, persons furnishing materials, artisans, and laborers of every class, shall have a lien upon the property upon which they have bestowed labor or furnished material for the value of such labor done and material furnished; and the Legislature shall provide, by law, for the speedy and efficient enforcement of such liens.

History: Sec. 3 added June 8, 1976, by Prop. 14. Res.Ch. 5, 1976.

Simplified explanation

Mechanics, material suppliers, artisans and laborers have a lien on the property they worked on or supplied, for the value of the labor and material. The Legislature must provide by law for speedy and efficient enforcement of these liens.
