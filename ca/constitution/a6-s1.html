Art. VI, § 1

Judicial power of the State

Power The Courts Art. VI · Judicial

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The judicial power of this State is vested in the Supreme Court, courts of appeal, and superior courts, all of which are courts of record.

History: Sec. 1 amended Nov. 5, 2002, by Prop. 48. Res.Ch. 88, 2002.
