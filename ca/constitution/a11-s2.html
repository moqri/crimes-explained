Art. XI, § 2

Cities

Limit Local Government Art. XI · Local Government

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

(a) The Legislature shall prescribe uniform procedure for city formation and provide for city powers.

(b) Except with approval by a majority of its voting on the question, a city may not be annexed to or consolidated into another.

History: Sec. 2 added June 2, 1970, by Prop. 2. Res.Ch. 331, 1969.

Simplified explanation

The Legislature shall prescribe uniform procedure for forming cities and provide for city powers. A city may not be annexed to or consolidated into another without approval by a majority of its electors voting on the question.
