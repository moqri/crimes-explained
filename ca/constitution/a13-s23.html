Art. XIII, § 23

Taxing property after boundary changes

Duty Taxes, Money & Commerce Art. XIII · Taxation

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

If state boundaries change, the Legislature shall determine how property affected shall be taxed.

History: Sec. 23 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 8. Res.Ch. 70, 1974.

Simplified explanation

If the state's boundaries change, the Legislature must determine how the affected property is taxed.
