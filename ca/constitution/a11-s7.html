Art. XI, § 7

Local ordinances and regulations

Power Local Government Art. XI · Local Government

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

A county or city may make and enforce within its limits all local, police, sanitary, and other ordinances and regulations not in conflict with general laws.

History: Sec. 7 added June 2, 1970, by Prop. 2. Res.Ch. 331, 1969.
