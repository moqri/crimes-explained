Art. I, § 11

Habeas corpus

Limit Criminal Justice Art. I · Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Habeas corpus may not be suspended unless required by public safety in cases of rebellion or invasion.

History: Sec. 11 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 7. Res.Ch. 90, 1974.
