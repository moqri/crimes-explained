Art. XIII A, § 4

Local special taxes by two-thirds vote

Power Taxes, Money & Commerce Art. XIII A · Tax Limitation

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Cities, Counties and special districts, by a two-thirds vote of the qualified of such district, may impose special taxes on such district, except ad valorem taxes on real property or a transaction tax or sales tax on the sale of real property within such City, County or special district.

History: Sec. 4 added June 6, 1978, by Prop. 13. Initiative measure.

Simplified explanation

Cities, counties and special districts may impose special taxes by a two-thirds vote of their qualified electors, except ad valorem taxes on real property or transaction or sales taxes on the sale of real property.
