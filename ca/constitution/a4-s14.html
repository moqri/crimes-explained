Art. IV, § 14

Legislators exempt from civil process during session

Right The Legislature Art. IV · Legislative

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

A member of the Legislature is not subject to civil process during a session of the Legislature or for 5 days before and after a session.

History: Sec. 14 added Nov. 8, 1966, by Prop. 1-a. Res.Ch. 139, 1966 1st Ex. Sess.
