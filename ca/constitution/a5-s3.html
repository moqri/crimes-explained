Art. V, § 3

Governor's yearly report to the Legislature

Duty The Governor & Executive Art. V · Executive

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The Governor shall report to the Legislature each calendar year on the condition of the State and may make recommendations.

History: Sec. 3 amended Nov. 7, 1972, by Prop. 4. Res.Ch. 81, 1972.
