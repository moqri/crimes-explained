Art. VII, § 5

Temporary appointments

Limit Public Officers & Employees Art. VII · Public Officers and Employees

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

A temporary appointment may be made to a position for which there is no employment list. No person may serve in one or more positions under temporary appointment longer than 9 months in 12 consecutive months.

History: Sec. 5 added June 8, 1976, by Prop. 14. Res.Ch. 5, 1976.

Simplified explanation

A temporary appointment may be made to a position with no employment list, but no one may serve under temporary appointment longer than 9 months in 12 consecutive months.
