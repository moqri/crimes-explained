Art. II, § 19

Recall of local officers

Duty Local Government Art. II · Voting, Initiative and Referendum, and Recall

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The Legislature shall provide for recall of local officers. This section does not affect counties and cities whose charters provide for recall.

History: Sec. 19 added June 8, 1976, by Prop. 14. Res.Ch. 5, 1976.
