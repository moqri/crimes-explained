Art. I, § 13

Searches, seizures and warrants

Right Individual Rights Art. I · Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The right of the people to be secure in their persons, houses, papers, and effects against unreasonable seizures and searches may not be violated; and a warrant may not issue except on , supported by oath or , particularly describing the place to be searched and the persons and things to be seized.
