Art. IX, § 7.5

Textbooks for grades one through eight

Duty Education Art. IX · Education

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The State Board of Education shall adopt textbooks for use in grades one through eight throughout the State, to be furnished without cost as provided by statute.

History: Sec. 7.5 added June 2, 1970, by Prop. 6. Res.Ch. 358, 1969.
