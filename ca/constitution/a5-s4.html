Art. V, § 4

Governor may require information

Power The Governor & Executive Art. V · Executive

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The Governor may require executive officers and agencies and their employees to furnish information relating to their duties.

History: Sec. 4 added Nov. 8, 1966, by Prop. 1-a. Res.Ch. 139, 1966 1st Ex. Sess.
