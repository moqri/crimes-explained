Art. I, § 22

No property qualification to vote or hold office

Limit Voting & Elections Art. I · Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The right to vote or hold office may not be conditioned by a property qualification.

History: Sec. 22 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 7. Res.Ch. 90, 1974.
