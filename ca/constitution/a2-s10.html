Art. II, § 10

When measures take effect; amendment; petitions

Structure Voting & Elections Art. II · Voting, Initiative and Referendum, and Recall

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

(a) An initiative statute or referendum approved by a majority of votes cast thereon takes effect on the fifth day after the Secretary of State files the statement of the vote for the election at which the measure is voted on, but the measure may provide that it becomes operative after its effective date. If a is filed against a part of a statute, the remainder of the statute shall not be delayed from going into effect.

(b) If provisions of two or more measures approved at the same election conflict, the provisions of the measure receiving the highest number of affirmative votes shall prevail.

(c) The Legislature may amend or repeal a referendum statute. The Legislature may amend or repeal an initiative statute by another statute that becomes effective only when approved by the unless the initiative statute permits amendment or repeal without the ’ approval.

(d) Before circulation of an initiative or for signatures, a copy shall be submitted to the Attorney General who shall prepare a title and summary of the measure as provided by law.

Simplified explanation

An approved initiative statute or referendum takes effect on the fifth day after the statement of the vote is filed. When approved measures conflict, the one with the most affirmative votes prevails. The Legislature may amend or repeal initiative statutes only with voter approval unless the initiative permits otherwise.
