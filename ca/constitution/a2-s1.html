Art. II, § 1

Political power inherent in the people

Right Founding & Purposes Art. II · Voting, Initiative and Referendum, and Recall

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

All political power is inherent in the people. Government is instituted for their protection, security, and benefit, and they have the right to alter or reform it when the public good may require.

History: Sec. 1 renumbered from Sec. 26 (of Art. 1) on June 8, 1976, by Prop. 14. Res.Ch. 5, 1976.
