Art. XX, § 5

Corporation laws may be changed

Power Taxes, Money & Commerce Art. XX · Miscellaneous Subjects

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

All laws now in force in this State concerning corporations and all laws that may be hereafter passed pursuant to this section may be altered from time to time or repealed.

History: Sec. 5 renumbered from Sec. 24 on June 8, 1976, by Prop. 14. Res.Ch. 5, 1976.

Simplified explanation

All laws in force concerning corporations, and all laws later passed under this section, may be altered from time to time or repealed.
