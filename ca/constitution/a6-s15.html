Art. VI, § 15

Qualifications of judges

Structure The Courts Art. VI · Judicial

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

A person is ineligible to be a judge of a court of record unless for 10 years immediately preceding selection, the person has been a member of the State Bar or served as a judge of a court of record in this State.

History: Sec. 15 amended Nov. 5, 2002, by Prop. 48. Res.Ch. 88, 2002.

Simplified explanation

A person cannot be a judge of a court of record unless, for the 10 years just before selection, the person was a State Bar member or a judge of a court of record in California.
