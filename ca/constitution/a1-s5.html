Art. I, § 5

Military subordinate to civil power

Limit Individual Rights Art. I · Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The military is subordinate to civil power. A standing army may not be maintained in peacetime. Soldiers may not be quartered in any house in wartime except as prescribed by law, or in peacetime without the owner’s consent.

History: Sec. 5 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 7. Res.Ch. 90, 1974.

Simplified explanation

The military is subordinate to civil power. No standing army may be kept in peacetime, and soldiers may not be quartered in any house in wartime except as prescribed by law, or in peacetime without the owner's consent.
