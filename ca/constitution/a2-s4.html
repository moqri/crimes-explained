Art. II, § 4

Improper election practices and disqualification of electors

Duty Voting & Elections Art. II · Voting, Initiative and Referendum, and Recall

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The Legislature shall prohibit improper practices that affect elections and shall provide for the disqualification of while mentally incompetent or serving a state or federal prison term for the conviction of a felony.

History: Sec. 4 amended Nov. 3, 2020, by Prop. 17. Res.Ch. 24, 2020. Effective December 16, 2020.
