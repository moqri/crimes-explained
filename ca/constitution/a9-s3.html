Art. IX, § 3

County superintendents of schools

Structure Education Art. IX · Education

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

A Superintendent of Schools for each county may be elected by the qualified thereof at each gubernatorial election or may be appointed by the county board of education, and the manner of the selection shall be determined by a majority vote of the of the county voting on the question; provided, that two or more counties may, by an election conducted pursuant to Section 3.2 of this article, unite for the purpose of electing or appointing one joint superintendent for the counties so uniting.

History: Sec. 3 amended Nov. 2, 1976, by Prop. 8. Res.Ch. 57, 1976.

Simplified explanation

A county superintendent of schools may be elected by the county's voters or appointed by the county board of education, as a majority of the county's voters decide. Two or more counties may unite to elect or appoint one joint superintendent.
