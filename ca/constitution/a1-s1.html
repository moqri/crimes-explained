Art. I, § 1

Inalienable rights

Right Individual Rights Art. I · Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

All people are by nature free and independent and have inalienable rights. Among these are enjoying and defending life and liberty, acquiring, possessing, and protecting property, and pursuing and obtaining safety, happiness, and privacy.

History: Sec. 1 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Proposition 7. Resolution Chapter 90, 1974.

Simplified explanation

All people are by nature free and independent and have inalienable rights, among them enjoying and defending life and liberty, acquiring, possessing, and protecting property, and pursuing and obtaining safety, happiness, and privacy.
