Art. XIII B, § 10

When the spending limitation takes effect

Structure Taxes, Money & Commerce Art. XIII B · Government Spending Limitation

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

This Article shall be effective commencing with the first day of the fiscal year following its adoption.

History: Sec. 10 added Nov. 6, 1979, by Prop. 4. Initiative measure.

Simplified explanation
