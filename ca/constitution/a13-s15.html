Art. XIII, § 15

Reassessing damaged or destroyed property

Power Taxes, Money & Commerce Art. XIII · Taxation

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The Legislature may authorize local government to provide for the assessment or reassessment of taxable property physically damaged or destroyed after the lien date to which the assessment or reassessment relates.

History: Sec. 15 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 8. Res.Ch. 70, 1974, third resolved clause.
