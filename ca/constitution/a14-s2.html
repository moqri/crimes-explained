Art. XIV, § 2

Eight-hour day on public works

Limit Labor Art. XIV · Labor Relations

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Worktime of mechanics or workers on public works may not exceed eight hours a day except in wartime or extraordinary emergencies that endanger life or property. The Legislature shall provide for enforcement of this section.

History: Sec. 2 added June 8, 1976, by Prop. 14. Res.Ch. 5, 1976.

Simplified explanation

Mechanics and workers on public works may not work more than eight hours a day, except in wartime or extraordinary emergencies that endanger life or property. The Legislature must provide for enforcement.
