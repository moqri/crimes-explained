Art. V, § 9

Lieutenant Governor's qualifications and Senate role

Structure The Governor & Executive Art. V · Executive

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The Lieutenant Governor shall have the same qualifications as the Governor. The Lieutenant Governor is President of the Senate but has only a casting vote.

History: Sec. 9 amended Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 11. Res.Ch. 96, 1974.
