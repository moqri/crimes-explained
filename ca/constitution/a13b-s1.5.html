Art. XIII B, § 1.5

Audit of local appropriations limits

Duty Taxes, Money & Commerce Art. XIII B · Government Spending Limitation

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The annual calculation of the appropriations limit under this article for each entity of local government shall be reviewed as part of an annual financial audit.

History: Sec. 1.5 added June 5, 1990, by Prop. 111. Res.Ch. 66, 1989. Effective July 1, 1990.

Simplified explanation

Each local government's annual calculation of its appropriations limit must be reviewed as part of an annual financial audit.
