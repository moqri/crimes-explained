§ 508

Embezzlement by employees

See penalty details+ Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 6 · Embezzlement

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every clerk, agent, or servant of any person who fraudulently appropriates to his own use, or secretes with a fraudulent intent to appropriate to his own use, any property of another which has come into his control or care by virtue of his employment as such clerk, agent, or servant, is guilty of embezzlement.

History: Enacted 1872.

1 crime in this section

  1. Appropriates to his own use, or secretes, any property of another

    Ways to commit
    • appropriates · secretes
    Who
    • A clerk, agent, or servant of any person
    Mental state
    • Fraudulently
    • With a fraudulent intent to appropriate to his own use (for secreting)
    Conditions
    • The property came into his control or care by virtue of his employment as such clerk, agent, or servant
    Penalty
    • Guilty of embezzlement (no punishment is stated in this section)

Simplified explanation

A clerk, agent, or servant who fraudulently takes or hides another's property that came into their care through their employment is guilty of embezzlement; no punishment is stated here.
