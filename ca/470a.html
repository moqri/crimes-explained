§ 470a

Forging a driver's license or ID card

Up to 1 year+ Fraud & Money Title 13, Ch. 4 · Forgery and Counterfeiting

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every person who alters, falsifies, forges, duplicates or in any manner reproduces or counterfeits any driver’s license or identification card issued by a governmental agency with the intent that such driver’s license or identification card be used to facilitate the commission of any forgery, is punishable by imprisonment in a county jail for not more than one year, or by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170.

History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 358. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68.

Altering, forging, copying, or counterfeiting a government-issued driver's license or ID card to help commit forgery is punishable by up to one year in county jail or as a felony.
