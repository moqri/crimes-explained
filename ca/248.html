§ 248

Light shined at aircraft

Up to 1 year Transportation & Aviation Ch. 9 · Assault and Battery

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Any person who, with the intent to interfere with the operation of an aircraft, shines a light or other bright device, of an intensity capable of impairing the operation of an aircraft, at an aircraft, shall be punished by a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000), or by imprisonment in a county jail not exceeding one year, or by both that fine and imprisonment.

History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 218, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.

1 crime in this section

  1. Shines a light or other bright device, of an intensity capable of impairing the operation of an aircraft, at an aircraft

    Intent
    • With the intent to interfere with the operation of an aircraft
    • Willfully
    Conditions
    • The light or device is of an intensity capable of impairing the operation of an aircraft
    Penalty
    • A fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000), or imprisonment in a county jail not exceeding one year, or both that fine and imprisonment

Simplified explanation

Willfully shining a bright light at an aircraft to interfere with its operation is punishable by a fine up to $1,000, county jail up to one year, or both.
