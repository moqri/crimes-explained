§ 556

Advertising signs on public property

See penalty details Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 12 · Unlawful Interference With Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

It is a for any person to place or maintain, or cause to be placed or maintained without lawful permission upon any property of the State, or of a city or of a county, any sign, picture, transparency, advertisement, or mechanical device which is used for the purpose of advertising or which advertises or brings to notice any person, article of merchandise, business or profession, or anything that is to be or has been sold, bartered, or given away.

History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 32.

Simplified explanation

Placing or keeping advertising signs, pictures, or devices on state, city, or county property without lawful permission is a misdemeanor; no term is stated here.
