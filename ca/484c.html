§ 484c

False vouchers for construction loans

See penalty details+ Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 5 · Larceny

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Any person who submits a false voucher to obtain construction loan funds and does not use the funds for the purpose for which the claim was submitted is guilty of embezzlement.

History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1145.

1 crime in this section

  1. Submits a false voucher to obtain construction loan funds and does not use the funds for the purpose for which the claim was submitted

    Conditions
    • The person does not use the funds for the purpose for which the claim was submitted
    Penalty
    • Guilty of embezzlement (no punishment is stated in this section)

Simplified explanation

Submitting a false voucher to get construction loan funds and not using the funds for the stated purpose is embezzlement.
