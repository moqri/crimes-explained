§ 487i

Defrauding public housing programs

See penalty details+ Fraud & Money Title 13, Ch. 5 · Larceny

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Any person who defrauds a housing program of a public housing authority of more than four hundred dollars ($400) is guilty of grand theft.

History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 105, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.

1 crime in this section

  1. Defrauds a housing program of a public housing authority

    Conditions
    • The amount is more than four hundred dollars ($400)
    Penalty
    • Guilty of grand theft (no punishment is stated in this section)

Simplified explanation

Defrauding a public housing authority's housing program of more than $400 is grand theft.
