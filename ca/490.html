§ 490

Punishment for petty theft

Up to 6 months Property Crimes Title 13, Ch. 5 · Larceny

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Petty theft is punishable by fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000), or by imprisonment in the county jail not exceeding six months, or both.

History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1125.

Simplified explanation

Petty theft is punishable by a fine up to $1,000, up to six months in county jail, or both.
