§ 210

Posing as kidnapper for ransom

Up to 4 years Violence & Threats Ch. 3 · Kidnapping

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every person who for the purpose of obtaining any ransom or reward, or to extort or exact from any person any money or , poses as, or in any manner represents himself to be a person who has seized, confined, inveigled, enticed, decoyed, abducted, concealed, kidnapped or carried away any person, or who poses as, or in any manner represents himself to be a person who holds or detains such person, or who poses as, or in any manner represents himself to be a person who has aided or abetted any such act, or who poses as or in any manner represents himself to be a person who has the influence, power, or ability, to obtain the release of such person so seized, confined, inveigled, enticed, decoyed, abducted, concealed, kidnapped or carried away, is guilty of a and upon conviction thereof shall be punished by imprisonment for two, three or four years.

Nothing in this section prohibits any person who, in good faith believes that he can rescue any person who has been seized, confined, inveigled, enticed, decoyed, abducted, concealed, kidnapped or carried away, and who has had no part in, or connection with, such confinement, inveigling, decoying, abducting, concealing, kidnapping, or carrying away, from offering to rescue or obtain the release of such person for a monetary consideration or other .

History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1139.

1 crime in this section

  1. Poses as, or in any manner represents himself to be, a person who has seized, confined, inveigled, enticed, decoyed, abducted, concealed, kidnapped or carried away any person, or who holds or detains such person, or who has aided or abetted any such act, or who has the influence, power, or ability to obtain the release of such person

    Ways to commit
    • Poses as a person who has seized, confined, inveigled, enticed, decoyed, abducted, concealed, kidnapped or carried away any person · Poses as a person who holds or detains such person · Poses as a person who has aided or abetted any such act · Poses as a person who has the influence, power, or ability to obtain the release of such person
    Intent
    • For the purpose of obtaining any ransom or reward, or to extort or exact from any person any money or thing of value
    Exceptions
    • Nothing in this section prohibits any person who, in good faith believes that he can rescue any person who has been seized, confined, inveigled, enticed, decoyed, abducted, concealed, kidnapped or carried away, and who has had no part in, or connection with, such confinement, inveigling, decoying, abducting, concealing, kidnapping, or carrying away, from offering to rescue or obtain the release of such person for a monetary consideration or other thing of value
    Penalty
    • Felony punishable by imprisonment for two, three or four years

Simplified explanation

Posing as a kidnapper, or as someone able to obtain a victim's release, to obtain ransom, reward, or money is a felony punishable by imprisonment for two, three, or four years.
