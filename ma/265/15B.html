c. 265, § 15B

Assault with dangerous weapon; victim sixty or older; punishment; subsequent offenses

Up to 5 years Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever, by means of a , commits an assault upon a person sixty years or older, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than five years or by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars or imprisonment in jail for not more than two and one-half years.

Whoever, after having been convicted of the crime of assault upon a person sixty years or older, by means of a , commits a second or subsequent such crime, shall be punished by imprisonment for not less than two years. Said sentence shall not be reduced until one year of said sentence has been served nor shall the person convicted be eligible for probation, parole, furlough, work release or receive any deduction from his sentence for good conduct until he shall have served one year of such sentence; provided, however, that the commissioner of correction may, on the recommendation of the warden, superintendent, or other person in charge of a correctional institution, or the administrator of a county correctional institution, grant to said offender a temporary release in the custody of an officer of such institution for the following purposes only: to attend the funeral of next of kin or spouse; to visit a critically ill close relative or spouse; or to obtain emergency medical services unavailable at said institution. The provisions of section eighty-seven of chapter two hundred and seventy-six relative to the power of the court to place certain offenders on probation shall not apply to any person 18 years of age or over charged with a violation of this subsection.

For the purposes of prosecution, a conviction obtained under subsection (a) of section fifteen A or paragraph (a) of section 18 shall count as a prior criminal conviction for the purpose of prosecution and sentencing as a second or subsequent conviction.

(b) Whoever, by means of a , commits an assault upon another shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than five years or by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars or imprisonment in jail for not more than two and one-half years.

2 crimes in this section

  1. (a) Commits an assault upon a person sixty years or older by means of a dangerous weapon

    Conditions
    • The victim is 60 years or older
    Penalty
    • Up to 5 years in state prison, or a fine of up to $1,000 or up to 2½ years in jail
    • If the person has a prior conviction for this crime (a conviction under subsection (a) of section 15A or paragraph (a) of section 18 also counts): Imprisonment for not less than 2 years; the sentence shall not be reduced until 1 year has been servedmandatory minimumno paroleno probation
    Other consequences
    • Second or subsequent offense: not eligible for probation, parole, furlough, work release or good-conduct deductions until 1 year of the sentence is served
    • The commissioner of correction may grant a temporary release in custody only to attend the funeral of next of kin or spouse, visit a critically ill close relative or spouse, or obtain emergency medical services unavailable at the institution
    • The court's power to place certain offenders on probation (section 87 of chapter 276) does not apply to a person 18 years of age or over charged under this subsection

  2. (b) Commits an assault upon another by means of a dangerous weapon

    Penalty
    • Up to 5 years in state prison, or a fine of up to $1,000 or up to 2½ years in jail
