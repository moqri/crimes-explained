c. 265, § 13H

Indecent assault and battery on person fourteen or older; penalties

Up to 20 years Sex Crimes & Exploitation Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever commits an indecent assault and battery on a person who has attained age fourteen shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than five years, or by imprisonment for not more than two and one-half years in a jail or .

Whoever commits an indecent assault and battery on an elder or person with a disability, as defined in section 13K, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than 10 years, or by imprisonment in the house of correction for not more than 2½ years, and whoever commits a second or subsequent such offense shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than 20 years. A prosecution commenced under this paragraph shall not be placed on file nor continued without a finding.

2 crimes in this section

  1. Commits an indecent assault and battery on a person who has attained age fourteen

    Conditions
    • The victim has attained age fourteen
    Penalty
    • Up to 5 years in state prison or up to 2½ years in a jail or house of correction

  2. Commits an indecent assault and battery on an elder or person with a disability

    Conditions
    • The victim is an elder or person with a disability, as defined in section 13K of chapter 265
    Penalty
    • Up to 10 years in state prison or up to 2½ years in the house of correction
    • If the person has a prior conviction for this crime: Up to 20 years in state prison
    Other consequences
    • Prosecution shall not be placed on file nor continued without a finding

Simplified explanation

Indecent assault and battery on a person 14 or older is punishable by up to 5 years in state prison; on an elder or person with a disability, up to 10 years, or 20 for a repeat offense.
