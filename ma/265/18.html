c. 265, § 18

Assault with intent to rob or murder; weapons; punishment; victim sixty years or older; minimum sentence for repeat offenders

Up to 20 years Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever, being armed with a , assaults a person sixty years or older with intent to rob or murder shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than twenty years. Whoever commits any offense described herein while armed with a firearm, as defined in section 121 of chapter 140, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not less than ten years.

Whoever, after having been convicted of the crime of assault upon a person sixty years or older with intent to rob or murder while being armed with a , commits a second or subsequent such crime, shall be punished by imprisonment for not less than two years. Said sentence shall not be reduced until two years of said sentence have been served nor shall the person convicted be eligible for probation, parole, furlough, work release or receive any deduction from his sentence for good conduct until he shall have served two years of such sentence; provided, however, that the commissioner of correction may, on the recommendation of the warden, superintendent, or other person in charge of a correctional institution, or the administrator of a county correctional institution, grant to said offender a temporary release in the custody of an officer of such institution for the following purposes only: to attend the funeral of next of kin or spouse; to visit a critically ill close relative or spouse; or to obtain emergency medical services unavailable at said institution. The provisions of section eighty-seven of chapter two hundred and seventy-six relating to the power of the court to place certain offenders on probation shall not apply to any person 18 years of age or over charged with a violation of this subsection. Whoever, after having been convicted of the crime of assault upon a person 60 years or older with intent to rob or murder while armed with a firearm, as defined in section 121 of chapter 140, commits a second or subsequent such crime shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not less than 20 years.

(b) Whoever, being armed with a , assaults another with intent to rob or murder shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than twenty years. Whoever, being armed with a firearm, shotgun, rifle, machine gun or assault weapon assaults another with intent to rob or murder shall be punished by imprisonment in state prison for not less than five years and not more than 20 years.

2 crimes in this section

  1. (a) Assaults a person sixty years or older

    Conditions
    • The person is armed with a dangerous weapon
    • The victim is 60 years or older
    Penalty
    • Up to 20 years in state prison
    • If the offense is committed while armed with a firearm, as defined in section 121 of chapter 140: Not less than 10 years in state prisonmandatory minimum
    • If the person has a prior conviction for this crime: Imprisonment for not less than 2 years; the sentence shall not be reduced until 2 years have been servedmandatory minimumno paroleno probation
    • If the person has a prior conviction for this crime committed while armed with a firearm and commits it again: Not less than 20 years in state prisonmandatory minimum
    Other consequences
    • Second or subsequent offense: not eligible for probation, parole, furlough, work release or good-conduct deductions until 2 years of the sentence are served
    • The commissioner of correction may grant a temporary release in custody only to attend the funeral of next of kin or spouse, visit a critically ill close relative or spouse, or obtain emergency medical services unavailable at the institution
    • The court's power to place certain offenders on probation (section 87 of chapter 276) does not apply to a person 18 years of age or over charged under this subsection

  2. (b) Assaults another

    Conditions
    • The person is armed with a dangerous weapon
    Penalty
    • Up to 20 years in state prison
    • If the person is armed with a firearm, shotgun, rifle, machine gun or assault weapon: Not less than 5 and not more than 20 years in state prisonmandatory minimum
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Intent
  • With intent to rob or murder

Simplified explanation

Assaulting someone while armed with a dangerous weapon, with intent to rob or murder, is punishable by up to 20 years in state prison, with mandatory minimums if armed with a firearm.
