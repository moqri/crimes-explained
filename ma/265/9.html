c. 265, § 9

Prize fighting; engaging

Up to 10 years Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, except as provided in sections thirty-two to fifty, inclusive, of chapter one hundred and forty-seven, by previous appointment or arrangement, engages in a fight with another person shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than ten years or by a fine of not more than five thousand dollars.

1 crime in this section

  1. Engages in a fight with another person

    Conditions
    • By previous appointment or arrangement
    Exceptions
    • As provided in sections thirty-two to fifty, inclusive, of chapter one hundred and forty-seven
    Penalty
    • Up to 10 years in state prison or a fine of up to $5,000
