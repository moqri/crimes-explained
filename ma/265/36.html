c. 265, § 36

Throwing or dropping objects at sporting events; punishment

Up to 1 year Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Any person who drops, throws or otherwise releases any object, missile or other article at any sporting event with the intent to injure any person at such event shall be punished by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars or by imprisonment in the house of correction for not more than one year, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Drops, throws or otherwise releases any object, missile or other article at any sporting event

    Ways to commit
    • drops · throws · otherwise releases
    Intent
    • Willfully

    Penalty
