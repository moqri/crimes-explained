c. 265, § 15A

Assault and battery with dangerous weapon; victim sixty or older; punishment; subsequent offenses

Up to 15 years Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever commits assault and battery upon a person sixty years or older by means of a shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than ten years or by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars or imprisonment in jail for not more than two and one-half years.

Whoever, after having been convicted of the crime of assault and battery upon a person sixty years or older, by means of a , commits a second or subsequent such crime, shall be punished by imprisonment for not less than two years. Said sentence shall not be reduced until two years of said sentence have been served nor shall the person convicted be eligible for probation, parole, furlough, work release or receive any deduction from his sentence for good conduct until he shall have served two years of such sentence; provided, however, that the commissioner of correction may, on the recommendation of the warden, superintendent, or other person in charge of a correctional institution, or the administrator of a county correctional institution, grant to said offender a temporary release in the custody of an officer of such institution for the following purposes only: to attend the funeral of next of kin or spouse; to visit a critically ill close relative or spouse; or to obtain emergency medical services unavailable at said institution. The provisions of section eighty-seven of chapter two hundred and seventy-six relating to the power of the court to place certain offenders on probation shall not apply to any person 18 years of age or over charged with a violation of this subsection.

(b) Whoever commits an assault and battery upon another by means of a shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than 10 years or in the house of correction for not more than 2½ years, or by a fine of not more than $5,000, or by both such fine and imprisonment.

(c) Whoever:

(i) by means of a , commits an assault and battery upon another and by such assault and battery causes ;

(ii) by means of a , commits an assault and battery upon another who is pregnant at the time of such assault and battery, knowing or that the person is pregnant;

(iii) by means of a , commits an assault and battery upon another who he knows has an outstanding temporary or permanent vacate, restraining or no contact order or judgment issued pursuant to section 18, section 34B or section 34C of chapter 208, section 32 of chapter 209, section 3, 4 or 5 of chapter 209A, or section 15 or 20 of chapter 209C, in effect against him at the time of such assault and battery; or

(iv) is 18 years of age or older and, by means of a , commits an assault and battery upon a child under the age of 14;

shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than 15 years or in the house of correction for not more than 2½ years, or by a fine of not more than $10,000, or by both such fine and imprisonment.

(d) For the purposes of this section, “” shall mean which results in a permanent disfigurement, loss or impairment of a bodily function, limb or organ, or a substantial risk of death.

6 crimes in this section

  1. (a) Commits assault and battery upon a person sixty years or older by means of a dangerous weapon

    Conditions
    • The victim is 60 years or older
    Penalty
    • Up to 10 years in state prison, or a fine of up to $1,000 or up to 2½ years in jail
    • If the person has a prior conviction for this crime: Imprisonment for not less than 2 years; the sentence shall not be reduced until 2 years have been servedmandatory minimumno paroleno probation
    Other consequences
    • Second or subsequent offense: not eligible for probation, parole, furlough, work release or good-conduct deductions until 2 years of the sentence are served
    • The commissioner of correction may grant a temporary release in custody only to attend the funeral of next of kin or spouse, visit a critically ill close relative or spouse, or obtain emergency medical services unavailable at the institution
    • The court's power to place certain offenders on probation (section 87 of chapter 276) does not apply to a person 18 years of age or over charged under this subsection

  2. (b) Commits an assault and battery upon another by means of a dangerous weapon

    Penalty
    • Up to 10 years in state prison or up to 2½ years in a house of correction, a fine of up to $5,000, or both

  3. (c)(i) Commits an assault and battery upon another by means of a dangerous weapon and by such assault and battery causes serious bodily injury

    Conditions
    • The assault and battery causes serious bodily injury
    Penalty
    • Up to 15 years in state prison or up to 2½ years in a house of correction, a fine of up to $10,000, or both
    Key terms
    • "Serious bodily injury": bodily injury which results in a permanent disfigurement, loss or impairment of a bodily function, limb or organ, or a substantial risk of death

  4. (c)(ii) Commits an assault and battery upon another by means of a dangerous weapon who is pregnant

    Knowledge
    • Knowing or having reason to know that the person is pregnant
    Conditions
    • The victim is pregnant at the time of the assault and battery
    Penalty
    • Up to 15 years in state prison or up to 2½ years in a house of correction, a fine of up to $10,000, or both

  5. (c)(iii) Commits an assault and battery upon another by means of a dangerous weapon who has an outstanding vacate, restraining or no contact order or judgment in effect against him

    Knowledge
    • Knows the person has an outstanding temporary or permanent vacate, restraining or no contact order or judgment in effect against him
    Conditions
    • The victim has an outstanding temporary or permanent vacate, restraining or no contact order or judgment in effect against the defendant at the time
    Penalty
    • Up to 15 years in state prison or up to 2½ years in a house of correction, a fine of up to $10,000, or both

  6. (c)(iv) Commits an assault and battery upon a child under the age of 14 by means of a dangerous weapon

    Who
    • A person 18 years of age or older
    Conditions
    • The victim is under the age of 14
    Penalty
    • Up to 15 years in state prison or up to 2½ years in a house of correction, a fine of up to $10,000, or both

Simplified explanation

Assault and battery with a dangerous weapon is punishable by up to 10 years in state prison; causing serious injury, or attacking a pregnant person, a child under 14, or someone with a protective order against you, carries up to 15 years.
