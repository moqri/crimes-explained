c. 265, § 34

Tattooing body of person by other than qualified physician; punishment

Up to 1 year Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, not being registered as a qualified physician under section two of chapter one hundred and twelve, or corresponding provisions of earlier laws, marks the body of any person by means of tattooing, shall be punished by a fine of not more than three hundred dollars or by imprisonment for not more than one year, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Marks the body of any person by means of tattooing

    Who
    • A person not registered as a qualified physician under section two of chapter one hundred and twelve, or corresponding provisions of earlier laws
    Penalty
    • A fine of up to $300 or imprisonment for up to one year, or both
