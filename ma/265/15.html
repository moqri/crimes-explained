c. 265, § 15

Assault; intent to murder or maim; penalty

Up to 10 years Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever assaults another with intent to commit murder, or to maim or disfigure his person in any way described in the preceding section, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than ten years or by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars and imprisonment in jail for not more than two and one half years.

1 crime in this section

    Mental state
    • With intent to commit murder, or to maim or disfigure his person in any way described in the preceding section
    Penalty
    • Up to 10 years in state prison, or a fine of up to $1,000 and up to 2½ years in jail

Simplified explanation

Assaulting someone with intent to murder, maim or disfigure is punishable by up to 10 years in state prison.
