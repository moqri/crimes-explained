c. 265, § 40

Causing serious bodily injury to participants in physical exercise training programs; punishment

Up to 2½ years Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, having the direct management or direct control over the conduct of physical exercise as part of a course of study or training program at any public or private institution, agency or entity, , wantonly and causes to a person participating in a course of study or training program involving physical exercise, shall be punished by a fine of not more than five thousand dollars or by imprisonment in a jail or house of correction for not more than two and one-half years or both.

For the purposes of this section “” shall mean which creates a substantial risk of death or which involves either total disability or the loss or substantial impairment of some bodily function for a substantial period of time.

1 crime in this section

  1. Causes serious bodily injury to a person participating in a course of study or training program involving physical exercise

    Who
    • A person having the direct management or direct control over the conduct of physical exercise as part of a course of study or training program at any public or private institution, agency or entity
    Mental state
    • Willfully, wantonly and recklessly
    Penalty
    • A fine of up to $5,000 or up to 2½ years in a jail or house of correction, or both
    Key terms
    • "Serious bodily injury": bodily injury which creates a substantial risk of death or which involves either total disability or the loss or substantial impairment of some bodily function for a substantial period of time

Simplified explanation

A person running a physical exercise course or training program who willfully, wantonly and recklessly causes serious bodily injury to a participant faces up to 2½ years in jail and a $5,000 fine.
