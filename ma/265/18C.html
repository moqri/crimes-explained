c. 265, § 18C

Entry of dwelling place; persons present within; weapons; punishment

Up to life Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever knowingly enters the dwelling place of another knowing or that one or more persons are present within or knowingly enters the dwelling place of another and remains in such dwelling place knowing or that one or more persons are present within while armed with a , uses force or threatens the imminent use of force upon any person within such dwelling place whether or not injury occurs, or intentionally causes any injury to any person within such dwelling place shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for life or for any term of not less than twenty years.

1 crime in this section

  1. Enters the dwelling place of another, or enters and remains in such dwelling place, while armed with a dangerous weapon, and uses force or threatens the imminent use of force upon any person within such dwelling place, or causes any injury to any person within such dwelling place

    Ways to commit
    • enters · enters and remains
    Knowledge
    • Knowingly enters (or enters and remains)
    • Knowing or having reason to know that one or more persons are present within
    Intent
    • Intentionally (for causing injury)
    Conditions
    • One or more persons are present within the dwelling place
    • The person is armed with a dangerous weapon
    • Force is used or threatened, whether or not injury occurs, or injury is intentionally caused
    Penalty
    • Life or any term of not less than 20 years in state prisonmandatory minimum
