c. 265, § 29

Assault; intent to commit felony; punishment

Up to 10 years Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever assaults another with intent to commit a shall, if the punishment of such assault is not hereinbefore provided, be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than ten years or by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars and imprisonment in jail for not more than two and one half years.

1 crime in this section

  1. Assaults another

    Mental state
    • With intent to commit a felony
    Conditions
    • The punishment of such assault is not hereinbefore provided
    Penalty
    • Up to 10 years in the state prison, or a fine of up to $1,000 and up to 2½ years in jail

Simplified explanation

Assaulting someone with intent to commit a felony, where no other law sets the punishment, is punishable by up to 10 years in state prison.
