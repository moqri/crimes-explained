c. 265, § 12

Boxing, kickboxing, mixed martial arts or other unarmed combative sporting matches or sparring exhibitions; penalty

Up to 3 months Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever directly or indirectly, except as provided in sections 32 to 50A, inclusive, of chapter 147, gives, promotes or engages in a public boxing, kickboxing, mixed martial arts or other unarmed combative sporting match or sparring exhibition, or engages in a private boxing, kickboxing, mixed martial arts or other unarmed combative sporting event match or sparring exhibition, for which the contestants have received or have been promised any pecuniary reward, remuneration or consideration whatsoever shall be punished by imprisonment in the house of corrections for not more than 3 months or by a fine of not more than $5,000, or both such fine and imprisonment.

2 crimes in this section

  1. Gives, promotes or engages in a public boxing, kickboxing, mixed martial arts or other unarmed combative sporting match or sparring exhibition

    Ways to commit
    • gives · promotes · engages in
    Conditions
    • Directly or indirectly
    Exceptions
    • As provided in sections 32 to 50A, inclusive, of chapter 147

  2. Engages in a private boxing, kickboxing, mixed martial arts or other unarmed combative sporting event match or sparring exhibition

    Conditions
    • Directly or indirectly
    • The contestants have received or have been promised any pecuniary reward, remuneration or consideration whatsoever
    Exceptions
    • As provided in sections 32 to 50A, inclusive, of chapter 147
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Penalty
  • Up to 3 months in the house of correction, a fine of up to $5,000, or both

Simplified explanation

Giving, promoting or engaging in an unlicensed public boxing, kickboxing or MMA match, or a private one for pay, is punishable by up to 3 months in a house of correction, a $5,000 fine, or both.
