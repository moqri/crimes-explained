c. 265, § 21

Stealing by confining or putting in fear

Up to life Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, with intent to commit larceny or any , confines, maims, injures or wounds, or or threatens to kill, confine, maim, injure or wound, or puts any person in fear, for the purpose of stealing from a building, bank, safe, vault or other depository of money, bonds or other valuables, or by intimidation, force or threats compels or to compel any person to disclose or surrender the means of opening any building, bank, safe, vault or other depository of money, bonds, or other valuables, shall whether he succeeds or fails in the perpetration of such larceny or , be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for life or for any term of years.

Simplified explanation

Confining, injuring, threatening or frightening a person to steal from a building, bank, safe or vault, or forcing someone to reveal how to open one, is punishable by up to life in state prison.
