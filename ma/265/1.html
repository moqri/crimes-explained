c. 265, § 1

Murder defined

See penalty details+ Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Murder committed with deliberately premeditated , or with extreme atrocity or cruelty, or in the commission or attempted commission of a crime punishable with death or imprisonment for life, is murder in the first degree. Murder which does not appear to be in the first degree is murder in the second degree. Petit treason shall be prosecuted and punished as murder. The degree of murder shall be found by the jury.
