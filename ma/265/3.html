c. 265, § 3

Duel; wound without and death within state; venue

See penalty details+ Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

An inhabitant or resident of this commonwealth who, by previous appointment or engagement made within the same, fights a duel outside its jurisdiction, and in so doing inflicts a mortal wound upon a person whereof he dies within the commonwealth shall be guilty of murder within this commonwealth, and may be indicted, tried and convicted in the county where the death occurs.

1 crime in this section

  1. Fights a duel outside its jurisdiction, and in so doing inflicts a mortal wound upon a person whereof he dies within the commonwealth

    Who
    • An inhabitant or resident of this commonwealth
    Conditions
    • The duel was by previous appointment or engagement made within the commonwealth
    • The person dies within the commonwealth
    Penalty
    • Guilty of murder within this commonwealth; punishment not set in this section
    Other consequences
    • May be indicted, tried and convicted in the county where the death occurs

Simplified explanation

A Massachusetts resident who arranges a duel here, fights it out of state, and mortally wounds someone who dies in Massachusetts is guilty of murder here; the punishment is set elsewhere.
