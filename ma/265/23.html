c. 265, § 23

Rape and abuse of child

Up to life Sex Crimes & Exploitation Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever unlawfully has sexual intercourse or unnatural sexual intercourse, and abuses a child under 16 years of age, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for life or for any term of years or, except as otherwise provided, for any term in a jail or . A prosecution commenced under this section shall neither be continued without a finding nor placed on file.

1 crime in this section

  1. Unlawfully has sexual intercourse or unnatural sexual intercourse, and abuses a child under 16 years of age

    Conditions
    • The victim is under 16 years of age
    Penalty
    • Life or any term of years in state prison or, except as otherwise provided, any term in a jail or house of correction
    Other consequences
    • The prosecution shall neither be continued without a finding nor placed on file

Simplified explanation

Statutory rape (unlawfully having sexual intercourse with and abusing a child under 16) is punishable by up to life in state prison or any term in a jail or house of correction.
