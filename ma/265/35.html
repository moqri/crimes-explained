c. 265, § 35

Throwing or dropping objects onto public way; punishment

Up to 1 year Transportation & Aviation Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever or drops, throws or otherwise releases any object, missile or other article onto any way as defined in section one of chapter ninety, the turnpike as defined in clause (b) of section four of chapter three hundred and fifty-four of the acts of nineteen hundred and fifty-two or the tunnels as defined in clause (d) of section one of chapter five hundred and ninety-eight of the acts of nineteen hundred and fifty-eight so that the lives or safety of the public might be endangered shall be punished by a fine of not more than one hundred dollars, or by imprisonment for not more than one year, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Drops, throws or otherwise releases any object, missile or other article onto any way, the turnpike or the tunnels

    Key terms
    • "Way", "turnpike" and "tunnels": as defined in section one of chapter ninety, clause (b) of section four of chapter 354 of the acts of 1952, and clause (d) of section one of chapter 598 of the acts of 1958

Simplified explanation

Willfully or negligently dropping or throwing an object onto a road, the turnpike, or certain tunnels so that public safety might be endangered is punishable by a $100 fine, up to one year, or both.
