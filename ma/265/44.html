c. 265, § 44

Coercion of child under eighteen into criminal conspiracy; penalties

Up to 10 years Family & Children Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever commits an assault and battery on a child under the age of eighteen for the purpose of causing or coercing such child to join or participate in a criminal in violation of section seven of chapter two hundred and seventy-four, including but not limited to a criminal street gang or other organization of three or more persons which has a common name, identifying sign or symbol and whose members individually or collectively engage in criminal activity, shall, for the first offense, be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not less than three nor more than five years or by imprisonment in the house of correction for not more than two and one-half years; and for a second or subsequent offense by imprisonment in the state prison for not less than five nor more than ten years.

1 crime in this section

    Mental state
    • For the purpose of causing or coercing such child to join or participate in a criminal conspiracy, including but not limited to a criminal street gang or other organization of three or more persons which has a common name, identifying sign or symbol and whose members individually or collectively engage in criminal activity
    Conditions
    • The victim is a child under the age of eighteen
    Penalty
    • Not less than 3 nor more than 5 years in the state prison, or up to 2½ years in the house of correction
    • If the person has a prior conviction for this crime: Not less than 5 nor more than 10 years in the state prison

Simplified explanation

Committing assault and battery on a child under 18 to force or coerce the child into joining a criminal conspiracy such as a street gang is punishable by up to 5 years in state prison; repeat offenses 5 to 10 years.
