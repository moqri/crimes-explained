c. 265, § 24

Assault with intent to commit rape; weapons; punishment; eligibility for furloughs, education, training or employment programs

Up to life Sex Crimes & Exploitation Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever assaults a person with intent to commit a rape shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than twenty years or by imprisonment in a jail or house of correction for not more than two and one-half years; and whoever commits a second or subsequent such offense shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for life or for any term of years. Whoever commits any offense described in this section while armed with a firearm, as defined in section 121 of chapter 140, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not less than five years. Whoever commits a second or subsequent such offense shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for life or for any term of years, but not less than 20 years.

No person serving a sentence for a second or subsequent such offense shall be eligible for furlough, temporary release, or education, training or employment programs established outside a correctional facility until such person shall have served two-thirds of such minimum sentence or if such person has two or more sentences to be served otherwise than concurrently, two-thirds of the aggregate of the minimum terms of such several sentences.

1 crime in this section

  1. Assaults a person

    Mental state
    • With intent to commit a rape
    Penalty
    • Up to 20 years in state prison or up to 2½ years in a jail or house of correction
    • If the person has a prior conviction for this crime: Life or any term of years in state prison
    • If the offense is committed while armed with a firearm, as defined in section 121 of chapter 140: Not less than 5 years in state prisonmandatory minimum
    • If a second or subsequent offense is committed while armed with a firearm: Life or any term of years in state prison, but not less than 20 yearsmandatory minimum
    Other consequences
    • Second or subsequent offense: not eligible for furlough, temporary release, or education, training or employment programs outside a correctional facility until two-thirds of the minimum sentence (or of the aggregate minimum terms of non-concurrent sentences) is served

Simplified explanation

Assaulting a person with intent to commit rape is punishable by up to 20 years in state prison; repeat offenses carry up to life, with mandatory minimums if armed with a firearm.
