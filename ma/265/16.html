c. 265, § 16

Attempt to murder

Up to 20 years Violence & Threats Ch. 265 · Crimes Against the Person

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever to commit murder by poisoning, drowning or strangling another person, or by any means not constituting an assault with intent to commit murder, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than twenty years or by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars and imprisonment in jail for not more than two and one half years.

1 crime in this section

  1. Attempts to commit murder by poisoning, drowning or strangling another person, or by any means not constituting an assault with intent to commit murder

    Ways to commit
    • poisoning · drowning · strangling · any means not constituting an assault with intent to commit murder
    Penalty
    • Up to 20 years in state prison, or a fine of up to $1,000 and up to 2½ years in jail
