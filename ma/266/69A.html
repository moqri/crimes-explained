c. 266, § 69A

Fraudulent use of labor union seal, trademark or insignia

Fine only Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever knowingly and fraudulently displays or otherwise uses, in any manner whatsoever, the seal, trademark or insignia of any labor organization as defined by subsection (5) of section two of chapter one hundred and fifty A shall be punished by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars.

Knowingly and fraudulently displaying or using a labor organization's seal, trademark or insignia is punishable by a fine of up to $1,000.
