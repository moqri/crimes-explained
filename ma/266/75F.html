c. 266, § 75F

Deduction of gratuity from gift certificate without consent; penalty

Fine only Fraud & Money Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever redeems a gift certificate, as defined in section 1 of chapter 255D and deducts a gratuity therefrom without the consent of the holder of the gift certificate, shall be punished by a fine of not more than $300 per violation.

1 crime in this section

  1. Redeems a gift certificate and deducts a gratuity therefrom

    Conditions
    • Without the consent of the holder of the gift certificate
    Penalty
    • A fine of up to $300 per violation
    Key terms
    • "gift certificate": as defined in section 1 of chapter 255D
