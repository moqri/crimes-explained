c. 266, § 130

Penal institutions; injury to property

Up to 2½ years Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being a prisoner at a jail or , wilfully and injures or destroys any public property or any materials furnished for the employment of prisoners in such jail or may be punished by imprisonment for not less than six months nor more than two and one half years.

1 crime in this section

  1. Injures or destroys any public property or any materials furnished for the employment of prisoners in such jail or house of correction

    Ways to commit
    • injures · destroys
    Who
    • A prisoner at a jail or house of correction
    Intent
    • Wilfully and maliciously
    Penalty
    • Imprisonment for not less than 6 months nor more than 2½ years

Simplified explanation

A prisoner at a jail or house of correction who wilfully and maliciously injures or destroys public property or materials furnished for prisoners' work may be imprisoned for 6 months to 2½ years.
