c. 266, § 10

Insured property; burning with intent to defraud

Up to 5 years Fraud & Money Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, wilfully and with intent to defraud or injure the insurer, sets fire to, or to set fire to, or whoever causes to be burned, or whoever aids, counsels or procures the burning of, a building, or any goods, wares, merchandise or other chattels, belonging to himself or another, and which are at the time insured against loss or damage by fire, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than five years or in a jail or house of correction for not more than two and one half years.

1 crime in this section

  1. Sets fire to, or attempts to set fire to, or causes to be burned, or aids, counsels or procures the burning of, a building, or any goods, wares, merchandise or other chattels, belonging to himself or another
