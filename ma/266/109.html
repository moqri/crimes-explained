c. 266, § 109

Vessel; fitting out with intent to destroy

Up to 20 years Fraud & Money Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever lades, equips or fits out, or assists in lading, equipping or fitting out, a ship or vessel, with intent that it shall be wilfully cast away, burnt, sunk or otherwise destroyed, and with intent to injure or defraud an owner or insurer of such ship or vessel, or of any property laden on board the same, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than twenty years or by a fine of not more than five thousand dollars and imprisonment in jail for not more than two and one half years.

1 crime in this section

    Ways to commit

    Intent
    • With intent that it shall be wilfully cast away, burnt, sunk or otherwise destroyed
    • With intent to injure or defraud an owner or insurer of such ship or vessel, or of any property laden on board the same
    Penalty
    • Up to 20 years in state prison, or a fine of up to $5,000 and up to 2½ years in jail

Simplified explanation

Loading, equipping or fitting out a ship or vessel, or helping to, intending it be destroyed in order to injure or defraud its owner or insurer, is punishable by up to 20 years in state prison.
