c. 266, § 16

Breaking and entering at night

Up to 20 years Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, in the night time, breaks and enters a building, ship, vessel or vehicle, with intent to commit a , or who to or does break, burn, blow up or otherwise injures or destroys a safe, vault or other depository of money, bonds or other valuables in any building, vehicle or place, with intent to commit a larceny or , whether he succeeds or fails in the perpetration of such larceny or , shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than twenty years or in a jail or house of correction for not more than two and one-half years.

2 crimes in this section

  1. Breaks and enters a building, ship, vessel or vehicle in the night time

    Intent
    • With intent to commit a felony
    Conditions
    • In the night time

  2. Attempts to or does break, burn, blow up or otherwise injure or destroy a safe, vault or other depository of money, bonds or other valuables in any building, vehicle or place

    Ways to commit
    • break · burn · blow up · otherwise injure or destroy
    Intent
    • With intent to commit a larceny or felony
    Conditions
    • Applies whether he succeeds or fails in the perpetration of the larceny or felony
    Also covers
    • + attempt
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Penalty
  • Up to 20 years in state prison or up to 2½ years in a jail or house of correction

Simplified explanation

Breaking into a building, ship or vehicle at night to commit a felony, or breaking or blowing open a safe or vault to commit larceny or a felony, is punishable by up to 20 years in state prison.
