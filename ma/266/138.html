c. 266, § 138

Dams or reservoirs; malicious injury

Up to 5 years Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever wilfully, intentionally and without right breaks down, injures, removes or destroys a dam, reservoir, canal or trench, or a gate, flume, flashboards or other appurtenances thereof, or a wheel, or mill gear, or machinery of a water mill or steam mill, or wilfully or , without color of right, draws off the water contained in a mill pond, reservoir, canal or trench, or obstructs such water from flowing out of the same, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than five years or by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars and imprisonment in jail for not more than two years.

2 crimes in this section

  1. Breaks down, injures, removes or destroys a dam, reservoir, canal or trench, or a gate, flume, flashboards or other appurtenances thereof, or a wheel, or mill gear, or machinery of a water mill or steam mill

  2. Draws off the water contained in a mill pond, reservoir, canal or trench, or obstructs such water from flowing out of the same

    Ways to commit
    • draws off the water · obstructs such water from flowing out
    Mental state
    • Wilfully or wantonly
    Conditions
    • Without color of right
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Penalty
  • Up to 5 years in state prison, or a fine of up to $500 and up to 2 years in jail

Simplified explanation

Wilfully damaging a dam, reservoir, canal, mill gear or mill machinery, or wilfully or wantonly draining or blocking water in a mill pond or reservoir, is punishable by up to 5 years in state prison.
