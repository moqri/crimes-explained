c. 266, § 30C

Possession, use, counterfeit, etc. of receipts, price ticket or universal product code label with intent to defraud; penalty

Up to 5 years Fraud & Money Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person who, with intent to cheat or defraud a retailer, possesses, uses, utters, transfers, makes, alters, counterfeits or reproduces a retail sales or return receipt, price ticket or universal product code label shall be punished by imprisonment in a house of correction for not more than 2½ years or by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than 5 years or by a fine of not more than $10,000, or by both such fine and imprisonment.

Simplified explanation

Possessing, using, making or counterfeiting retail receipts, price tickets or UPC labels to cheat a retailer is punishable by up to 5 years in state prison.
