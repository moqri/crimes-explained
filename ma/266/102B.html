c. 266, § 102B

Malicious explosion

Up to 25 years Weapons & Terrorism Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, without lawful authority, discharges, ignites or explodes any destructive or incendiary device or substance shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison by not less than 15 years nor more than 25 years or by a fine of $50,000 or by both such fine and imprisonment.

Simplified explanation

Without lawful authority willfully setting off or exploding a destructive or incendiary device or substance is punishable by 15 to 25 years in state prison, a $50,000 fine, or both.
