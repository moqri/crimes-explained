c. 266, § 80

Encumbered land; conveyance without notice

Up to 1 year Fraud & Money Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever conveys land, knowing that an encumbrance exists thereon, without informing the grantee, before the consideration is paid, of the existence and nature of such encumbrance, so far as he has knowledge thereof, shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than one year or by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars.

Simplified explanation

Conveying land known to be encumbered without telling the buyer about the encumbrance before payment is punishable by up to 1 year of imprisonment or a $1,000 fine.
