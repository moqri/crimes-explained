c. 266, § 121

Entry on land with firearms

Up to 61 days Weapons & Terrorism Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, without right, enters upon the land of another with firearms, with intent to fire or discharge them thereon, and, having been requested by the owner or occupant of such land or by his agent to leave such land, remains thereon, shall be punished by a fine of not more than two hundred dollars or by imprisonment for not more than two months, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Enters upon the land of another with firearms and, having been requested by the owner or occupant of such land or by his agent to leave such land, remains thereon

    Intent
    • With intent to fire or discharge the firearms thereon
    Conditions
    • Without right
    • Having been requested by the owner or occupant or his agent to leave, the person remains
    Penalty
    • A fine of up to two hundred dollars or imprisonment for up to two months, or both
