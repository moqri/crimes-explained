c. 266, § 143E

Violations of Secs. 143A to 143C; punishment

Up to 5 years Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever violates any provision of section 143A to section 143C, inclusive, shall be punished:

(i) by imprisonment for not more than 1 year in the or by a fine of not more than $25,000, or by both such fine and imprisonment;

(ii) by imprisonment in the house of correction for not more than 2 years or by a fine of not more than $100,000, or by both such fine and imprisonment if the offense involves less than 1,000 but not less than 100 unlawful sound recordings or less the 65 but not less than 7 unlawful audio visual recordings; or

(iii) by imprisonment in state prison for not more than 5 years or by a fine of not more than $250,000, or by both such fine and imprisonment if the offense involves not less than 1,000 unlawful sound recordings or not less than 65 unlawful audio visual recordings.

1 crime in this section

  1. Violates any provision of section 143A to section 143C, inclusive

    Penalty
    • Up to 1 year in the house of correction or a fine of up to $25,000, or both
    • If the offense involves less than 1,000 but not less than 100 unlawful sound recordings or less than 65 but not less than 7 unlawful audio visual recordings: Up to 2 years in the house of correction or a fine of up to $100,000, or both
    • If the offense involves not less than 1,000 unlawful sound recordings or not less than 65 unlawful audio visual recordings: Up to 5 years in state prison or a fine of up to $250,000, or both

Simplified explanation

Sets penalties for violating sections 143A to 143C on unauthorized recordings: up to 1 year in a house of correction, rising to up to 5 years in state prison for 1,000 or more sound or 65 or more audiovisual recordings.
