c. 266, § 12

Fire alarm, engine or apparatus; injury during fire to prevent alarm or extinction of fire

Up to 7 years Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, during the burning of a building or other property, wilfully and cuts or removes a bell rope or a wire or conduit connected with a fire alarm signal system or injures or disables any fire alarm signal box or any part of such system in the vicinity of such building or property, or otherwise prevents an alarm being given, or whoever cuts, injures or destroys an engine, hose or other fire apparatus, in said vicinity, or otherwise wilfully and prevents or obstructs the extinction of a fire shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than seven years or in jail for not more than two and one half years or by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars.

2 crimes in this section

  1. Cuts or removes a bell rope or a wire or conduit connected with a fire alarm signal system or injures or disables any fire alarm signal box or any part of such system in the vicinity of such building or property, or otherwise prevents an alarm being given

    Ways to commit
    • cuts or removes a bell rope, wire or conduit · injures or disables any fire alarm signal box or part of the system · otherwise prevents an alarm being given
    Conditions
    • During the burning of a building or other property

  2. Cuts, injures or destroys an engine, hose or other fire apparatus, in said vicinity, or otherwise prevents or obstructs the extinction of a fire

    Ways to commit
    • cuts, injures or destroys fire apparatus · otherwise prevents or obstructs the extinction of a fire
    Conditions
    • During the burning of a building or other property
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Intent
  • Wilfully and maliciously
Penalty
  • Up to 7 years in state prison, or up to 2½ years in jail, or a fine of up to $1,000

Simplified explanation

During a fire, wilfully and maliciously disabling a fire alarm system, preventing an alarm, damaging fire apparatus, or obstructing putting out the fire is punishable by up to 7 years in state prison.
