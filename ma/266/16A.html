c. 266, § 16A

Building, vessel or vehicle; breaking and entering with intent to commit misdemeanor

Up to 6 months Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever in the nighttime or daytime breaks and enters a building, ship, vessel or vehicle with intent to commit a shall be punished by a fine of not more than two hundred dollars or by imprisonment for not more than six months, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Breaks and enters a building, ship, vessel or vehicle in the nighttime or daytime

    Mental state
    • With intent to commit a misdemeanor
    Penalty
    • A fine of up to $200 or up to 6 months of imprisonment, or both

Simplified explanation

Breaking and entering a building, ship, vessel or vehicle, day or night, with intent to commit a misdemeanor is punishable by up to 6 months of imprisonment, a $200 fine, or both.
