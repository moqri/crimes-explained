c. 266, § 81

Attached land; conveyance without notice

Up to 3 years Fraud & Money Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, knowing that his land is attached on mesne process, sells and conveys it without giving notice of the attachment to the grantee, and with intent to defraud, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than three years or in jail for not more than one year.

1 crime in this section

  1. Sells and conveys his land without giving notice of the attachment to the grantee

    Knowledge
    • Knowing that his land is attached on mesne process
    Intent

    Penalty
    • Up to 3 years in state prison or up to 1 year in jail
