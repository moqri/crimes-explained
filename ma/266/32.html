c. 266, § 32

Fraudulent conversion of property by captain of vessel

See penalty details+ Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being a captain of a vessel, or fraudulently converts or appropriates money, goods or property, held or possessed by or delivered to him, which belong wholly or in part to the crew of such vessel, the owners of the vessel, or to those who have furnished supplies to the vessel, although he is a joint charterer or co-partner with the members of the crew or with the owners of the vessel, or with the person who furnished the supplies, shall be guilty of larceny.

1 crime in this section

  1. Embezzles or fraudulently converts or appropriates money, goods or property, held or possessed by or delivered to him

    Ways to commit
    • embezzles · fraudulently converts · appropriates
    Who
    • A captain of a vessel
    Conditions
    • The property belongs wholly or in part to the crew, the owners of the vessel, or those who furnished supplies to the vessel
    • Applies although he is a joint charterer or co-partner with the crew, owners or suppliers
    Penalty
    • Guilty of larceny; the punishment is set in another section of the law

Simplified explanation

A ship captain who embezzles or fraudulently converts money, goods or property belonging to the crew, owners or suppliers of the vessel is guilty of larceny.
