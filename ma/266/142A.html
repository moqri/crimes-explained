c. 266, § 142A

Gold, silver and platinum dealers; records; penalty

Up to 1 year Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever is in the business of purchasing gold, silver or platinum shall enter in a book kept for that purpose a description of the item, quantity purchased, the purchase price and the name and address of the seller; provided that the purchase price of such item is at least fifty dollars. Any person who sells gold, silver or platinum shall be required to show to the buyer prior to said sale identification which includes a photograph of said seller. Said book shall at all times be open to the inspection of the chief of police of a city or town or of any other officer having similar duties or any officer authorized by either of them, or a state police officer. Whoever violates any provision of this section shall be punished by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars or imprisonment of not more than one year, or both such fine and imprisonment.

3 crimes in this section

  1. Fails to enter in a book kept for that purpose a description of the item, quantity purchased, the purchase price and the name and address of the seller

    Who
    • A person in the business of purchasing gold, silver or platinum
    Conditions
    • The purchase price of such item is at least $50

  2. Fails to keep said book at all times open to the inspection of the chief of police of a city or town or of any other officer having similar duties or any officer authorized by either of them, or a state police officer

    Who
    • A person in the business of purchasing gold, silver or platinum

  3. Fails to show to the buyer prior to said sale identification which includes a photograph of said seller

    Who
    • A person who sells gold, silver or platinum
Applies to all 3 crimes above
Penalty
  • A fine of up to $1,000 or up to 1 year imprisonment, or both

Simplified explanation

Gold, silver and platinum buyers must record purchases of $50 or more in a book open to police, and sellers must show photo ID; violations bring up to 1 year in prison, a $1,000 fine, or both.
