c. 266, § 13

Fire engines; wanton or malicious injury

Up to 2 years Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever wantonly or injures a fire engine or other fire apparatus shall be punished by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars or by imprisonment for not more than two years, and shall be further ordered to recognize with sufficient surety or sureties for his good behavior during such term as the court shall order.

1 crime in this section

  1. Injures a fire engine or other fire apparatus

    Intent
    • Wantonly or maliciously
    Penalty
    • A fine of up to $500 or up to 2 years of imprisonment
    Other consequences
    • Ordered to recognize with sufficient surety or sureties for good behavior during such term as the court shall order

Simplified explanation

Wantonly or maliciously injuring a fire engine or other fire apparatus is punishable by up to 2 years of imprisonment or a $500 fine, plus a court-ordered good-behavior bond.
