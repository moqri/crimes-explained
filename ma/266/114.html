c. 266, § 114

Trees and fences; malicious injury

Up to 6 months Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever wilfully and or wantonly breaks glass in a building which is not his own, or whoever wilfully and breaks down, injures, mars or defaces a fence belonging to or enclosing land which is not his own, or wilfully and throws down or opens a gate, bars or fence, and leaves the same down or open, or and injuriously severs from the freehold of another any produce thereof or anything attached thereto, shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than six months or by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars.

4 crimes in this section

  1. Breaks glass in a building which is not his own

    Intent
    • Wilfully and maliciously or wantonly

  2. Breaks down, injures, mars or defaces a fence belonging to or enclosing land which is not his own

    Ways to commit
    • breaks down · injures · mars · defaces
    Intent
    • Wilfully and maliciously

  3. Throws down or opens a gate, bars or fence, and leaves the same down or open

    Ways to commit
    • throws down · opens
    Intent
    • Wilfully and maliciously

  4. Severs from the freehold of another any produce thereof or anything attached thereto

    Intent
    • Maliciously and injuriously
Applies to all 4 crimes above
Penalty
  • Up to six months imprisonment or a fine of up to five hundred dollars

Simplified explanation

Wilfully and maliciously (or wantonly) breaking glass in another's building, damaging fences or gates, or severing produce from another's land is punishable by up to six months or a $500 fine.
