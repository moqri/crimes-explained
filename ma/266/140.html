c. 266, § 140

Sale or offer for sale of master keys; penalty

Up to 1 year Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever sells or offers to sell or solicits offers to purchase a master key designed to fit more than one motor vehicle knowing, or , that said key will be used for an illegal purpose shall be punished by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars or by imprisonment for not more than one year, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Sells or offers to sell or solicits offers to purchase a master key designed to fit more than one motor vehicle

    Ways to commit
    • sells · offers to sell · solicits offers to purchase
    Knowledge
    • Knowing, or having reasonable cause to believe, that said key will be used for an illegal purpose
    Penalty
    • A fine of up to $500 or up to 1 year imprisonment, or both
