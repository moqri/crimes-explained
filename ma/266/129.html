c. 266, § 129

Correctional institutions; injury to property

Up to 3 years Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

An inmate of a correctional institution of the commonwealth who wilfully and destroys or injures the property of the commonwealth at such correctional institution, or the property of any person who furnishes materials for the employment of the prisoners, may be sentenced to the for not more than three years.
