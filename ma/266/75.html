c. 266, § 75

Obtaining property by trick

See penalty details+ Fraud & Money Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, by a game, device, sleight of hand, pretended fortune telling or by any trick or other means by the use of cards or other implements or instruments, fraudulently obtains from another person property of any description shall be punished as in the case of larceny of property of like value.

1 crime in this section

Simplified explanation

Fraudulently obtaining property by a game, sleight of hand, pretended fortune telling, or a trick with cards or other implements is punished the same as larceny of property of like value.
