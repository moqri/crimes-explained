c. 266, § 43

Paper designated for bank bills; retention by printer with intent to pass

Up to life Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, having been employed to print or having assisted in printing a printed piece of paper or blank described in the preceding section, or having been intrusted with the care or custody thereof, retains it in his possession without the knowledge and consent of the corporation for which it was printed, with intent to injure or defraud either by uttering or passing it or causing or allowing it to be uttered or passed as true, either with or without alteration or addition, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for life or for any term of years.

1 crime in this section

  1. Retains it in his possession without the knowledge and consent of the corporation for which it was printed

    Who
    • A person employed to print, or who assisted in printing, or was intrusted with the care or custody of, the paper or blank described in the preceding section
    Intent
    • With intent to injure or defraud either by uttering or passing it or causing or allowing it to be uttered or passed as true, with or without alteration or addition
    Penalty
    • Life or any term of years in state prison

Simplified explanation

A printer or custodian who keeps bank-bill paper or blanks without the corporation's consent, intending to pass them as true, is punishable by life or any term of years in state prison.
