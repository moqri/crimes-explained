c. 266, § 69

Insignia of societies; unlawful use

Up to 29 days Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, not being a member of a society, association or labor union, for the purpose of representing that he is a member thereof, wilfully wears or uses the insignia, ribbon, badge, rosette, button or emblem thereof, if it has been registered in the office of the state secretary, shall be punished by a fine of not more than twenty dollars or by imprisonment for not more than one month, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Wears or uses the insignia, ribbon, badge, rosette, button or emblem of a society, association or labor union

    Ways to commit
    • wears · uses
    Who
    • A person who is not a member of the society, association or labor union
    Intent
    • Wilfully
    • For the purpose of representing that he is a member thereof
    Conditions
    • The insignia, ribbon, badge, rosette, button or emblem has been registered in the office of the state secretary
    Penalty
    • A fine of up to $20 or imprisonment for up to 1 month, or both

Simplified explanation

A non-member who wilfully wears or uses a registered society, association or labor union insignia to pose as a member faces up to one month in prison, a $20 fine, or both.
