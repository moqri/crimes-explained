c. 266, § 38A

Construction loan; misapplication

Up to 1 year Fraud & Money Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever obtains a building or construction loan, secured by a mortgage of real estate, for the payment for labor furnished or to be furnished and/or materials used and/or employed or to be used and/or employed in the construction, repair, removal or alteration of a building or other structure which is attached or is to be attached to such real estate, and, before payment in full for all labor furnished or to be furnished and/or materials used or to be used and/or employed or to be employed as aforesaid, applies the proceeds of such loan, or any part thereof, to any use other than payment for labor and/or materials as aforesaid, shall be punished by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars or by imprisonment in jail for not more than one year, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Applies the proceeds of such loan, or any part thereof, to any use other than payment for labor and/or materials

    Who
    • A person who obtains a building or construction loan secured by a mortgage of real estate
    Conditions
    • The loan was obtained for payment for labor and/or materials for the construction, repair, removal or alteration of a building or other structure attached or to be attached to the real estate
    • Before payment in full for all labor furnished or to be furnished and materials used or to be used
    Penalty
    • A fine of up to $500, or up to 1 year in jail, or both

Simplified explanation

Using the proceeds of a mortgage-secured construction loan for anything other than paying labor and materials before they are paid in full is punishable by up to 1 year in jail or a $500 fine.
