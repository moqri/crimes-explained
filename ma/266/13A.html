c. 266, § 13A

Duty of hotel manager to notify fire department and sound alarm; penalty

Up to 2½ years Courts & Justice Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

The manager of a hotel or family hotel or such other person as may be in charge of the premises in the absence of the manager, shall, as soon as he becomes aware that there is a fire therein, notify the fire department and, if such fire, or heat, smoke or gas therefrom, threatens to spread to rooms occupied by guests, sound the alarm system required by the state building code.

Whoever violates any provision of this section shall be punished by imprisonment in a jail or house of correction for not more than two and a half years or by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars.

2 crimes in this section

  1. Fails to notify the fire department as soon as he becomes aware that there is a fire therein

    Conditions
    • There is a fire in the hotel
    • He is aware of the fire

  2. Fails to sound the alarm system required by the state building code

    Conditions
    • The fire, or heat, smoke or gas therefrom, threatens to spread to rooms occupied by guests
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Who
  • The manager of a hotel or family hotel, or such other person as may be in charge of the premises in the absence of the manager
Penalty
  • Up to 2½ years in a jail or house of correction or a fine of up to $1,000

Simplified explanation

A hotel manager, or whoever is in charge, who fails to notify the fire department of a fire and sound the required alarm when it threatens guest rooms can get up to 2½ years in jail.
