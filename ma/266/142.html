c. 266, § 142

Record of purchases by dealers in scrap copper wire; inspection; penalty

Fine only Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever is in the business of purchasing copper line wire or scrap copper wire shall enter in a book kept for that purpose a description of the same, the quantity purchased, the purchase price and a name and address of the seller. Said book shall at all times be open to the inspection of the chief of police of a city or town or any other officer having similar duties or any officer authorized by either of them, or a state police officer. Whoever violates any provision of this section shall be punished by a fine of not more than fifty dollars.

2 crimes in this section

  1. Fails to enter in a book kept for that purpose a description of the same, the quantity purchased, the purchase price and a name and address of the seller

  2. Fails to keep said book at all times open to the inspection of the chief of police of a city or town or any other officer having similar duties or any officer authorized by either of them, or a state police officer

Applies to all 2 crimes above
Who
  • A person in the business of purchasing copper line wire or scrap copper wire
Penalty
  • A fine of up to $50

Simplified explanation

A business buying copper line wire or scrap copper wire that fails to record each purchase and seller in a book open to police inspection faces a fine of up to $50.
