c. 266, § 59

Simple larceny; embezzlement from voluntary association

See penalty details Fraud & Money Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever or fraudulently converts, or secretes with intent to embezzle or fraudulently convert, money, goods or property or any part thereof which has been delivered to him, which may be the subject of larceny and which belong to any organization of the volunteer militia, post of the Grand Army of the Republic, or other voluntary association, shall be guilty of simple larceny, although he is a member of such organization or voluntary association and, as such, entitled to an interest in the property thereof. In a prosecution under this section, it shall be sufficient to describe such organization or association by the name by which it is generally known and as a voluntary association.

1 crime in this section

  1. Embezzles or fraudulently converts, or secretes, money, goods or property or any part thereof which has been delivered to him, which may be the subject of larceny and which belong to any organization of the volunteer militia, post of the Grand Army of the Republic, or other voluntary association

    Ways to commit
    • embezzles · fraudulently converts · secretes with intent to embezzle or fraudulently convert
    Intent
    • With intent to embezzle or fraudulently convert (when secreting)
    Conditions
    • The property may be the subject of larceny
    • The property belongs to an organization of the volunteer militia, post of the Grand Army of the Republic, or other voluntary association
    Penalty
    • Guilty of simple larceny; penalty not set in this section
    Other consequences
    • Applies although he is a member of the organization or association and entitled to an interest in its property
