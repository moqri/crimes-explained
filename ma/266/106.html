c. 266, § 106

Ice ponds; injury

Fine only Property Crimes Ch. 266 · Crimes Against Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever wilfully, intentionally and without right or license, cuts, injures, mars or otherwise damages or destroys ice upon waters from which ice is or may be taken as an article of merchandise, whereby the taking thereof is hindered or the value thereof diminished for that purpose, shall be punished by a fine of not more than one hundred dollars.

1 crime in this section

    Ways to commit
    • cuts · injures · mars · damages · destroys
    Intent
    • Wilfully, intentionally and without right or license
    Conditions

    Penalty
    • A fine of up to $100

Simplified explanation

Wilfully and without right damaging ice on waters where ice is harvested for sale, so that taking it is hindered or its value reduced, is punishable by a fine of up to $100.
