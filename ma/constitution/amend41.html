Amend. Art. XLI

Taxing wild or forest lands

Power Taxes, Money & Commerce Annulled Amendments · Articles of Amendment

[Full power and authority are hereby given and granted to the to prescribe for wild or forest lands such methods of taxation as will develop and conserve the forest resources of the commonwealth.]
