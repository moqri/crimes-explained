Decl. of Rights, Art. XV

Right to a jury in civil cases

Right The Courts Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

In all controversies concerning property, and in all suits between two or more persons, except in cases in which it has heretofore been otherways used and practiced, the parties have a right to a trial by jury; and this method of procedure shall be held sacred, unless, in causes arising on the high seas, and such as relate to mariners' wages, the legislature shall hereafter find it necessary to alter it. .

Note (Massachusetts Legislature): [See Amendments, Art. XLVIII, The Initiative, II, sec. 2]

Simplified explanation

In controversies about property and suits between persons, the parties have a right to a jury trial, which shall be held sacred, except where other practice was used before, and unless the legislature finds it necessary to alter it in high-seas and mariners' wages cases.
