Pt. 2, c. I, § III, Art. I

Representation of the people

Structure The Legislature Partly superseded Pt. 2, c. I · Legislative Power

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

There shall be, in the legislature of this commonwealth, a representation of the people, [annually] elected, and founded upon the principle of equality.

Note (Massachusetts Legislature): [See Amendments, Art. LXIV.]

Simplified explanation

The legislature shall include a representation of the people, elected and founded upon the principle of equality. The bracketed word "annually" is printed as superseded.
