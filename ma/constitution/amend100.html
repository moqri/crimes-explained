Amend. Art. C

Voting age changed to eighteen

Structure Voting & Elections Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Article III of the Amendments to the Constitution, as amended, is hereby further amended by striking out the word indicating the age at which a citizen shall have a right to vote in an election of Governor and other public officers and inserting in place thereof the following word: -- eighteen.
