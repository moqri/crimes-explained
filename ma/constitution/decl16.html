Decl. of Rights, Art. XVI

Liberty of the press

Right Individual Rights Annulled Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

[The liberty of the press is essential to the security of freedom in a state: it ought not, therefore, to be restrained in this commonwealth.]

Note (Massachusetts Legislature): [See Amendments, Art. XLVIII, The Initiative, II, sec. 2.]

Simplified explanation

Annulled: This article, printed in brackets as superseded, said the liberty of the press is essential to the security of freedom and ought not to be restrained in this commonwealth. A bracketed note in the text says it was annulled and superseded by Amendments, Art. LXXVII.
