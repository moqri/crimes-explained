Pt. 2, c. II, § III, Art. VII

Order of legislative elections

Structure The Legislature Annulled Pt. 2, c. II · Executive Power

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

[And whereas the elections appointed to be made by this constitution, on the last Wednesday in May annually, by the two houses of the legislature, may not be completed on that day, the said elections may be adjourned from day to day until the same shall be completed. And the order of elections shall be as follows: the vacancies in the senate, if any, shall first be filled up; the governor and lieutenant governor shall then be elected, provided there should be no choice of them by the people: and afterwards the two houses shall proceed to the election of the council.]

Note (Massachusetts Legislature): [See Amendments, Art. XIV.]

Note (Massachusetts Legislature): [Superseded by Amendments, Arts. XVI and XXV.]

Simplified explanation

Annulled: This article let the May elections by the two houses be adjourned day to day until completed, and set their order: senate vacancies first, then governor and lieutenant governor if the people made no choice, then the council. A note says it was superseded by Amendments, Arts. XVI and XXV.
