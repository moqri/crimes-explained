Decl. of Rights, Art. XXIV

No ex post facto laws

Limit Criminal Justice Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Laws made to punish for actions done before the existence of such laws, and which have not been declared crimes by preceding laws, are unjust, oppressive, and inconsistent with the fundamental principles of a free government.

  1. Laws made to punish for actions done before the existence of such laws, and which have not been declared crimes by preceding laws, are unjust, oppressive, and inconsistent with the fundamental principles of a free government limit

Simplified explanation

Laws that punish actions done before those laws existed, and that were not declared crimes by earlier laws, are unjust, oppressive and inconsistent with the fundamental principles of a free government.
