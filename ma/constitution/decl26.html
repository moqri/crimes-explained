Decl. of Rights, Art. XXVI

No excessive bail or cruel punishment

Limit Criminal Justice Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

No magistrate or court of law, shall demand excessive or sureties, impose excessive fines, or inflict cruel or unusual punishments.

Note (Massachusetts Legislature): [See Amendments, Art. XLVIII, The Initiative, II, sec. 2, and CXVI.]
