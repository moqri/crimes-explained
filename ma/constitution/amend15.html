Amend. Art. XV

Day of the state election

Structure Voting & Elections Superseded Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

[The meeting for the choice of governor, lieutenant-governor, senators and representatives, shall be held on the Tuesday next after the first Monday in November, annually; but in case of a failure to elect representatives on that day, a second meeting shall be holden for that purpose on the fourth Monday of the same month of November.]
