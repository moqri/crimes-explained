Decl. of Rights, Art. XXVII

Quartering of soldiers

Limit Military & Militia Declaration of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

In time of peace, no soldier ought to be quartered in any house without the consent of the owner; and in time of war, such quarters ought not to be made but by the civil magistrate, in a manner ordained by the legislature.

Simplified explanation

In peacetime no soldier ought to be quartered in any house without the owner's consent. In wartime, quarters ought to be made only by the civil magistrate, in a manner ordained by the legislature.
