Amend. Art. LXXVII

Liberty of the press and free speech

Right Individual Rights Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Article XVI of Part the First is hereby annulled and the following is adopted in place thereof:

Article XVI. The liberty of the press is essential to the security of freedom in a state: it ought not, therefore, to be restrained in this commonwealth. The right of free speech shall not be abridged.
