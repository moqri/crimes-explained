Amend. Art. XCVI

State loans for higher education

Power Education & Religion Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The shall have power to authorize the commonwealth to make loans, on such terms as it may deem reasonable, to any residents of the commonwealth for tuition and board at any college, university or other institution of higher learning.
