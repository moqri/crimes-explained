Amend. Art. LXX

Limited town meetings in larger towns

Power Local Government Annulled Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Article II of the articles of amendment to the constitution of the commonwealth is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof the following new paragraph: --

Nothing in this article shall prevent the from establishing in any corporate town or towns in this commonwealth containing more than six thousand inhabitants a form of town government providing for a town meeting limited to such inhabitants of the town as may be elected to meet, deliberate, act and vote in the exercise of the corporate powers of the town subject to such restrictions and regulations as the may prescribe; provided, that such establishment be with the consent, and on the application of a majority of the inhabitants of such town, present and voting thereon, pursuant to a vote at a meeting duly warned and holden for that purpose.

Note (Massachusetts Legislature): [Annulled by Amendments, Art. LXXXIX.]
