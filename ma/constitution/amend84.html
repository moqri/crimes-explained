Amend. Art. LXXXIV

Giving or lending the commonwealth's credit

Limit Taxes, Money & Commerce Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Article LXII of the Amendments to the Constitution is hereby amended by striking out section 1 and inserting in place thereof the following section: -

Section 1. The commonwealth may give, loan or pledge its credit only by a vote, taken by the yeas and nays, of two-thirds of each house of the present and voting thereon. The credit of the commonwealth shall not in any manner be given or loaned to or in aid of any individual, or of any private association, or of any corporation which is privately owned and managed.
