Amend. Art. XLIII

Taking land to relieve congestion and provide homes

Power The Legislature Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The shall have power to authorize the commonwealth to take land and to hold, improve, sub-divide, build upon and sell the same, for the purpose of relieving congestion of population and providing homes for citizens; provided, however, that this amendment shall not be deemed to authorize the sale of such land or buildings at less than the cost thereof.

Simplified explanation

The general court may authorize the commonwealth to take land and to hold, improve, sub-divide, build upon and sell it to relieve congestion of population and provide homes for citizens, but not to sell such land or buildings at less than cost.
