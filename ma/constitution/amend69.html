Amend. Art. LXIX

Sex no bar to office; women re-registering after name change

Limit Equality & Citizenship Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Section 1. No person shall be deemed to be ineligible to hold state, county or municipal office by reason of sex.

No person shall be deemed ineligible to hold state, county or municipal office by reason of sex. It also amends Article IV so that a woman who changes her name shall re-register under her new name and pay a fee set by the general court.
