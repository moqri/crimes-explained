Amend. Art. XLVIII, General Provisions, V. The Veto Power of the Governor

Veto power over amending laws approved by the people

Power The Legislature Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Subject to the veto power of the governor and to the right of referendum by petition as herein provided, the may amend or repeal a law approved by the people.
