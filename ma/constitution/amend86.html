Amend. Art. LXXXVI

Governor and lieutenant-governor voted on as a group

Structure Voting & Elections Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Names of candidates of political parties for the offices of governor and lieutenant-governor shall be grouped on the official ballot for use at state elections according to the parties they represent, and the voter may cast a single vote for any such group, which shall count as a vote for each candidate in such group, but may not cast a vote for only one of the candidates in such group.

Simplified explanation

Party candidates for governor and lieutenant-governor are grouped on the state election ballot by party; a voter may cast a single vote for a group, counting for each candidate in it, but may not vote for only one of them.
