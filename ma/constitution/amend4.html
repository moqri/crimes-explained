Amend. Art. IV

Notaries public and other appointments

Structure The Governor & Executive Partly superseded Amendments · Articles of Amendment

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Notaries public shall be appointed by the governor in the same manner as judicial officers are appointed, and shall hold their offices during seven years, unless sooner removed by the governor with the consent of the council, upon the address of both houses of the legislature. .

[In case the office of secretary or treasurer of the commonwealth shall become vacant from any cause during the recess of the , the governor, with the advice and consent of the council, shall nominate and appoint, under such regulations as may be prescribed by law, a competent and suitable person to such vacant office, who shall hold the same until a successor shall be appointed by the .]

[Whenever the exigencies of the commonwealth shall require the appointment of a commissary-general, he shall be nominated, appointed and commissioned in such manner as the legislature may, by law, prescribe.

All officers commissioned to command in the militia may be removed from office in such manner as the legislature may, by law, prescribe.]

Note (Massachusetts Legislature): [See Amendments, Articles XXXVII, LXII, and LXIX, section 2]

Note (Massachusetts Legislature): [This paragraph superseded by Amendments, Art. XVII.]

Note (Massachusetts Legislature): [Last two paragraphs annulled and superseded by Amendments, Art. LIII.]
