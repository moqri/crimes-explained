c. 268, § 14

Receipt of gift by juror, arbitrator, umpire, referee, master or auditor

Up to 5 years Courts & Justice Ch. 268 · Crimes Against Public Justice

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being summoned as a juror or chosen or appointed as an arbitrator, umpire or referee, or, being a master in chancery, master or auditor, takes anything to give his verdict, award or report, or receives any gift or gratuity from a party to a suit, cause or proceeding for the trial or decision of which such juror has been summoned, or for the hearing or determination of which such master in chancery, master, auditor, arbitrator, umpire or referee has been chosen or appointed, shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than five years or by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars and imprisonment in jail for not more than one year.

2 crimes in this section

  1. Takes anything to give his verdict, award or report

  2. Receives any gift or gratuity from a party to a suit, cause or proceeding for the trial or decision of which such juror has been summoned, or for the hearing or determination of which such master in chancery, master, auditor, arbitrator, umpire or referee has been chosen or appointed

Applies to all 2 crimes above
Who
  • A person summoned as a juror, or chosen or appointed as an arbitrator, umpire or referee, or a master in chancery, master or auditor
Mental state
  • Corruptly
Penalty
  • Up to 5 years in state prison, or a fine of up to $1,000 and up to 1 year in jail

Simplified explanation

A juror, arbitrator, umpire, referee, master or auditor who corruptly takes anything to give a verdict or receives a gift from a party faces up to 5 years in state prison.
