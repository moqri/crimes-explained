c. 268, § 21A

Officer or other employee of penal or correctional institution; sexual relations with inmate; punishment

Up to 5 years Sex Crimes & Exploitation Ch. 268 · Crimes Against Public Justice

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

An officer or other person who is employed by or contracts with any penal or correctional institution in the commonwealth, and who, in the course of such employment or contract or as a result thereof, engages in sexual relations with an inmate confined therein, within or outside of such institution, or an inmate who is otherwise under the direct custodial supervision and control of such officer or other person, shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than five years in a or by a fine of $10,000 or both. In a prosecution commenced under this section, an inmate shall be deemed incapable of consent to sexual relations with such person. For purposes of this section, sexual relations shall include intentional, inappropriate contact of a sexual nature, including, but not limited to conduct prohibited by section 22 or 24 of chapter 265 or section 2, 3, 35 or 53A of chapter 272.

1 crime in this section

  1. Engages in sexual relations with an inmate confined therein, within or outside of such institution, or an inmate who is otherwise under the direct custodial supervision and control of such officer or other person

    Who
    • An officer or other person who is employed by or contracts with any penal or correctional institution in the commonwealth
    Conditions
    • It is in the course of such employment or contract or as a result thereof
    Penalty
    • Up to 5 years in a state prison, or a fine of $10,000, or both
    Other consequences
    • In a prosecution under this section, an inmate is deemed incapable of consent to sexual relations with such person
    Key terms
    • "Sexual relations": includes intentional, inappropriate contact of a sexual nature, including, but not limited to, conduct prohibited by other sections named in this section
