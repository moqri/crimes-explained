c. 268, § 32A

Interference with fire fighting operations

Up to 5 years Courts & Justice Ch. 268 · Crimes Against Public Justice

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever obstructs, interferes with or hinders a fire fighter in the lawful performance of his duty, or whoever obstructs, interferes with or hinders a fire fighting force in the lawful performance of its duty, shall be punished by a fine of not less than one hundred nor more than one thousand dollars or by imprisonment in a jail or house of correction for not less than thirty days nor more than two and one half years or by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than five years, or by both such fine and imprisonment in a jail or house of correction.

1 crime in this section

  1. Obstructs, interferes with or hinders a fire fighter or a fire fighting force in the lawful performance of his or its duty

    Ways to commit
    • obstructs · interferes with · hinders
    Intent
    • Willfully
    Conditions
    • The fire fighter or fire fighting force is in the lawful performance of duty
    Penalty
    • A fine of not less than $100 nor more than $1,000, or imprisonment in a jail or house of correction for not less than 30 days nor more than 2½ years, or imprisonment in the state prison for up to 5 years, or both such fine and imprisonment in a jail or house of correctionmandatory minimum

Simplified explanation

Willfully obstructing, interfering with or hindering a fire fighter or fire fighting force in the lawful performance of duty is punishable by up to 5 years in state prison.
