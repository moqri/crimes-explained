c. 268, § 21

Suffering convict to be at large, visited, relieved or comforted

Fine only Courts & Justice Ch. 268 · Crimes Against Public Justice

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

An officer or person who, being employed in the , suffers a convict under sentence of solitary imprisonment to be at large or out of the cell assigned to him, or suffers any convict confined in the prison to be at large out of the prison, or to be visited, conversed with or in any way relieved or comforted, contrary to the regulations of the prison, shall be punished by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars.

3 crimes in this section

  1. Suffers a convict under sentence of solitary imprisonment to be at large or out of the cell assigned to him

  2. Suffers any convict confined in the prison to be at large out of the prison

  3. Suffers any convict confined in the prison to be visited, conversed with or in any way relieved or comforted

    Conditions
    • It is contrary to the regulations of the prison
Applies to all 3 crimes above
Who
  • An officer or person employed in the state prison
Penalty
  • A fine of up to $500

Simplified explanation

A state prison employee who lets a convict be at large, or be visited or comforted contrary to prison regulations, faces a fine of up to $500.
