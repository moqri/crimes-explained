c. 268, § 15

Aiding escape from a correctional institution or jail; rescue

Up to 10 years Courts & Justice Ch. 268 · Crimes Against Public Justice

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever conveys into a correctional institution of the commonwealth or into a jail, , house of reformation or like place of confinement, a disguise, instrument, tool, weapon or other thing which is adapted or useful to aid a prisoner in making his escape, with intent to aid the escape of a prisoner, or whoever, by any means, aids or assists such prisoner in endeavoring to escape therefrom, whether such escape is effected or attempted or not, and whoever forcibly or fraudulently rescues or to rescue a prisoner held in custody upon a conviction or charge of crime, shall, if the person whose escape or rescue was effected or intended is a convict under sentence to the or is charged with a , be punished by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars or by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than ten years; but if he is a convict under sentence to any other of said institutions, by imprisonment in the state prison for not more than seven years; and if he is charged with a , then by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars or by imprisonment in jail for not more than two years.

3 crimes in this section

  1. Conveys into a correctional institution of the commonwealth or into a jail, house of correction, house of reformation or like place of confinement, a disguise, instrument, tool, weapon or other thing which is adapted or useful to aid a prisoner in making his escape

    Intent
    • With intent to aid the escape of a prisoner

  2. Aids or assists such prisoner in endeavoring to escape therefrom, by any means

    Conditions
    • It does not matter whether the escape is effected or attempted or not

  3. Forcibly or fraudulently rescues or attempts to rescue a prisoner held in custody upon a conviction or charge of crime

    Ways to commit
    • forcibly rescues · fraudulently rescues
    Also covers
    • + attempt
Applies to all 3 crimes above
Penalty
  • If the person whose escape or rescue was effected or intended is a convict under sentence to the state prison or is charged with a felony: a fine of up to $500 or up to 10 years in state prison
  • If the person is a convict under sentence to any other of said institutions: Up to 7 years in state prison
  • If the person is charged with a misdemeanor: A fine of up to $500 or up to 2 years in jail

Simplified explanation

Conveying escape tools into a jail or prison, helping a prisoner escape, or rescuing a prisoner is punishable by up to 10 years in state prison, depending on the prisoner's status.
