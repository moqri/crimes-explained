c. 268, § 18

Jailer or officer suffering prisoner to escape

See penalty details Courts & Justice Ch. 268 · Crimes Against Public Justice

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A jailer or officer who, except as provided in the following section, voluntarily suffers a prisoner in his custody upon conviction or upon a charge of crime to escape shall suffer the punishment and penalties to which the prisoner whom he suffered to escape was sentenced or would be liable to suffer upon conviction of the crime wherewith he stood charged.

1 crime in this section

  1. Suffers a prisoner in his custody upon conviction or upon a charge of crime to escape

    Who
    • A jailer or officer
    Mental state
    • Voluntarily
    Exceptions
    • Except as provided in the following section
    Penalty
    • The punishment and penalties to which the prisoner whom he suffered to escape was sentenced or would be liable to suffer upon conviction of the crime wherewith he stood charged

Simplified explanation

A jailer or officer who voluntarily lets a prisoner in custody escape suffers the same punishment the prisoner was sentenced to or would face for the charged crime.
