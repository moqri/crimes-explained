c. 268, § 17

Aiding escape from officer or person having custody

Up to 2 years Courts & Justice Ch. 268 · Crimes Against Public Justice

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever aids or assists a prisoner in escaping or attempting to escape from an officer or person who has the lawful custody of such prisoner shall be punished by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars or by imprisonment for not more than two years.

1 crime in this section

Simplified explanation

Aiding a prisoner to escape or attempt to escape from an officer or person with lawful custody is punishable by up to 2 years in prison or a $500 fine.
