c. 268, § 15A

Escapes from jail

Up to 2½ years Courts & Justice Ch. 268 · Crimes Against Public Justice

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, after lawfully being placed in custody in a jail of a city or town, escapes from any such jail shall be punished by imprisonment in a jail or house of correction for not more than two and one-half years, or by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Escapes from any such jail

    Conditions
    • The person was lawfully placed in custody in a jail of a city or town
    Penalty
    • Up to 2½ years in a jail or house of correction, or a fine of up to $500, or both

Simplified explanation

Escaping from a city or town jail after being lawfully placed in custody is punishable by up to 2½ years in jail or house of correction, a $500 fine, or both.
