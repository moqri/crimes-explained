c. 268, § 29

Delivery, or permission of delivery, by officers, of alcoholic beverages, to prisoners; keeping together prisoners of different sexes or classes

Fine only Courts & Justice Ch. 268 · Crimes Against Public Justice

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

A sheriff, jailer, superintendent of a or officer of a correctional institution who, under any pretence, gives, sells or delivers or knowingly permits to be given, sold or delivered to a prisoner in his custody any alcoholic beverages, as defined in section one of chapter one hundred and thirty-eight, or cider, unless the physician of the penal institution certifies in writing that the health of the prisoner requires the same; or such sheriff, jailer, superintendent of a or officer of a correctional institution who willingly or suffers such prisoner to have or drink any alcoholic beverages, as so defined, or who places or keeps together prisoners in his custody of different sexes or classes, contrary to section twenty-two of chapter one hundred and twenty-seven, shall forfeit twenty-five dollars for the first offence and fifty dollars for any offence committed subsequent to the first conviction, and, upon such second conviction, shall be removed from office, and be ineligible to hold the office of sheriff, deputy sheriff, jailer, superintendent or keeper of any correctional institution for five years thereafter. If the physician certifies that the health of the prisoner requires such liquor, the prisoner shall be allowed the quantity prescribed and no more.

3 crimes in this section

  1. Gives, sells or delivers or knowingly permits to be given, sold or delivered to a prisoner in his custody any alcoholic beverages or cider

    Ways to commit
    • gives · sells · delivers · knowingly permits to be given, sold or delivered
    Knowledge
    • Knowingly (as to permitting)
    Exceptions
    • The physician of the penal institution certifies in writing that the health of the prisoner requires the same

  2. Willingly or negligently suffers such prisoner to have or drink any alcoholic beverages

    Mental state
    • Willingly or negligently
    Exceptions
    • The physician certifies that the health of the prisoner requires such liquor, in which case the prisoner is allowed the quantity prescribed and no more

  3. Places or keeps together prisoners in his custody of different sexes or classes

    Conditions
    • Contrary to the section of chapter one hundred and twenty-seven named in this section
Applies to all 3 crimes above
Who
  • A sheriff, jailer, superintendent of a house of correction or officer of a correctional institution
Penalty
  • Forfeit $25
  • If the offence is committed after a first conviction: Forfeit $50
Other consequences
  • Upon a second conviction, removal from office and ineligibility to hold the office of sheriff, deputy sheriff, jailer, superintendent or keeper of any correctional institution for five years thereafter

Simplified explanation

A sheriff, jailer or correctional officer who gives or permits alcohol to a prisoner, or keeps prisoners of different sexes or classes together, forfeits $25 or $50 and is removed from office on a second conviction.
