§ 140.25

Burglary in the second degree

Class C felony Property Crimes Art. 140 · Burglary and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of burglary in the second degree when he knowingly enters or remains unlawfully in a building with intent to commit a crime therein, and when:

1. In effecting entry or while in the building or in immediate flight therefrom, he or another participant in the crime:

(a) Is armed with explosives or a ; or

(b) Causes to any person who is not a participant in the crime; or

(c) Uses or threatens the immediate use of a ; or

(d) Displays what appears to be a pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, machine gun or other firearm; or

2. The building is a dwelling.

  1. 1(a) Enters or remains unlawfully in a building

Simplified explanation

Burglary in the second degree is burglary of a building while armed, injuring someone, using or threatening a dangerous instrument, or displaying an apparent firearm, or burglary of a dwelling; it is a class C felony.
