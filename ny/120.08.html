§ 120.08

Assault on a peace officer, police officer, firefighter or emergency medical services professional

Class C felony Violence & Threats Art. 120 · Assault and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of assault on a peace officer, police officer, firefighter or emergency medical services professional when, with intent to prevent a peace officer, police officer, a firefighter, including a firefighter acting as a paramedic or emergency medical technician administering first aid in the course of performance of duty as such firefighter, or an emergency medical service paramedic or emergency medical service technician, from performing a lawful duty, he or she causes to such peace officer, police officer, firefighter, paramedic or technician.

Simplified explanation

Assault on a peace officer, police officer, firefighter or emergency medical services professional covers causing serious physical injury to prevent the lawful duty; it is a class C felony.
