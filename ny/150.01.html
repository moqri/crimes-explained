§ 150.01

Arson in the fifth degree

Class A misdemeanor Property Crimes Art. 150 · Arson

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of arson in the fifth degree when he or she intentionally damages property of another without consent of the owner by intentionally starting a fire or causing an explosion.

Simplified explanation

Arson in the fifth degree is intentionally damaging another's property, without the owner's consent, by intentionally starting a fire or causing an explosion; it is a class A misdemeanor.
