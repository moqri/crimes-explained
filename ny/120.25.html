§ 120.25

Reckless endangerment in the first degree

Class D felony Violence & Threats Art. 120 · Assault and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of reckless endangerment in the first degree when, under circumstances evincing a depraved indifference to human life, he engages in conduct which creates a grave risk of death to another person.
