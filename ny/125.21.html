§ 125.21

Aggravated manslaughter in the second degree

Class C felony Violence & Threats Art. 125 · Homicide and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of aggravated manslaughter in the second degree when he or she causes the death of a police officer or peace officer where such officer was in the course of performing his or her official duties and the defendant knew or reasonably should have known that such victim was a police officer or peace officer.
