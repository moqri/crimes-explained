§ 121.13-A

Aggravated strangulation

Class C felony Violence & Threats Art. 121 · Strangulation and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of aggravated strangulation when, being a police officer as defined in subdivision thirty-four of section 1.20 of the criminal procedure law or a peace officer as defined in section 2.10 of the criminal procedure law, he or she commits the crime of criminal obstruction of breathing or blood circulation, as defined in section 121.11 of this article, or uses a chokehold or similar restraint, as described in paragraph b of subdivision one of section eight hundred thirty-seven-t of the executive law, and thereby causes or death to another person.

Simplified explanation

Aggravated strangulation is a police or peace officer committing criminal obstruction of breathing or blood circulation, or using a chokehold or similar restraint, and thereby causing serious physical injury or death; it is a class C felony.
