§ 120.05

Assault in the second degree

Class D felony Violence & Threats Art. 120 · Assault and Related Offenses

Simplified explanation

Assault in the second degree covers many listed assaults, including intentionally causing serious injury, injury with a weapon, injury to protected workers, children and the elderly; it is a class D felony.
