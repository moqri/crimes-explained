§ 135.61

Coercion in the second degree

Class E felony Sex Crimes & Exploitation Art. 135 · Kidnapping, Coercion and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of coercion in the second degree when he or she commits the crime of coercion in the third degree as defined in section 135.60 of this article and thereby compels or induces a person to engage in vaginal sexual contact, oral sexual contact or anal sexual contact as such terms are defined in section 130.00 of this title.

Simplified explanation

Coercion in the second degree is committing coercion in the third degree and thereby compelling or inducing a person to engage in vaginal, oral or anal sexual contact; it is a class E felony.
