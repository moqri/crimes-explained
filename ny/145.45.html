§ 145.45

Tampering with a consumer product in the first degree

Class E felony Violence & Threats Art. 145 · Criminal Mischief and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of tampering with a consumer product in the first degree when, having no right to do so nor any reasonable ground to believe that he has such right, and with intent to cause to another or with intent to instill in another a fear that he will cause such , he alters, adulterates or otherwise contaminates a consumer product and thereby creates a substantial risk of to one or more persons.

1 crime in this section

  1. Alters, adulterates or otherwise contaminates a consumer product and thereby creates a substantial risk of serious physical injury to one or more persons

    • Alters · Adulterates · Otherwise contaminates

    Penalty
    • Class E felony (sentence set in Penal Law Article 70)

Simplified explanation

Tampering with a consumer product in the first degree is contaminating a consumer product to injure or frighten and thereby creating a substantial risk of serious physical injury; it is a class E felony.
