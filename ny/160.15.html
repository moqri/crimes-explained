§ 160.15

Robbery in the first degree

Class B felony Violence & Threats Art. 160 · Robbery

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of robbery in the first degree when he forcibly steals property and when, in the course of the commission of the crime or of immediate flight therefrom, he or another participant in the crime:

1. Causes to any person who is not a participant in the crime; or

2. Is armed with a ; or

3. Uses or threatens the immediate use of a ; or

4. Displays what appears to be a pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, machine gun or other firearm; except that in any prosecution under this subdivision, it is an affirmative defense that such pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, machine gun or other firearm was not a loaded weapon from which a shot, readily capable of producing death or other , could be discharged. Nothing contained in this subdivision shall constitute a defense to a prosecution for, or preclude a conviction of, robbery in the second degree, robbery in the third degree or any other crime.

4 crimes in this section

  1. 1 Forcibly steals property and causes serious physical injury to any person who is not a participant in the crime

    Conditions
    • In the course of the commission of the crime or of immediate flight therefrom, he or another participant in the crime does so

  2. 2 Forcibly steals property and is armed with a deadly weapon

    Conditions
    • In the course of the commission of the crime or of immediate flight therefrom, he or another participant in the crime is armed

  3. 3 Forcibly steals property and uses or threatens the immediate use of a dangerous instrument

    Ways to commit
    • Uses · Threatens the immediate use
    Conditions
    • In the course of the commission of the crime or of immediate flight therefrom, he or another participant in the crime does so

  4. 4 Forcibly steals property and displays what appears to be a pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, machine gun or other firearm

    Conditions
    • In the course of the commission of the crime or of immediate flight therefrom, he or another participant in the crime does so
    Defenses
    • Affirmative defense: the pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, machine gun or other firearm was not a loaded weapon from which a shot, readily capable of producing death or other serious physical injury, could be discharged (this is no defense to a prosecution for, or conviction of, robbery in the second degree, robbery in the third degree or any other crime)
Applies to all 4 crimes above
Penalty
  • Class B felony (sentence set in Penal Law Article 70)

Simplified explanation

Robbery in the first degree is forcibly stealing property while seriously injuring a non-participant, being armed with a deadly weapon, using or threatening a dangerous instrument, or displaying an apparent firearm; it is a class B felony.
