§ 135.20

Kidnapping in the second degree

Class B felony Violence & Threats Art. 135 · Kidnapping, Coercion and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of kidnapping in the second degree when he abducts another person.
