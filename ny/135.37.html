§ 135.37

Aggravated labor trafficking

Class C felony Human Trafficking & Slavery Art. 135 · Kidnapping, Coercion and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of aggravated labor trafficking if he or she compels or induces another to engage in labor or recruits, entices, harbors, or transports such other person to engage in labor by means of intentionally unlawfully providing a controlled substance to such person with intent to impair said person's judgment.

Simplified explanation

Aggravated labor trafficking is compelling or inducing labor, or recruiting or transporting a person for labor, by unlawfully providing a controlled substance to impair judgment; it is a class C felony.
