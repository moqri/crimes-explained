§ 145.25

Reckless endangerment of property

Class B misdemeanor Property Crimes Art. 145 · Criminal Mischief and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of reckless endangerment of property when he engages in conduct which creates a substantial risk of damage to the property of another person in an amount exceeding two hundred fifty dollars.
