§ 140.30

Burglary in the first degree

Class B felony Property Crimes Art. 140 · Burglary and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of burglary in the first degree when he knowingly enters or remains unlawfully in a dwelling with intent to commit a crime therein, and when, in effecting entry or while in the dwelling or in immediate flight therefrom, he or another participant in the crime:

1. Is armed with explosives or a ; or

2. Causes to any person who is not a participant in the crime; or

3. Uses or threatens the immediate use of a ; or

4. Displays what appears to be a pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, machine gun or other firearm; except that in any prosecution under this subdivision, it is an affirmative defense that such pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, machine gun or other firearm was not a loaded weapon from which a shot, readily capable of producing death or other , could be discharged. Nothing contained in this subdivision shall constitute a defense to a prosecution for, or preclude a conviction of, burglary in the second degree, burglary in the third degree or any other crime.

4 crimes in this section

  1. 1 Enters or remains unlawfully in a dwelling

    Conditions
    • In effecting entry or while in the dwelling or in immediate flight therefrom, the person or another participant in the crime is armed with explosives or a deadly weapon

  2. 2 Enters or remains unlawfully in a dwelling

    Conditions
    • In effecting entry or while in the dwelling or in immediate flight therefrom, the person or another participant in the crime causes physical injury to any person who is not a participant in the crime

  3. 3 Enters or remains unlawfully in a dwelling

    Conditions
    • In effecting entry or while in the dwelling or in immediate flight therefrom, the person or another participant in the crime uses or threatens the immediate use of a dangerous instrument

  4. 4 Enters or remains unlawfully in a dwelling

    Conditions
    • In effecting entry or while in the dwelling or in immediate flight therefrom, the person or another participant in the crime displays what appears to be a pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, machine gun or other firearm
    Defenses
    • Affirmative defense: such pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, machine gun or other firearm was not a loaded weapon from which a shot, readily capable of producing death or other serious physical injury, could be discharged (this does not bar a prosecution for or conviction of burglary in the second degree, burglary in the third degree or any other crime)
Applies to all 4 crimes above
Mental state
  • Knowingly
  • With intent to commit a crime therein
Penalty
  • Class B felony (sentence set in Penal Law Article 70)

Simplified explanation

Burglary in the first degree is burglary of a dwelling while armed, injuring a non-participant, using or threatening a dangerous instrument, or displaying an apparent firearm; it is a class B felony.
