§ 165.20

Fraudulently obtaining a signature

Class A misdemeanor Fraud & Money Art. 165 · Other Offenses Relating to Theft

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of fraudulently obtaining a signature when, with intent to defraud or injure another or to acquire a substantial benefit for himself or a third person, he obtains the signature of a person to a written instrument by means of any misrepresentation of fact which he knows to be false.

Simplified explanation

Fraudulently obtaining a signature is getting someone to sign a written instrument through a known false misrepresentation, intending to defraud or injure or to gain a substantial benefit; it is a class A misdemeanor.
