Art. VII, § 3

Budget bills; appearances before legislature

Duty Taxes, Money & Commerce Art. VII · State Finances

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

At the time of submitting the budget to the legislature the governor shall submit a bill or bills containing all the proposed appropriations and reappropriations included in the budget and the proposed legislation, if any, recommended therein.

The governor may at any time within thirty days thereafter and, with the consent of the legislature, at any time before the adjournment thereof, amend or supplement the budget and submit amendments to any bills submitted by him or her or submit supplemental bills.

The governor and the heads of departments shall have the right, and it shall be the duty of the heads of departments when requested by either house of the legislature or an appropriate committee thereof, to appear and be heard in respect to the budget during the consideration thereof, and to answer inquiries relevant thereto. The procedure for such appearances and inquiries shall be provided by law.

Simplified explanation

With the budget, the governor must submit bills containing all proposed appropriations and recommended legislation, and may amend or supplement it within thirty days, or later with the legislature's consent. The governor and department heads may appear and be heard on the budget, and department heads must when requested.
