Art. IV, § 8

Departmental rules and regulations; filing; publication

Limit The Governor & Executive Art. IV · Executive

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

No rule or regulation made by any state department, board, bureau, officer, authority or commission, except such as relates to the organization or internal management of a state department, board, bureau, authority or commission shall be effective until it is filed in the office of the department of state. The legislature shall provide for the speedy publication of such rules and regulations by appropriate laws.

Simplified explanation

A rule or regulation of a state department, board, bureau, officer, authority or commission, other than one on organization or internal management, is not effective until filed in the office of the department of state. The legislature must provide for its speedy publication.
