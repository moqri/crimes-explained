Art. XVI, § 5

Compensation of public officers and employees subject to taxation

Structure Taxes, Money & Commerce Art. XVI · Taxation

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

All salaries, wages and other compensation, except pensions, paid to officers and employees of the state and its subdivisions and agencies shall be subject to taxation.
