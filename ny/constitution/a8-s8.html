Art. VIII, § 8

Indebtedness not to be invalidated by operation of this article

Limit Local Government Art. VIII · Local Finances

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

No indebtedness of a county, city, town, village or school district valid at the time of its inception shall thereafter become invalid by reason of the operation of any of the provisions of this article.

1 provision here

  1. No indebtedness of a county, city, town, village or school district valid at the time of its inception shall thereafter become invalid by reason of the operation of any of the provisions of this article limit

Simplified explanation

Debt of a county, city, town, village or school district that was valid when it began does not later become invalid because of any provision of this article.
