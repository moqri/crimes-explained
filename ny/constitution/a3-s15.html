Art. III, § 15

Private or local bills to embrace only one subject to be expressed in title

Limit The Legislature Art. III · Legislature

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

No private or local bill, which may be passed by the legislature, shall embrace more than one subject, and that shall be expressed in the title.

Simplified explanation

A private or local bill passed by the legislature may not embrace more than one subject, and that subject must be expressed in the title.
