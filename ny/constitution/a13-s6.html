Art. XIII, § 6

When office to be deemed vacant; legislature may declare

Power Public Officers & Civil Service Art. XIII · Public Officers

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The legislature may declare the cases in which any office shall be deemed vacant when no provision is made for that purpose in this constitution.

Simplified explanation

The legislature may declare when any office shall be deemed vacant, where this constitution makes no provision for that purpose.
