Art. I, § 6

Grand jury; waiver of indictment; right to counsel; informing accused; double jeopardy; self-incrimination; waiver of immunity by public officers; due process of law

Simplified explanation

No person shall answer for a capital or otherwise infamous crime without grand jury indictment, except listed cases or a written waiver. The accused may defend with counsel, be informed of the accusation and confront witnesses. Double jeopardy and compelled self-incrimination are barred, officers refusing to waive immunity are disqualified, and due process is guaranteed.
