Art. VI, § 24

Court for the trial of impeachments

The assembly shall have the power of by a vote of a majority of all the members elected thereto. The court for the trial of impeachments shall be composed of the president of the senate, the senators, or the major part of them, and the judges of the court of appeals, or the major part of them. On the trial of an against the governor or lieutenant-governor, neither the lieutenant-governor nor the temporary president of the senate shall act as a member of the court. No judicial officer shall exercise his or her office after articles of against him or her shall have been preferred to the senate, until he or she shall have been acquitted. Simplified explanation

The assembly may impeach by a majority of all members elected. The senate president, senators and court of appeals judges try impeachments; conviction needs two-thirds of members present. Judgment extends only to removal and disqualification, but the party impeached remains liable to indictment and punishment.
