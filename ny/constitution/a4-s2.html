Art. IV, § 2

Qualifications of governor and lieutenant-governor

Structure The Governor & Executive Art. IV · Executive

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

No person shall be eligible to the office of governor or lieutenant-governor, except a citizen of the United States, of the age of not less than thirty years, and who shall have been five years next preceding the election a resident of this state.

Simplified explanation

Only a citizen of the United States, at least thirty years old, who has been a resident of New York for the five years before the election may be governor or lieutenant-governor.
