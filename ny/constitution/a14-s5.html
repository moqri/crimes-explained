Art. XIV, § 5

Violations of article; how restrained

Right Conservation & Natural Resources Art. XIV · Conservation

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

A violation of any of the provisions of this article may be restrained at the suit of the people or, with the consent of the supreme court in appellate division, on notice to the attorney-general at the suit of any citizen.

Simplified explanation

A violation of this article may be restrained at the suit of the people or, with the consent of the supreme court in appellate division and on notice to the attorney-general, at the suit of any citizen.
