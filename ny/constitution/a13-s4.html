Art. XIII, § 4

Political year and legislative term

Structure The Legislature Art. XIII · Public Officers

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The political year and legislative term shall begin on the first day of January; and the legislature shall, every year, assemble on the first Wednesday after the first Monday in January.

Simplified explanation

The political year and legislative term begin on January 1, and the legislature must assemble every year on the first Wednesday after the first Monday in January.
