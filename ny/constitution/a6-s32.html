Art. VI, § 32

Safeguarding religion of children under jurisdiction of court

Duty The Courts Art. VI · Judiciary

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

When any court having jurisdiction over a child shall commit it or remand it to an institution or agency or place it in the custody of any person by parole, placing out, adoption or guardianship, the child shall be committed or remanded or placed, when practicable, in an institution or agency governed by persons, or in the custody of a person, of the same religious persuasion as the child.

Simplified explanation

When a court commits, remands or places a child in its jurisdiction with an institution, agency or person, the child must be placed, when practicable, with an institution or agency governed by persons, or a person, of the same religious persuasion as the child.
