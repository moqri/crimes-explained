Art. III, § 8

Time of elections of members

Structure The Legislature Art. III · Legislature

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

The elections of senators and members of assembly, pursuant to the provisions of this constitution, shall be held on the Tuesday succeeding the first Monday of November, unless otherwise directed by the legislature.

    Exceptions

Simplified explanation

Elections of senators and assembly members are held on the Tuesday after the first Monday of November, unless the legislature directs otherwise.
