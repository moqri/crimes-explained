§ 121.12

Strangulation in the second degree

Class D felony Violence & Threats Art. 121 · Strangulation and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of strangulation in the second degree when he or she commits the crime of criminal obstruction of breathing or blood circulation, as defined in section 121.11 of this article, and thereby causes stupor, loss of consciousness for any period of time, or any other or impairment.

Simplified explanation

Strangulation in the second degree is criminal obstruction of breathing or blood circulation that causes stupor, loss of consciousness, or other physical injury or impairment; it is a class D felony.
