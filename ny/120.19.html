§ 120.19

Assault on a retail worker

Class E felony Violence & Threats Art. 120 · Assault and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

1. A person is guilty of assault on a retail worker when, with the intent to prevent a retail worker from performing an act within the scope of such worker's employment, such person causes to such retail worker and the person knew or reasonably should have known that such individual was a retail worker.

2. For the purposes of this section:

a. "Retail worker" shall mean any person whose usual place of work is a retail establishment. This shall include, but is not limited to, an employee of the retail establishment, an owner of the retail establishment, or a person who works in the retail establishment under arrangements made between the person and the establishment.

b. "Retail establishment" shall mean any physical business or commercial entity engaged in the sale of goods, merchandise, or services directly to consumers.
