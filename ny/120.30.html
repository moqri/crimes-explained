§ 120.30

Promoting a suicide attempt

Class E felony Violence & Threats Art. 120 · Assault and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of promoting a suicide attempt when he intentionally causes or aids another person to attempt suicide.
