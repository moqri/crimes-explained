§ 120.06

Gang assault in the second degree

Class C felony Violence & Threats Art. 120 · Assault and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of gang assault in the second degree when, with intent to cause to another person and when aided by two or more other persons actually present, he causes to such person or to a third person.

Simplified explanation

Gang assault in the second degree covers causing serious physical injury with intent to cause physical injury while aided by two or more other persons present; it is a class C felony.
