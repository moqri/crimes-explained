§ 155.43

Aggravated grand larceny of an automated teller machine

Class C felony Property Crimes Art. 155 · Larceny

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of aggravated grand larceny of an automated teller machine when he or she commits the crime of grand larceny in the third degree, as defined in subdivision two of section 155.35 of this article and has been previously convicted of grand larceny in the third degree within the previous five years.

Simplified explanation

Aggravated grand larceny of an automated teller machine is stealing an ATM or its contents after a grand larceny third-degree conviction within the previous five years; it is a class C felony.
