§ 120.04

Vehicular assault in the first degree

Class D felony Transportation & Aviation Art. 120 · Assault and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of vehicular assault in the first degree when he or she commits the crime of vehicular assault in the second degree as defined in section 120.03 of this article, and either:

(1) commits such crime while operating a motor vehicle while such person has .18 of one per centum or more by weight of alcohol in such person's blood as shown by chemical analysis of such person's blood, breath, urine or saliva made pursuant to the provisions of section eleven hundred ninety-four of the vehicle and traffic law;

(2) commits such crime while knowing or having reason to know that: (a) his or her license or his or her privilege of operating a motor vehicle in another state or his or her privilege of obtaining a license to operate a motor vehicle in another state is suspended or revoked and such suspension or revocation is based upon a conviction in such other state for an offense which would, if committed in this state, constitute a of any of the provisions of section eleven hundred ninety-two of the vehicle and traffic law; or (b) his or her license or his or her privilege of operating a motor vehicle in the state or his or her privilege of obtaining a license issued by the commissioner of motor vehicles is suspended or revoked and such suspension or revocation is based upon either a refusal to submit to a chemical test pursuant to section eleven hundred ninety-four of the vehicle and traffic law or following a conviction for a of any of the provisions of section eleven hundred ninety-two of the vehicle and traffic law;

(3) has previously been convicted of violating any of the provisions of section eleven hundred ninety-two of the vehicle and traffic law within the preceding ten years, provided that, for the purposes of this subdivision, a conviction in any other state or jurisdiction for an offense which, if committed in this state, would constitute a of section eleven hundred ninety-two of the vehicle and traffic law, shall be treated as a of such law;

(4) causes to more than one other person;

(5) has previously been convicted of violating any provision of this article or article one hundred twenty-five of this title involving the operation of a motor vehicle, or was convicted in any other state or jurisdiction of an offense involving the operation of a motor vehicle which, if committed in this state, would constitute a of this article or article one hundred twenty-five of this title;

(6) commits such crime while operating a motor vehicle while a child who is fifteen years of age or less is a passenger in such motor vehicle and causes to such child; or

(7) commits such crime while operating a vessel in of paragraph (e-1) of subdivision two of section forty-nine-a of the navigation law while a child who is fifteen years of age or less is a passenger in such vessel and causes to such child.

If it is established that the person operating such motor vehicle caused such or injuries while unlawfully intoxicated or impaired by the use of alcohol or a drug, or by the combined influence of drugs or of alcohol and any drug or drugs, then there shall be a rebuttable presumption that, as a result of such intoxication or impairment by the use of alcohol or a drug, or by the combined influence of drugs or of alcohol and any drug or drugs, such person operated the motor vehicle in a manner that caused such or injuries, as required by this section and section 120.03 of this article.

7 crimes in this section

  1. 1 Commits the crime of vehicular assault in the second degree as defined in section 120.03 of this article

    Conditions
    • Commits such crime while operating a motor vehicle while the person has .18 of one per centum or more by weight of alcohol in the blood as shown by chemical analysis of blood, breath, urine or saliva made pursuant to section eleven hundred ninety-four of the vehicle and traffic law
    • Rebuttable presumption: if the person operating the motor vehicle caused the serious physical injury or injuries while unlawfully intoxicated or impaired by the use of alcohol or a drug, or by the combined influence of drugs or of alcohol and any drug or drugs, it is presumed that the person operated it in a manner that caused the injury or injuries as a result of the intoxication or impairment

  2. 2 Commits the crime of vehicular assault in the second degree as defined in section 120.03 of this article

    Knowledge
    • Knowing or having reason to know that either: (a) his or her license or privilege of operating a motor vehicle in another state, or privilege of obtaining a license there, is suspended or revoked based upon a conviction in such other state for an offense which would, if committed in this state, constitute a violation of section eleven hundred ninety-two of the vehicle and traffic law; or (b) his or her license or privilege of operating a motor vehicle in this state, or privilege of obtaining a license issued by the commissioner of motor vehicles, is suspended or revoked based upon a refusal to submit to a chemical test pursuant to section eleven hundred ninety-four of the vehicle and traffic law or following a conviction for a violation of section eleven hundred ninety-two of the vehicle and traffic law
    Conditions
    • Rebuttable presumption: if the person operating the motor vehicle caused the serious physical injury or injuries while unlawfully intoxicated or impaired by the use of alcohol or a drug, or by the combined influence of drugs or of alcohol and any drug or drugs, it is presumed that the person operated it in a manner that caused the injury or injuries as a result of the intoxication or impairment

    • Rebuttable presumption: if the person operating the motor vehicle caused the serious physical injury or injuries while unlawfully intoxicated or impaired by the use of alcohol or a drug, or by the combined influence of drugs or of alcohol and any drug or drugs, it is presumed that the person operated it in a manner that caused the injury or injuries as a result of the intoxication or impairment

  4. 4 Commits the crime of vehicular assault in the second degree as defined in section 120.03 of this article

    • Rebuttable presumption: if the person operating the motor vehicle caused the serious physical injury or injuries while unlawfully intoxicated or impaired by the use of alcohol or a drug, or by the combined influence of drugs or of alcohol and any drug or drugs, it is presumed that the person operated it in a manner that caused the injury or injuries as a result of the intoxication or impairment

Applies to all 7 crimes above
Penalty
  • Class D felony (sentence set in Penal Law Article 70)

Simplified explanation

Vehicular assault in the first degree covers committing vehicular assault in the second degree with an aggravating factor such as .18 blood alcohol, a suspended license, a prior conviction, multiple victims or a child passenger; it is a class D felony.
