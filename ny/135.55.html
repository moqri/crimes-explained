§ 135.55

Substitution of children

Class E felony Family & Children Art. 135 · Kidnapping, Coercion and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of substitution of children when, having been temporarily entrusted with a child less than one year old and intending to deceive a parent, guardian or other lawful custodian of such child, he substitutes, produces or returns to such parent, guardian or custodian a child other than the one entrusted.

Simplified explanation

Substitution of children is, having been entrusted with a child under one year old, substituting, producing or returning a different child to the parent or custodian intending to deceive; it is a class E felony.
