§ 120.10

Assault in the first degree

Class B felony Violence & Threats Art. 120 · Assault and Related Offenses

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person is guilty of assault in the first degree when:

1. With intent to cause to another person, he causes such injury to such person or to a third person by means of a or a ; or

2. With intent to disfigure another person seriously and permanently, or to destroy, amputate or disable permanently a member or organ of his body, he causes such injury to such person or to a third person; or

3. Under circumstances evincing a depraved indifference to human life, he engages in conduct which creates a grave risk of death to another person, and thereby causes to another person; or

4. In the course of and in furtherance of the commission or attempted commission of a or of immediate flight therefrom, he, or another participant if there be any, causes to a person other than one of the participants.

4 crimes in this section

  1. 1 Causes serious physical injury to another person or to a third person by means of a deadly weapon or a dangerous instrument

    Mental state
    • With intent to cause serious physical injury to another person

  2. 2 Causes serious and permanent disfigurement, or permanent destruction, amputation or disabling of a member or organ of the body, of another person or a third person

    Mental state
    • With intent to disfigure another person seriously and permanently, or to destroy, amputate or disable permanently a member or organ of his body

  3. 3 Engages in conduct which creates a grave risk of death to another person, and thereby causes serious physical injury to another person

    Mental state
    • Recklessly
    • Under circumstances evincing a depraved indifference to human life

  4. 4 Causes serious physical injury to a person other than one of the participants, in the course of and in furtherance of the commission or attempted commission of a felony or of immediate flight therefrom

Applies to all 4 crimes above
Penalty
  • Class B felony (sentence set in Penal Law Article 70)

Simplified explanation

Assault in the first degree covers intentionally causing serious injury with a weapon, intentional disfigurement or disabling, depraved-indifference recklessness, and injury during a felony; it is a class B felony.
