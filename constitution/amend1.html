Amend. I

Freedom of religion, speech, press, assembly and petition

Limit Individual Rights Amend. I–X · Bill of Rights

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion, or prohibiting the free exercise thereof; or abridging the freedom of speech, or of the press; of the right of the people peaceably to assemble, and to petition the Government for a redress of grievances.

Proposal and ratification: The first ten amendments were proposed to the legislatures of the several States by the First Congress on September 25, 1789. was completed on December 15, 1791.
