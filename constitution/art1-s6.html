Art. I, § 6

Pay, privileges and limits on members

Right Congress Art. I · Congress

Official text rightpowerlimitif conditionlegal term

1 The Senators and Representatives shall receive a Compensation for their Services, to be ascertained by Law, and paid out of the Treasury of the United States. They shall in all Cases, except Treason, Felony and Breach of the Peace, be privileged from Arrest during their Attendance at the Session of their respective Houses, and in going to and returning from the same; and for any Speech or Debate in either House, they shall not be questioned in any other Place.

2 No Senator or Representative shall, during the Time for which he was elected, be appointed to any civil Office under the Authority of the United States, which shall have been created, or the whereof shall have been encreased during such time; and no Person holding any Office under the United States, shall be a Member of either House during his Continuance in Office.

Note (Government Publishing Office): This clause has been affected by amendment XXVII.

Simplified explanation

Senators and Representatives receive pay set by law. Except for treason, felony and breach of the peace, they are privileged from arrest while attending sessions, and cannot be questioned elsewhere for speech or debate. They may not hold certain other federal offices.
