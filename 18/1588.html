§ 1588

Transportation of slaves from United States

Up to 10 years Human Trafficking & Slavery None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 77 · Peonage, Slavery, and Trafficking in Persons

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being the master or owner or person having charge of any vessel, receives on board any other person with the knowledge or intent that such person is to be carried from any place within the United States to any other place to be held or sold as a slave, or carries away from any place within the United States any such person with the intent that he may be so held or sold as a slave, shall be or imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 773 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB); Pub. L. 104–208, div. C, title II, §218(a), Sept. 30, 1996, 110 Stat. 3009–573.)

2 crimes in this section

  1. Receives on board any other person

    Knowledge
    • With the knowledge or intent that such person is to be carried from any place within the United States to any other place to be held or sold as a slave

  2. Carries away from any place within the United States any such person

    Intent
    • With the intent that he may be so held or sold as a slave
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Who
  • The master or owner or person having charge of any vessel
Federal basis
  • The person is to be carried, or is carried away, from a place within the United States
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both
