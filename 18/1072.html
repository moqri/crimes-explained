§ 1072

Concealing escaped prisoner

Up to 3 years Courts & Justice None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 49 · Fugitives From Justice

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever harbors or conceals any prisoner after his escape from the custody of the Attorney General or from a Federal penal or correctional institution, shall be imprisoned not more than three years.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 755 (PDF, 39 MB).)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to willfully hide or shelter a prisoner who has escaped from federal custody or a federal prison.
