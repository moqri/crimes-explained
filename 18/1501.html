§ 1501

Assault on process server

Up to 1 year Courts & Justice None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 73 · Obstruction of Justice

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever knowingly and obstructs, resists, or opposes any officer of the United States, or other person duly authorized, in serving, or attempting to serve or execute, any legal or judicial writ or process of any court of the United States, or United States magistrate judge; or

Whoever assaults, beats, or wounds any officer or other person duly authorized, knowing him to be such officer, or other person so duly authorized, in serving or executing any such writ, rule, order, process, warrant, or other legal or judicial writ or process—

Shall, except as otherwise provided by law, be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly and willfully block, resist, or oppose a federal officer or other authorized person who is serving court papers or carrying out a court order or warrant. Assaulting or injuring such a person, knowing who they are, is also a crime.
