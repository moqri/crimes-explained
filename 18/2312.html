§ 2312

Transportation of stolen vehicles

Up to 10 years Property Crimes 35 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 113 · Stolen Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever transports in a motor vehicle, vessel, or aircraft, knowing the same to have been stolen, shall be or imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 806 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 102–519, title I, §103, Oct. 25, 1992, 106 Stat. 3385 (PDF, 2.9 MB); Pub. L. 109–177, title III, §307(b)(2)(A), Mar. 9, 2006, 120 Stat. 240.)

1 crime in this section

  1. Transports in interstate or foreign commerce a motor vehicle, vessel, or aircraft

    Federal basis
    • Transports in interstate or foreign commerce
    Mental state
    • Knowing the same to have been stolen
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to move a stolen car, boat, or aircraft across state lines or national borders while knowing it was stolen.
