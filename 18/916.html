§ 916

4–H Club members or agents

Up to 6 months Fraud & Money None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 43 · False Personation

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, falsely and with intent to defraud, holds himself out as or represents or pretends himself to be a member of, associated with, or an agent or representative for the 4–H clubs, an organization established by the Extension Service of the United States Department of Agriculture and the land grant colleges, shall be or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 743 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(F), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

    Ways to commit
    • holds himself out as · represents · pretends
    Federal basis
    • The 4-H clubs are an organization established by the Extension Service of the United States Department of Agriculture and the land grant colleges
    Intent
    • With intent to defraud
    Conditions
    • The representation is false
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 6 months in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to falsely claim to be a member, agent, or representative of the 4-H clubs, or to be connected with them, in order to cheat someone. 4-H is a youth organization set up by the U.S. Department of Agriculture's Extension Service and land-grant colleges.
