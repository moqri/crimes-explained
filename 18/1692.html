§ 1692

Foreign mail as United States mail

See penalty details Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Every foreign mail, while being transported across the territory of the United States under authority of law, is mail of the United States, and any depredation thereon, or offense in respect thereto, shall be punishable as though it were United States mail.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 776 (PDF, 39 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Commits any depredation on, or offense in respect to, foreign mail

    Ways to commit
    • depredation · offense in respect thereto
    Federal basis
    • Foreign mail, while being transported across the territory of the United States under authority of law, is mail of the United States
    Conditions

    Penalty
    • Set in another law: punishable as though it were United States mail

Simplified explanation

Foreign mail being carried across the United States under legal authority counts as U.S. mail. Any theft, damage, or other crime involving it is punished as if it were U.S. mail.
