§ 1349

Attempt and conspiracy

See penalty details Fraud & Money 1,610 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 63 · Mail Fraud and Other Fraud Offenses

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Any person who or to commit any offense under this chapter shall be subject to the same penalties as those prescribed for the offense, the commission of which was the object of the attempt or .

History: (Added Pub. L. 107–204, title IX, §902(a), July 30, 2002, 116 Stat. 805.)

1 crime in this section

  1. Attempts or conspires to commit any offense under this chapter

    Ways to commit

    Federal basis
    • The object is an offense under this chapter
    Penalty
    • The same penalties as those prescribed for the offense that was the object of the attempt or conspiracy
    Also covers

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to attempt or conspire (agree with others) to commit any fraud crime in this chapter, such as mail, wire, bank, or health care fraud.
