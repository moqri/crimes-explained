§ 652

Disbursing officer paying lesser in lieu of lawful amount

Up to 2 years Property Crimes None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 31 · Embezzlement and Theft

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being an officer, clerk, agent, employee, or other person charged with the payment of any appropriation made by Congress, pays to any clerk or other employee of the United States, or of any department or agency thereof, a sum less than that provided by law, and requires such employee to receipt or give a voucher for an amount greater than that actually paid to and received by him, is guilty of embezzlement, and shall be or in double the amount so withheld, whichever is greater, or imprisoned not more than two years, or both; but if the amount embezzled is $1,000 or less, he shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 727 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), (2)(G), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB), 2148; Pub. L. 104–294, title VI, §606(a), Oct. 11, 1996, 110 Stat. 3511.)

1 crime in this section

  1. Pays to any clerk or other employee of the United States, or of any department or agency thereof, a sum less than that provided by law, and requires such employee to receipt or give a voucher for an amount greater than that actually paid to and received by him

    Who
    • An officer, clerk, agent, employee, or other person charged with the payment of any appropriation made by Congress
    Federal basis
    • The payment is of an appropriation made by Congress to a clerk or other employee of the United States or of any department or agency thereof
    Penalty
    • A fine under this title or double the amount so withheld, whichever is greater, up to 2 years in prison, or both
    • If the amount embezzled is $1,000 or less: A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both
