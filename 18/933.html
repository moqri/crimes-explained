§ 933

Trafficking in firearms

Up to 15 years Weapons & Terrorism 174 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 44 · Firearms

(a) In General. It shall be unlawful for any person to—

(1) ship, transport, transfer, cause to be transported, or otherwise dispose of any firearm to another person in or otherwise affecting , if such person knows or that the use, carrying, or possession of a firearm by the recipient would constitute a (as defined in section 932(a));

(2) receive from another person any firearm in or otherwise affecting , if the recipient knows or that such receipt would constitute a ; or

(3) attempt or conspire to commit the conduct described in paragraph (1) or (2).

(b) Penalty. Any person who violates subsection (a) shall be , imprisoned for not more than 15 years, or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 117–159, div. A, title II, §12004(a)(1), June 25, 2022, 136 Stat. 1327.)
