§ 917

Red Cross members or agents

Up to 5 years Fraud & Money Ch. 43 · False Personation

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, within the United States, falsely or fraudulently holds himself out as or represents or pretends himself to be a member of or an agent for the American National Red Cross for the purpose of soliciting, collecting, or receiving money or material, shall be or imprisoned not more than 5 years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 743 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(G), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB); Pub. L. 107–56, title X, §1011(c), Oct. 26, 2001, 115 Stat. 396.)
