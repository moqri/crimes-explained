§ 371

Conspiracy to commit offense or to defraud United States

Up to 5 years Government & Corruption 1,778 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 19 · Conspiracy

If two or more persons conspire either to commit any offense against the United States, or to defraud the United States, or any agency thereof in any manner or for any purpose, and one or more of such persons do any act to effect the object of the , each shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

If, however, the offense, the commission of which is the object of the , is a only, the punishment for such shall not exceed the maximum punishment provided for such .

1 crime in this section

  1. Conspires either to commit any offense against the United States, or to defraud the United States, or any agency thereof in any manner or for any purpose

    Ways to commit
    • to commit any offense against the United States · to defraud the United States, or any agency thereof
    Federal basis
    • The object is an offense against the United States, or to defraud the United States or any agency thereof
    Conditions
    • Two or more persons conspire
    • One or more of such persons do any act to effect the object of the conspiracy
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both
    • If the offense that is the object of the conspiracy is a misdemeanor only: Not to exceed the maximum punishment provided for such misdemeanor

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for two or more people to agree (conspire) to commit any federal crime, or to cheat or defraud the United States or any federal agency in any way. At least one of the people must take some action to carry out the plan.
