§ 1427

Sale of naturalization or citizenship papers

Up to 25 years Immigration & Citizenship None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 69 · Nationality and Citizenship

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever unlawfully sells or disposes of a declaration of intention to become a citizen, certificate of naturalization, certificate of citizenship or copies or duplicates or other documentary evidence of naturalization or citizenship, shall be or imprisoned not more than 25 years (if the offense was committed to facilitate an act of international terrorism (as defined in section 2331 of this title)), 20 years (if the offense was committed to facilitate a drug trafficking crime (as defined in section 929(a) of this title)), 10 years (in the case of the first or second such offense, if the offense was not committed to facilitate such an act of international terrorism or a drug trafficking crime), or 15 years (in the case of any other offense), or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Sells or disposes of a declaration of intention to become a citizen, certificate of naturalization, certificate of citizenship or copies or duplicates or other documentary evidence of naturalization or citizenship

    Ways to commit
    • sells · disposes of
    Federal basis
    • Naturalization or citizenship documents
    Conditions
    • The sale or disposal is unlawful
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both (first or second such offense, not committed to facilitate international terrorism or a drug trafficking crime)
    • If the offense is not a first or second such offense: A fine, up to 15 years in prison, or both
    • If the offense was committed to facilitate a drug trafficking crime (as defined in section 929(a)): A fine, up to 20 years in prison, or both
    • If the offense was committed to facilitate an act of international terrorism (as defined in section 2331): A fine, up to 25 years in prison, or both
    Key terms
    • "International terrorism": as defined in section 2331 (violent acts or acts dangerous to human life violating U.S. or State criminal laws, appearing intended to intimidate or coerce a civilian population or influence or affect a government, occurring primarily outside the U.S. or transcending national boundaries)
    • "Drug trafficking crime": as defined in section 929(a) (any felony punishable under the Controlled Substances Act, the Controlled Substances Import and Export Act, or chapter 705 of title 46)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to unlawfully sell or give away naturalization certificates, citizenship certificates, declarations of intention to become a citizen, or copies of them.
