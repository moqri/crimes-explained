§ 1306

Participation by financial institutions

Up to 1 year Gambling & Obscenity None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 61 · Lotteries

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever knowingly violates section 5136A [1] of the Revised Statutes of the United States, section 9A of the Federal Reserve Act, or section 20 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 90–203, §5(a), Dec. 15, 1967, 81 Stat. 611; amended Pub. L. 101–73, title IX, §962(b), Aug. 9, 1989, 103 Stat. 502 (PDF, 56 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 See References in Text note below.

    • Violation of section 5136A of the Revised Statutes of the United States, section 9A of the Federal Reserve Act, or section 20 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act
    Knowledge
    • Knowingly
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly break certain federal banking laws named in this section (in the Revised Statutes, the Federal Reserve Act, and the Federal Deposit Insurance Act).
