§ 1114

Protection of officers and employees of the United States

See penalty details Violence & Threats 14 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 51 · Homicide

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) In General. Whoever kills or to kill any officer or employee of the United States or of any agency in any branch of the United States Government (including any member of the uniformed services) while such officer or employee is engaged in or on account of the performance of official duties, or any person assisting such an officer or employee in the performance of such duties or on account of that assistance, shall be punished—

(1) in the case of murder, as provided under section 1111;

(2) in the case of manslaughter, as provided under section 1112; or

(3) in the case of attempted murder or manslaughter, as provided in section 1113.

(b) Extraterritorial Jurisdiction. There is extraterritorial jurisdiction over the conduct prohibited by this section.

3 crimes in this section

  1. (a)(1) Kills any officer or employee of the United States or of any agency in any branch of the United States Government (including any member of the uniformed services), or any person assisting such an officer or employee in the performance of such duties (murder)

    Conditions
    • The officer or employee is engaged in or on account of the performance of official duties, or the person is assisting or killed on account of that assistance
    Penalty
    • Set in section 1111(b)

  2. (a)(2) Kills any officer or employee of the United States or of any agency in any branch of the United States Government (including any member of the uniformed services), or any person assisting such an officer or employee in the performance of such duties (manslaughter)

    Conditions
    • The officer or employee is engaged in or on account of the performance of official duties, or the person is assisting or killed on account of that assistance
    Penalty
    • Set in section 1112(b)

  3. (a)(3) Attempts to kill any officer or employee of the United States or of any agency in any branch of the United States Government (including any member of the uniformed services), or any person assisting such an officer or employee in the performance of such duties

    Conditions
    • The officer or employee is engaged in or on account of the performance of official duties, or the person is assisting or targeted on account of that assistance
    Penalty
    • Set in section 1113
Applies to all 3 crimes above
Federal basis
  • The victim is an officer or employee of the United States or of any agency in any branch of the United States Government (including any member of the uniformed services), or a person assisting such an officer or employee
  • There is extraterritorial jurisdiction over the conduct prohibited by this section

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to kill or try to kill any U.S. government officer or employee, including members of the military, because of or during their official duties. It also protects people helping those officers.
