§ 1460

Possession with intent to sell, and sale, of obscene matter on Federal property

Up to 2 years Gambling & Obscenity None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 71 · Obscenity

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever, either—

(1) in the , or on any land or building owned by, leased to, or otherwise used by or under the control of the Government of the United States; or

(2) in the Indian country as defined in section 1151 of this title,

knowingly sells or possesses with intent to sell an obscene visual depiction shall be punished by a fine in accordance with the provisions of this title or imprisoned for not more than 2 years, or both.

(b)

For the purposes of this section, the term "visual depiction" includes undeveloped film and videotape but does not include mere words.

History: (Added Pub. L. 100–690, title VII, §7526(a), Nov. 18, 1988, 102 Stat. 4503 (PDF, 56 MB); amended Pub. L. 101–647, title III, §323(c), Nov. 29, 1990, 104 Stat. 4819 (PDF, 27 MB).)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly sell, or possess with intent to sell, an obscene picture, film, or video on federal property, in special federal areas, or in Indian country.
