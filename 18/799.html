§ 799

Violation of regulations of National Aeronautics and Space Administration

Up to 1 year Espionage & National Security None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 37 · Espionage and Censorship

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever shall violate, attempt to violate, or conspire to violate any regulation or order promulgated by the Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration for the protection or security of any laboratory, station, base or other facility, or part thereof, or any aircraft, missile, spacecraft, or similar vehicle, or part thereof, or other property or equipment in the custody of the Administration, or any real or personal property or equipment in the custody of any contractor under any contract with the Administration or any subcontractor of any such contractor, shall be , or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 85–568, title III, §304(c)(1), July 29, 1958, 72 Stat. 434 (PDF, 2.5 MB); amended Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to willfully violate NASA regulations or orders meant to protect the security of its facilities, aircraft, spacecraft, and other property. This includes property held by NASA contractors and subcontractors.
