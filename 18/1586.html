§ 1586

Service on vessels in slave trade

Up to 2 years Human Trafficking & Slavery None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 77 · Peonage, Slavery, and Trafficking in Persons

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being a citizen or resident of the United States, voluntarily serves on board of any vessel employed or made use of in the transportation of slaves from any foreign country or place to another, shall be or imprisoned not more than two years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 773 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(I), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Serves on board of any vessel employed or made use of in the transportation of slaves from any foreign country or place to another

    Who
    • A citizen or resident of the United States
    Federal basis
    • The offender is a citizen or resident of the United States
    Intent
    • Voluntarily
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 2 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a U.S. citizen or resident to voluntarily serve on a ship used to carry slaves from one foreign place to another.
