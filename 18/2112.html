§ 2112

Personal property of United States

Up to 15 years Property Crimes None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 103 · Robbery and Burglary

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever robs or to rob another of any kind or description of personal property belonging to the United States, shall be imprisoned not more than fifteen years.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 796 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXII, §320903(a)(2), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2124 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Robs another of any kind or description of personal property belonging to the United States

    Federal basis
    • The personal property belongs to the United States
    Penalty

    Also covers

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to rob or try to rob someone of personal property that belongs to the United States government.
