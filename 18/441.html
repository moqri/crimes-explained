§ 441

Postal supply contracts

Up to 1 year Government & Corruption None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 23 · Contracts

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

No contract for furnishing supplies to the Postal Service shall be made with any person who has entered, or proposed to enter, into any combination to prevent the making of any bid for furnishing such supplies, or to fix a price or prices therefor, or who has made any agreement, or given or performed, or promised to give or perform, any consideration whatever to induce any other person not to bid for any such contract, or to bid at a specified price or prices thereon.

Whoever violates this section shall be [1] or imprisoned not more than one year, or both; and if the offender is a contractor for furnishing such supplies his contract may be annulled.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 704 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 91–375, §6(j)(4), Aug. 12, 1970, 84 Stat. 777 (PDF, 12 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(L), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 See 1994 Amendment note below.

1 crime in this section

Makes a contract for furnishing supplies to the Postal Service with any person who has entered, or proposed to enter, into any combination to prevent the making of any bid for furnishing such supplies, or to fix a price or prices therefor, or who has made any agreement, or given or performed, or promised to give or perform, any consideration whatever to induce any other person not to bid for any such contract, or to bid at a specified price or prices thereon

    Federal basis
    • Contract for furnishing supplies to the Postal Service
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 years in prison, or both
    Other consequences
    • If the offender is a contractor for furnishing such supplies, his contract may be annulled

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to rig bidding on contracts to supply the Postal Service, such as joining a group to block bids or fix prices, or offering anything to get someone not to bid or to bid at a set price. The Postal Service may not contract with anyone who has done this.
