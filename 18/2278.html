§ 2278

Explosives on vessels carrying steerage passengers

Up to 1 year Transportation & Aviation None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 111 · Shipping

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being the master of a steamship or other vessel referred to in section 151 of Title 46, except as otherwise expressly provided by law, takes, carries, or has on board of any such vessel any nitroglycerin, dynamite, or any other explosive article or compound, or any vitriol or like acids, or gunpowder, except for the ship's use, or any article or number of articles, whether as a cargo or ballast, which, by reason of the nature or quantity or mode of storage thereof, shall, either singly or collectively, be likely to endanger the health or lives of the passengers or the safety of the vessel, shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 805 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

Takes, carries, or has on board of any such vessel any nitroglycerin, dynamite, or any other explosive article or compound, or any vitriol or like acids, or gunpowder, or any article or number of articles, whether as cargo or ballast, likely to endanger the health or lives of the passengers or the safety of the vessel

    Ways to commit
    • takes · carries · has on board
    Who
    • The master of a steamship or other vessel referred to in section 151 of Title 46
    Federal basis
    • The vessel is a steamship or other vessel referred to in section 151 of Title 46
    Conditions
    • For other articles: by reason of the nature or quantity or mode of storage, singly or collectively, they are likely to endanger the health or lives of the passengers or the safety of the vessel
    Exceptions
    • Except as otherwise expressly provided by law
    • Gunpowder (and the listed explosives/acids) carried for the ship's use
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for the captain of certain passenger ships to carry explosives, gunpowder, strong acids, or other cargo likely to endanger passengers or the ship. Explosives or gunpowder for the ship's own use are allowed.
