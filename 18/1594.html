§ 1594

General provisions

Up to life Human Trafficking & Slavery Ch. 77 · Peonage, Slavery, and Trafficking in Persons

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever to violate section 1581, 1583, 1584, 1589, 1590, or 1591 shall be punishable in the same manner as a completed violation of that section.

(b) Whoever with another to violate section 1581, 1583, 1589, 1590, or 1592 shall be punished in the same manner as a completed violation of such section.

(c) Whoever with another to violate section 1591 shall be , imprisoned for any term of years or for life, or both.

(d) The court, in imposing sentence on any person convicted of a violation of this chapter, shall order, in addition to any other sentence imposed and irrespective of any provision of State law, that such person shall forfeit to the United States—

(1) such person's interest in any property, real or personal, that was involved in, used, or intended to be used to commit or to facilitate the commission of such violation, and any property traceable to such property; and

(2) any property, real or personal, constituting or derived from, any proceeds that such person obtained, directly or indirectly, as a result of such violation, or any property traceable to such property.

(e)

(1) The following shall be subject to forfeiture to the United States and no property right shall exist in them:

(A) Any property, real or personal, involved in, used, or intended to be used to commit or to facilitate the commission of any violation of this chapter, and any property traceable to such property.

(B) Any property, real or personal, which constitutes or is derived from proceeds traceable to any violation of this chapter.

(2) The provisions of chapter 46 of this title relating to civil forfeitures shall extend to any seizure or civil forfeiture under this subsection.

(f) Transfer of Forfeited Assets.

(1) In general. Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Attorney General shall transfer assets pursuant to this section, or the proceeds derived from the sale thereof, to satisfy victim restitution orders arising from violations of this chapter.

(2) Priority. Transfers pursuant to paragraph (1) shall have priority over any other claims to the assets or their proceeds.

(3) Use of nonforfeited assets. Transfers pursuant to paragraph (1) shall not reduce or otherwise mitigate the obligation of a person convicted of a violation of this chapter to satisfy the full amount of a restitution order through the use of non- assets or to reimburse the Attorney General for the value of assets or proceeds transferred under this subsection through the use of nonforfeited assets.

(g) Witness Protection. Any violation of this chapter shall be considered an organized criminal activity or other serious offense for the purposes of application of chapter 224 (relating to witness protection).

History: (Added Pub. L. 106–386, div. A, §112(a)(2), Oct. 28, 2000, 114 Stat. 1489; amended Pub. L. 110–457, title II, §222(c), Dec. 23, 2008, 122 Stat. 5070; Pub. L. 114–22, title I, §105(a), May 29, 2015, 129 Stat. 236.)

Other consequences
  • Criminal forfeiture of the person's interest in property involved in, used, or intended to be used to commit or facilitate the violation, and property traceable to it
  • Criminal forfeiture of property constituting or derived from proceeds of the violation, and property traceable to it
  • Civil forfeiture of property involved in, used, or intended to be used to commit or facilitate any violation of this chapter, and of proceeds traceable to any violation
  • Forfeited assets or their proceeds are transferred to satisfy victim restitution orders, without reducing the person's obligation to satisfy the full restitution order

  1. (a) Attempts to violate section 1581, 1583, 1584, 1589, 1590, or 1591

    Penalty
    • Punishable in the same manner as a completed violation of that section
    • If the violation is of section 1581: A fine, up to 20 years in prison, or both (section 1581(a))
    • If the violation is of section 1581 and death results, or the violation includes kidnapping or an attempt to kidnap, aggravated sexual abuse or the attempt to commit aggravated sexual abuse, or an attempt to kill: A fine, imprisonment for any term of years or life, or both (section 1581(a))
    • If the violation is of section 1591 and was effected by means of force, threats of force, fraud, or coercion, or the person had not attained the age of 14 years: A fine and imprisonment for any term of years not less than 15 or for life (section 1591(b)(1))mandatory minimum
    • If the violation is of section 1591, was not so effected, and the person had attained the age of 14 but not 18 years: A fine and imprisonment for not less than 10 years or for life (section 1591(b)(2))mandatory minimum

  2. (b) Conspires with another to violate section 1581, 1583, 1589, 1590, or 1592

    Penalty
    • Punished in the same manner as a completed violation of such section
    • If the violation is of section 1581: A fine, up to 20 years in prison, or both (section 1581(a))
    • If the violation is of section 1581 and death results, or the violation includes kidnapping or an attempt to kidnap, aggravated sexual abuse or the attempt to commit aggravated sexual abuse, or an attempt to kill: A fine, imprisonment for any term of years or life, or both (section 1581(a))

  3. (c) Conspires with another to violate section 1591

    Penalty
    • A fine, imprisonment for any term of years or for life, or both
