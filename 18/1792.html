§ 1792

Mutiny and riot prohibited

Up to 10 years Courts & Justice 6 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 87 · Prisons

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever instigates, connives, to cause, assists, or to cause any mutiny or riot, at any Federal penal, detention, or correctional facility, shall be imprisoned not more than ten years or , or both.

1 crime in this section

    Ways to commit

    Federal basis
    • At any Federal penal, detention, or correctional facility
    Intent

    Penalty
    • A fine, up to ten years in prison, or both
    Also covers
    • + attempt+ conspiracy

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to start, plot, try to cause, help, or conspire to cause a mutiny or riot at a federal prison, jail, or detention center.
