§ 651

Disbursing officer falsely certifying full payment

Up to 2 years Property Crimes Ch. 31 · Embezzlement and Theft

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being an officer charged with the disbursement of the public moneys, accepts, receives, or transmits to the Government Accountability Office to be allowed in his favor any receipt or voucher from a creditor of the United States without having paid the full amount specified therein to such creditor in such funds as the officer received for disbursement, or in such funds as he may be authorized by law to take in exchange, shall be or in double the amount so withheld, whichever is greater, or imprisoned not more than two years, or both; but if the amount withheld does not exceed $1,000, he shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 727 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), (2)(G), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB), 2148; Pub. L. 104–294, title VI, §606(a), Oct. 11, 1996, 110 Stat. 3511; Pub. L. 108–271, §8(b), July 7, 2004, 118 Stat. 814.)

The crime in this section

  1. Accepts, receives, or transmits to the Government Accountability Office to be allowed in his favor any receipt or voucher from a creditor of the United States

    Ways to commit
    • accepts · receives · transmits to the Government Accountability Office
    Who
    • An officer charged with the disbursement of the public moneys
    Federal basis
    • The offender disburses public moneys and the voucher is from a creditor of the United States
    Conditions
    • He has not paid the full amount specified therein to such creditor in such funds as the officer received for disbursement, or in such funds as he may be authorized by law to take in exchange
    Penalty
    • A fine under this title or double the amount so withheld, whichever is greater, up to 2 years in prison, or both
    • If the amount withheld does not exceed $1,000: A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both
