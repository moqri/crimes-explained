§ 664

Theft or embezzlement from employee benefit plan

Up to 5 years Property Crimes None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 31 · Embezzlement and Theft

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Any person who , steals, or unlawfully and abstracts or converts to his own use or to the use of another, any of the moneys, funds, securities, premiums, credits, property, or other assets of any employee welfare benefit plan or employee pension benefit plan, or of any fund connected therewith, shall be , or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

As used in this section, the term "any employee welfare benefit plan or employee pension benefit plan" means any employee benefit plan subject to any provision of title I of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974.

1 crime in this section

  1. Embezzles, steals, or unlawfully abstracts or converts to his own use or to the use of another, any of the moneys, funds, securities, premiums, credits, property, or other assets of the plan or connected fund

    Ways to commit
    • embezzles · steals · unlawfully abstracts · converts to his own use or to the use of another
    Federal basis
    • The assets belong to an employee welfare benefit plan or employee pension benefit plan (subject to title I of ERISA), or a fund connected therewith
    Intent
    • Willfully (for abstracting or converting)
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both
    Key terms
    • "Any employee welfare benefit plan or employee pension benefit plan": any employee benefit plan subject to any provision of title I of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to embezzle, steal, or willfully take money or property belonging to an employee benefit or pension plan covered by the federal ERISA law (a law governing workplace benefit plans).
