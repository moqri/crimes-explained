§ 2316

Transportation of livestock

Up to 5 years Property Crimes None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 113 · Stolen Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever transports in any livestock, knowing the same to have been stolen, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 807 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 98–473, title II, §1113, Oct. 12, 1984, 98 Stat. 2149 (PDF, 62 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Transports in interstate or foreign commerce any livestock

    Federal basis
    • Transports in interstate or foreign commerce
    Mental state
    • Knowing the same to have been stolen
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to move stolen livestock across state or national lines while knowing the animals were stolen.
