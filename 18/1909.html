§ 1909

Examiner performing other services

Up to 1 year Government & Corruption None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 93 · Public Officers and Employees

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being a national-bank examiner, Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation examiner, or farm credit examiner, performs any other service, for compensation, for any bank or banking or loan association, or for any officer, director, or employee thereof, or for any person connected therewith in any capacity, shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 792 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §§330004(12), 330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2142 (PDF, 52 MB), 2147.)

1 crime in this section

  1. Performs any other service, for compensation, for any bank or banking or loan association, or for any officer, director, or employee thereof, or for any person connected therewith in any capacity

    Who
    • A national-bank examiner, Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation examiner, or farm credit examiner
    Federal basis
    • A federal bank or farm credit examiner
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to one year in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a national bank examiner, FDIC examiner, or farm credit examiner to do any paid work for a bank, loan association, or anyone connected to one.
