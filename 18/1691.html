§ 1691

Laws governing postal savings

See penalty details Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

All the safeguards provided by law for the protection of public moneys, and all statutes relating to the embezzlement, conversion, improper handling, retention, use, or disposal of postal and money-order funds, false returns of postal and money-order business, forgery, counterfeiting, alteration, improper use or handling of postal and money-order blanks, forms, vouchers, accounts, and records, and the dies, plates, and engravings therefor, with the punishments provided for such offenses are extended and made applicable to postal savings depository business and funds and related matters.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 776 (PDF, 39 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Commits an offense under the statutes relating to the embezzlement, conversion, improper handling, retention, use, or disposal of postal and money-order funds, false returns of postal and money-order business, forgery, counterfeiting, alteration, improper use or handling of postal and money-order blanks, forms, vouchers, accounts, and records, and the dies, plates, and engravings therefor

    Federal basis
    • Postal savings depository business and funds and related matters
    Conditions
    • The offense concerns postal savings depository business and funds and related matters
    Penalty
    • Set in another law: the punishments provided for such offenses by the statutes on postal and money-order funds, returns, and blanks

Simplified explanation

This section applies the existing federal crimes for stealing, mishandling, forging, or counterfeiting postal and money-order funds, forms, and records to postal savings deposits and funds as well.
