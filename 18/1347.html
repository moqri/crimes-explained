§ 1347

Health care fraud

Up to life Fraud & Money 191 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 63 · Mail Fraud and Other Fraud Offenses

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever knowingly and executes, or to execute, a scheme or artifice—

(1) to defraud any health care benefit program; or

(2) to obtain, by means of false or fraudulent pretenses, representations, or promises, any of the money or property owned by, or under the custody or control of, any health care benefit program,

in connection with the delivery of or payment for health care benefits, items, or services, shall be or imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both. If the violation results in (as defined in section 1365 of this title), such person shall be or imprisoned not more than 20 years, or both; and if the violation results in death, such person shall be , or imprisoned for any term of years or for life, or both.

(b) With respect to violations of this section, a person need not have actual knowledge of this section or specific intent to commit a violation of this section.

Knowledge
  • Knowingly
  • Actual knowledge of this section or specific intent to commit a violation of this section is not required
Intent
  • Willfully
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both
  • If the violation results in serious bodily injury: A fine, up to 20 years in prison, or both
  • If the violation results in death: A fine, imprisonment for any term of years or for life, or both
Key terms
  • "Serious bodily injury": as defined in section 1365 (bodily injury involving a substantial risk of death, extreme physical pain, protracted and obvious disfigurement, or protracted loss or impairment of the function of a bodily member, organ, or mental faculty)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly and willfully carry out, or try to carry out, a scheme to cheat a health care benefit program or to get its money or property by lying, in connection with health care services or payments.
