§ 1652

Citizens as pirates

Up to life Transportation & Aviation None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 81 · Piracy and Privateering

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being a citizen of the United States, commits any murder or robbery, or any act of hostility against the United States, or against any citizen thereof, on the high seas, under color of any commission from any foreign prince, or state, or on pretense of authority from any person, is a pirate, and shall be imprisoned for life.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 774 (PDF, 39 MB).)

    Penalty

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a U.S. citizen to commit murder, robbery, or a hostile act against the United States or its citizens on the high seas while claiming authority from a foreign ruler, nation, or any person. Such a person is a pirate.
