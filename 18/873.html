§ 873

Blackmail

Up to 1 year Violence & Threats 1 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 41 · Extortion and Threats

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, under a threat of informing, or as a consideration for not informing, against any violation of any law of the United States, demands or receives any money or other valuable thing, shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 740 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(I), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Demands or receives any money or other valuable thing

    Ways to commit
    • demands · receives
    Federal basis
    • The threat or consideration concerns informing against a violation of a law of the United States
    Conditions
    • The demand or receipt is under a threat of informing, or as a consideration for not informing, against any violation of any law of the United States
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to demand or take money or anything of value in exchange for not reporting someone's violation of federal law, or by threatening to report it. This is commonly called blackmail.
