§ 1697

Transportation of persons acting as private express

Fine only Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, having charge or control of any conveyance operating by land, air, or water, knowingly conveys or knowingly permits the conveyance of any person acting or employed as a private express for the conveyance of letters or packets, and actually in possession of the same for the purpose of conveying them contrary to law, shall be .
