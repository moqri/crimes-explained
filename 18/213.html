§ 213

Acceptance of loan or gratuity by financial institution examiner

Up to 1 year Government & Corruption None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 11 · Bribery, Graft, and Conflicts of Interest

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) In General. Whoever, being an examiner or assistant examiner, accepts a loan or gratuity from any bank, branch, agency, organization, corporation, association, or institution examined by the examiner or from any person connected with it, shall—

(1) be , imprisoned not more than 1 year, or both;

(2) may be fined a further sum equal to the money so loaned or gratuity given; and

(3) shall be disqualified from holding office as an examiner.

(b) Definitions. In this section, the terms "examiner", "Federal financial institution regulatory agency", "financial institution", and "loan" have the same meanings as in section 212.

History: (Added Pub. L. 108–198, §2(a), Dec. 19, 2003, 117 Stat. 2900.)

1 crime in this section

  1. (a) Accepts a loan or gratuity from any bank, branch, agency, organization, corporation, association, or institution examined by the examiner or from any person connected with it

    Who
    • An examiner or assistant examiner
    Federal basis
    • The offender is an examiner (appointed by a Federal financial institution regulatory agency or under State law) of the financial institution
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both
    Other consequences
    • May be fined a further sum equal to the money so loaned or gratuity given
    • Shall be disqualified from holding office as an examiner
    Key terms
    • "Examiner", "Federal financial institution regulatory agency", "financial institution", and "loan": same meanings as in section 212
    • "Examiner": any person appointed by a Federal financial institution regulatory agency or pursuant to the laws of any State to examine a financial institution, or elected under the law of any State to conduct examinations of any financial institutions
    • "Federal financial institution regulatory agency": the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, the FDIC, the Federal Housing Finance Agency, the Farm Credit Administration, the Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation, and the Small Business Administration
    • "Financial institution": does not include a credit union, a Federal Reserve Bank, a Federal home loan bank, or a depository institution holding company
    • "Loan": does not include a credit card account under an open end consumer credit plan or a loan secured by residential real property that is the examiner's principal residence, if the applicant satisfies generally applicable financial requirements, the terms are no more favorable to the examiner than those generally offered to other borrowers in comparable circumstances, and (for residential real property loans) the loan is for the applicant's primary residence

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a bank examiner or assistant examiner (a government inspector of financial institutions) to accept a loan or gift from an institution they examine, or from anyone connected with it.
