§ 1661

Robbery ashore

Up to life Transportation & Aviation None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 81 · Piracy and Privateering

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being engaged in any piratical cruise or enterprise, or being of the crew of any piratical vessel, lands from such vessel and commits robbery on shore, is a pirate, and shall be imprisoned for life.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 775 (PDF, 39 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Lands from such vessel and commits robbery on shore

    Who
    • A person engaged in any piratical cruise or enterprise, or of the crew of any piratical vessel
    Penalty
    • Imprisonment for lifemandatory minimum

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for anyone on a pirate voyage or pirate ship crew to go ashore and commit robbery. Such a person is a pirate.
