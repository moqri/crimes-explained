§ 1002

Possession of false papers to defraud United States

Up to 5 years Fraud & Money None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 47 · Fraud and False Statements

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, knowingly and with intent to defraud the United States, or any agency thereof, possesses any false, altered, forged, or counterfeited writing or document for the purpose of enabling another to obtain from the United States, or from any agency, officer or agent thereof, any sum of money, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 749 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(L), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Possesses any false, altered, forged, or counterfeited writing or document

    Federal basis
    • Intent to defraud the United States or any agency thereof; money sought from the United States or any agency, officer or agent thereof
    Mental state
    • Knowingly
    • With intent to defraud the United States, or any agency thereof
    • For the purpose of enabling another to obtain from the United States, or from any agency, officer or agent thereof, any sum of money
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly have false, altered, forged, or counterfeit documents with intent to cheat the United States, so that someone else can get money from the government.
