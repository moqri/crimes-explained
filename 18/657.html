§ 657

Lending, credit and insurance institutions

Up to 30 years Fraud & Money 6 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 31 · Embezzlement and Theft

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being an officer, agent or employee of or connected in any capacity with the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, National Credit Union Administration, any Federal home loan bank, the Federal Housing Finance Agency, Farm Credit Administration, Department of Housing and Urban Development, Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, the Secretary of Agriculture acting through the Farmers Home Administration or successor agency, the Rural Development Administration or successor agency, or the Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation, a Farm Credit Bank, a bank for cooperatives or any lending, mortgage, insurance, credit or savings and loan corporation or association authorized or acting under the laws of the United States or any institution, other than an insured bank (as defined in section 656), the accounts of which are insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, or by the National Credit Union Administration Board or any small business investment company, or any community development financial institution receiving financial assistance under the Riegle Community Development and Regulatory Improvement Act of 1994, and whoever, being a receiver of any such institution, or agent or employee of the receiver, , abstracts, purloins or misapplies any moneys, funds, credits, securities or other things of value belonging to such institution, or pledged or otherwise intrusted to its care, shall be fined not more than $1,000,000 or imprisoned not more than 30 years, or both; but if the amount or value embezzled, abstracted, purloined or misapplied does not exceed $1,000, he shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Embezzles, abstracts, purloins or misapplies any moneys, funds, credits, securities or other things of value belonging to such institution, or pledged or otherwise intrusted to its care

    Ways to commit
    • embezzles · abstracts · purloins · willfully misapplies
    Who
    • An officer, agent or employee of or connected in any capacity with the FDIC, National Credit Union Administration, any Federal home loan bank, the Federal Housing Finance Agency, Farm Credit Administration, Department of Housing and Urban Development, Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, the Secretary of Agriculture acting through the Farmers Home Administration or successor agency, the Rural Development Administration or successor agency, the Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation, a Farm Credit Bank, a bank for cooperatives, any lending, mortgage, insurance, credit or savings and loan corporation or association authorized or acting under the laws of the United States, any institution (other than an insured bank as defined in section 656) whose accounts are insured by the FDIC or the National Credit Union Administration Board, any small business investment company, or any community development financial institution receiving assistance under the Riegle Community Development and Regulatory Improvement Act of 1994; or a receiver of any such institution, or agent or employee of the receiver
    Federal basis
    • The institution is a federal agency or a lending, credit, or insurance institution authorized under federal law, federally insured, or federally assisted, as listed
    Intent
    • Willfully (misapplies)
    Penalty
    • A fine of up to $1,000,000, up to 30 years in prison, or both
    • If the amount or value embezzled, abstracted, purloined or misapplied does not exceed $1,000: A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both
    Key terms
    • "insured bank": as defined in section 656 (such banks are excluded here)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for anyone working for or connected with certain federal lending, credit, or insurance agencies and institutions (such as the FDIC, credit unions, federal home loan banks, or farm credit banks) to embezzle, take, or willfully misuse their money or property.
