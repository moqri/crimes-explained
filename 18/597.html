§ 597

Expenditures to influence voting

Up to 2 years Elections & Voting None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 29 · Elections and Political Activities

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever makes or offers to make an expenditure to any person, either to vote or withhold his vote, or to vote for or against any candidate; and

Whoever solicits, accepts, or receives any such expenditure in consideration of his vote or the withholding of his vote—

Shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both; and if the violation was willful, shall be or imprisoned not more than two years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 721 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB); Pub. L. 104–294, title VI, §601(a)(12), Oct. 11, 1996, 110 Stat. 3498.)

2 crimes in this section

  2. Solicits, accepts, or receives any such expenditure in consideration of his vote or the withholding of his vote

    Ways to commit

Applies to all 2 crimes above
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both
  • If the violation was willful: A fine, up to 2 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to pay or offer to pay someone to vote, not vote, or vote for or against a candidate. It is also a crime to ask for or accept such payment in exchange for a vote.
