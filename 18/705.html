§ 705

Badge or medal of veterans' organizations

Up to 6 months Property Crimes None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 33 · Emblems, Insignia, and Names

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever knowingly manufactures, reproduces, sells or purchases for resale, either separately or on or appended to, any article of merchandise manufactured or sold, any badge, medal, emblem, or other insignia or any colorable imitation thereof, of any veterans' organization incorporated by enactment of Congress, or of any organization formally recognized by any such veterans' organization as an auxiliary of such veterans' organization, or knowingly prints, lithographs, engraves or otherwise reproduces on any poster, circular, periodical, magazine, newspaper, or other publication, or circulates or distributes any such printed matter bearing a reproduction of such badge, medal, emblem, or other insignia or any colorable imitation thereof, except when authorized under rules and regulations prescribed by any such organization, shall be or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 732 (PDF, 39 MB); Aug. 4, 1950, ch. 578, 64 Stat. 413; Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(E), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2146 (PDF, 52 MB).)

Federal basis
  • The badge, medal, emblem, or insignia is of a veterans' organization incorporated by enactment of Congress, or of an organization formally recognized by it as an auxiliary
Knowledge

Penalty
  • A fine, up to 6 months in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly make, copy, sell, buy for resale, or print the badge, medal, or emblem of a veterans' organization chartered by Congress (or its recognized auxiliary), or a close imitation, or to distribute printed matter showing it, without the organization's permission.
