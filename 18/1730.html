§ 1730

Uniforms of carriers

Up to 6 months Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, not being connected with the letter-carrier branch of the Postal Service, wears the uniform or badge which may be prescribed by the Postal Service to be worn by letter carriers, shall be or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.

The provisions of the preceding paragraph shall not apply to an actor or actress in a theatrical, television, or motion-picture production who wears the uniform or badge of the letter-carrier branch of the Postal Service while portraying a member of that service.

1 crime in this section

  1. Wears the uniform or badge which may be prescribed by the Postal Service to be worn by letter carriers

    Who
    • A person not connected with the letter-carrier branch of the Postal Service
    Federal basis
    • Uniform or badge prescribed by the Postal Service for letter carriers
    Exceptions
    • An actor or actress in a theatrical, television, or motion-picture production who wears the uniform or badge while portraying a member of that service
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 6 months in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for someone who is not part of the Postal Service's letter-carrier branch to wear the uniform or badge of a letter carrier.
