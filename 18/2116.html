§ 2116

Railway or steamboat post office

Up to 3 years Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 103 · Robbery and Burglary

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, by violence, enters a post-office car, or any part of any car, steamboat, or vessel, assigned to the use of the mail service, or or assaults or interferes with any postal clerk in the discharge of his duties in connection with such car, steamboat, vessel, or apartment thereof, shall be or imprisoned not more than three years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 797 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 104–294, title VI, §601(a)(8), Oct. 11, 1996, 110 Stat. 3498.)

2 crimes in this section

  1. Enters a post-office car, or any part of any car, steamboat, or vessel, assigned to the use of the mail service

    Federal basis
    • The car, steamboat, or vessel is assigned to the use of the mail service
    Conditions
    • By violence

  2. Assaults or interferes with any postal clerk in the discharge of his duties in connection with such car, steamboat, vessel, or apartment thereof

    Ways to commit
    • assaults · interferes with
    Federal basis
    • The victim is a postal clerk in the discharge of his duties
    Intent
    • Willfully or maliciously
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 3 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to violently enter a mail car or part of a train, boat, or ship assigned to mail service, or to willfully or maliciously assault or interfere with a postal clerk working there.
