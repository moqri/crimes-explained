§ 914

Creditors of the United States

Up to 5 years Fraud & Money Ch. 43 · False Personation

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever falsely personates any true and lawful holder of any share or sum in the public stocks or debt of the United States, or any person entitled to any annuity, dividend, pension, wages, or other debt due from the United States, and, under color of such false personation, transfers or endeavors to transfer such public stock or any part thereof, or receives or endeavors to receive the money of such true and lawful holder thereof, or the money of any person really entitled to receive such annuity, dividend, pension, wages, or other debt, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 742 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)
