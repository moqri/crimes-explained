§ 250

Penalties for civil rights offenses involving sexual misconduct

Up to life Civil Rights Ch. 13 · Civil Rights

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Offense. It shall be unlawful for any person to, in the course of committing an offense under this chapter or under section 901 of the Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. 3631), engage in, or cause another to engage in, sexual misconduct.

(b) Penalties. Any person who violates subsection (a) shall be—

(1) in the case of an offense involving aggravated sexual abuse, as defined in section 2241, or if the offense involved sexual abuse, as defined in section 2242, or if the offense involved an attempt to commit such aggravated sexual abuse or sexual abuse, and imprisoned for any term of years or for life;

(2) in the case of an offense involving abusive sexual contact of a child who has not attained the age of 16, of the type prohibited by section 2244(a)(5), and imprisoned for any term of years or for life;

(3) in the case of an offense involving a sexual act, as defined in section 2246, with another person without the other person's permission, and it does not amount to sexual abuse or aggravated sexual abuse, be and imprisoned for not more than 40 years;

(4) in the case of an offense involving abusive sexual contact of the type prohibited by subsection (a)(1) or (b) of section 2244, but excluding abusive sexual contact through the clothing—

(A) and imprisoned for not more than 10 years; and

(B) if the offense involves a child who has not attained the age of 12 years, imprisoned for not more than 30 years;

(5) in the case of an offense involving abusive sexual contact of the type prohibited by section 2244(a)(2)—

(A) and imprisoned for not more than 3 years; and

(B) if the offense involves a child under the age of 12, imprisoned for not more than 20 years; and

(6) in the case of an offense involving abusive sexual contact through the clothing of the type prohibited by subsection (a)(3), (a)(4), or (b) of section 2244—

(A) and imprisoned for not more than 2 years; and

(B) if the offense involves a child under the age of 12, imprisoned for not more than 10 years.

History: (Added Pub. L. 117–103, div. W, title XII, §1202(a)(1), Mar. 15, 2022, 136 Stat. 923.)

The crime in this section

  1. (a) Engages in, or causes another to engage in, sexual misconduct

    Ways to commit
    • engages in · causes another to engage in
    Federal basis
    • In the course of committing an offense under this chapter or under section 901 of the Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. 3631)
    Penalty
    • Depends on the type of sexual misconduct involved (see tiers)
    • If the offense involves aggravated sexual abuse (section 2241) or sexual abuse (section 2242), or an attempt to commit such aggravated sexual abuse or sexual abuse: A fine and imprisonment for any term of years or for life
    • If the offense involves abusive sexual contact of a child who has not attained the age of 16, of the type prohibited by section 2244(a)(5): A fine and imprisonment for any term of years or for life
    • If the offense involves a sexual act (section 2246) with another person without the other person's permission, not amounting to sexual abuse or aggravated sexual abuse: A fine and up to 40 years in prison
    • If the offense involves abusive sexual contact of the type prohibited by section 2244(a)(1) or (b), excluding abusive sexual contact through the clothing: A fine and up to 10 years in prison
    • If such contact (type prohibited by section 2244(a)(1) or (b)) involves a child who has not attained the age of 12 years: Up to 30 years in prison
    • If the offense involves abusive sexual contact of the type prohibited by section 2244(a)(2): A fine and up to 3 years in prison
    • If such contact (type prohibited by section 2244(a)(2)) involves a child under the age of 12: Up to 20 years in prison
    • If the offense involves abusive sexual contact through the clothing of the type prohibited by section 2244(a)(3), (a)(4), or (b): A fine and up to 2 years in prison
    • If such contact through the clothing involves a child under the age of 12: Up to 10 years in prison
    Key terms
    • "sexual act": as defined in section 2246 (penile contact with the vulva or anus upon penetration; mouth to genital or anal contact; penetration of the anal or genital opening by a hand, finger, or object with intent to abuse, humiliate, harass, degrade, or arouse or gratify sexual desire; or intentional touching, not through the clothing, of the genitalia of a person under 16 with such intent)
    • "aggravated sexual abuse": as defined in section 2241
    • "sexual abuse": as defined in section 2242
