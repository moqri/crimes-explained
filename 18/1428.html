§ 1428

Surrender of canceled naturalization certificate

Up to 5 years Immigration & Citizenship None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 69 · Nationality and Citizenship

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, having in his possession or control a certificate of naturalization or citizenship or a copy thereof which has been canceled as provided by law, fails to surrender the same after at least sixty days' notice by the appropriate court or the Commissioner or Deputy Commissioner of Immigration, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 767 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Fails to surrender the same

    Who
    • A person having in his possession or control a certificate of naturalization or citizenship or a copy thereof which has been canceled as provided by law
    Federal basis
    • Notice by the appropriate court or the Commissioner or Deputy Commissioner of Immigration
    Conditions
    • The certificate has been canceled as provided by law
    • At least sixty days' notice was given by the appropriate court or the Commissioner or Deputy Commissioner of Immigration
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to keep a canceled naturalization or citizenship certificate (or a copy) and fail to turn it in after at least 60 days' notice from the court or immigration officials.
