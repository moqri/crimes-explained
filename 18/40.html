§ 40

Commercial motor vehicles required to stop for inspections

Up to 1 year Transportation & Aviation None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 2 · Aircraft and Motor Vehicles

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) A driver of a commercial motor vehicle (as defined in section 31132 of title 49) shall stop and submit to inspection of the vehicle, driver, cargo, and required records when directed to do so by an authorized employee of the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration of the Department of Transportation, at or in the vicinity of an inspection site. The driver shall not leave the inspection site until authorized to do so by an authorized employee.

(b) A driver of a commercial motor vehicle, as defined in subsection (a), who knowingly fails to stop for inspection when directed to do so by an authorized employee of the Administration at or in the vicinity of an inspection site, or leaves the inspection site without authorization, shall be or imprisoned not more than 1 year, or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 109–59, title IV, §4143(a), Aug. 10, 2005, 119 Stat. 1747, §39; renumbered §40, Pub. L. 110–244, title III, §301(j), June 6, 2008, 122 Stat. 1616.)

Simplified explanation

Drivers of commercial motor vehicles (such as large trucks) must stop for inspection when directed by an authorized Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration employee at or near an inspection site, and must not leave until allowed. It is a federal crime to knowingly fail to stop or to leave without permission.
