§ 658

Property mortgaged or pledged to farm credit agencies

Up to 5 years Property Crimes None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 31 · Embezzlement and Theft

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, with intent to defraud, knowingly conceals, removes, disposes of, or converts to his own use or to that of another, any property mortgaged or pledged to, or held by, the Farm Credit Administration, any Federal intermediate credit bank, or the Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, the Secretary of Agriculture acting through the Farmers Home Administration or successor agency, the Rural Development Administration or successor agency, any production credit association organized under sections 1131–1134m of Title 12, any regional agricultural credit corporation, or any bank for cooperatives, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both; but if the value of such property does not exceed $1,000, he shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

1 crime in this section

  1. Conceals, removes, disposes of, or converts to his own use or to that of another, any property mortgaged or pledged to, or held by, the listed farm credit agencies

    Ways to commit
    • conceals · removes · disposes of · converts to his own use or to that of another
    Federal basis
    • The property is mortgaged or pledged to, or held by, the Farm Credit Administration, any Federal intermediate credit bank, the Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, the Secretary of Agriculture acting through the Farmers Home Administration or successor agency, the Rural Development Administration or successor agency, any production credit association organized under sections 1131-1134m of Title 12, any regional agricultural credit corporation, or any bank for cooperatives
    Knowledge

    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both
    • If the value of such property does not exceed $1,000: A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to hide, remove, sell off, or take for oneself property that is pledged as security to (or held by) certain federal farm credit agencies, with intent to cheat.
