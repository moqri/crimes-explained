§ 1716B

Nonmailable plants

Up to 1 year Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever knowingly deposits for mailing or delivery, or knowingly causes to be delivered by mail, according to the direction thereon, or at any place at which it is directed to be delivered by the person to whom it is addressed, anything declared nonmailable by section 3014(b) of title 39, unless in accordance with the rules and regulations prescribed by the Postal Service under section 3014(c) of such title, shall be , or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 100–574, §1(b)(1), Oct. 31, 1988, 102 Stat. 2893.)

1 crime in this section

    Ways to commit

    • Uses the mails

    • Knowingly
    Conditions
    • The matter is declared nonmailable by section 3014(b) of title 39 (plants and plant products)
    Exceptions
    • Mailing in accordance with the rules and regulations prescribed by the Postal Service under section 3014(c) of title 39
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly mail plants that federal postal law (39 U.S.C. 3014(b)) bans from the mail, unless done under Postal Service rules.
