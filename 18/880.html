§ 880

Receiving the proceeds of extortion

Up to 3 years Property Crimes None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 41 · Extortion and Threats

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

A person who receives, possesses, conceals, or disposes of any money or other property which was obtained from the commission of any offense under this chapter that is punishable by imprisonment for more than 1 year, knowing the same to have been unlawfully obtained, shall be imprisoned not more than 3 years, , or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXII, §320601(a)(1), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2115 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Receives, possesses, conceals, or disposes of any money or other property which was obtained from the commission of any offense under this chapter

    Ways to commit
    • receives · possesses · conceals · disposes of
    Federal basis
    • The money or property was obtained from the commission of an offense under this chapter
    Knowledge
    • Knowing the same to have been unlawfully obtained
    Conditions
    • The offense under this chapter from which the property was obtained is punishable by imprisonment for more than 1 year
    Penalty
    • Up to 3 years in prison, a fine, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to receive, keep, hide, or get rid of money or property that someone got through an extortion or threat crime under this chapter that carries more than 1 year in prison.
