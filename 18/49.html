§ 49

Enforcement of animal fighting prohibitions

Up to 5 years Animals & Wildlife 15 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 3 · Animals, Birds, Fish, and Plants

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) In General. Whoever violates subsection (a)(1), (b), (c), or (d) of section 26 of the Animal Welfare Act shall be , imprisoned for not more than 5 years, or both, for each violation.

(b) Attending an Animal Fighting Venture. Whoever violates subsection (a)(2)(A) of section 26 of the Animal Welfare Act (7 U.S.C. 2156) shall be , imprisoned for not more than 1 year, or both, for each violation.

(c) Causing an Individual Who Has Not Attained the Age of 16 To Attend an Animal Fighting Venture.—Whoever violates subsection (a)(2)(B) of section 26 (7 U.S.C. 2156) of the Animal Welfare Act shall be , imprisoned for not more than 3 years, or both, for each violation.

3 crimes in this section

  1. (a) Violates subsection (a)(1), (b), (c), or (d) of section 26 of the Animal Welfare Act

    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both, for each violation

  2. (b) Violates subsection (a)(2)(A) of section 26 of the Animal Welfare Act (attending an animal fighting venture)

    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both, for each violation

  3. (c) Violates subsection (a)(2)(B) of section 26 of the Animal Welfare Act (causing an individual who has not attained the age of 16 to attend an animal fighting venture)

    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 3 years in prison, or both, for each violation
Applies to all 3 crimes above
Federal basis
  • The conduct violates section 26 of the Animal Welfare Act (7 U.S.C. 2156)

Simplified explanation

This section does not itself define the crimes; it sets the criminal penalties for breaking the animal fighting rules in section 26 of the Animal Welfare Act. It covers animal fighting ventures, attending them, and bringing a child under 16 to one.
