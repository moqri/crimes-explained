§ 2317

Sale or receipt of livestock

Up to 5 years Property Crimes None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 113 · Stolen Property

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever receives, conceals, stores, barters, buys, sells, or disposes of any livestock, moving in or constituting a part of , knowing the same to have been stolen, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 807 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 98–473, title II, §1114, Oct. 12, 1984, 98 Stat. 2149 (PDF, 62 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Receives, conceals, stores, barters, buys, sells, or disposes of any livestock

    Ways to commit
    • receives · conceals · stores · barters · buys · sells · disposes of
    Federal basis
    • The livestock is moving in or constitutes a part of interstate or foreign commerce
    Mental state
    • Knowing the same to have been stolen
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to receive, hide, store, trade, buy, or sell stolen livestock that is moving in or part of trade between states or countries, while knowing it was stolen.
