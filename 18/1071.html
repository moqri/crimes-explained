§ 1071

Concealing person from arrest

Up to 5 years Courts & Justice 3 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 49 · Fugitives From Justice

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever harbors or conceals any person for whose arrest a warrant or process has been issued under the provisions of any law of the United States, so as to prevent his discovery and arrest, after notice or knowledge of the fact that a warrant or process has been issued for the apprehension of such person, shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both; except that if the warrant or process issued on a charge of , or after conviction of such person of any offense, the punishment shall be a , or imprisonment for not more than five years, or both.
