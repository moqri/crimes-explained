§ 2271

Conspiracy to destroy vessels

Up to 10 years Transportation & Aviation None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 111 · Shipping

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, on the high seas, or within the United States, and , combines, and confederates with any other person, such other person being either within or without the United States, to cast away or otherwise destroy any vessel, with intent to injure any person that may have underwritten or may thereafter underwrite any policy of insurance thereon or on goods on board thereof, or with intent to injure any person that has lent or advanced, or may lend or advance, any money on such vessel on bottomry or respondentia; or

Whoever, within the United States, builds, or fits out any vessel to be cast away or destroyed, with like intent—

Shall be or imprisoned not more than ten years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 803 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(L), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

2 crimes in this section

  1. first paragraph Conspires, combines, and confederates with any other person to cast away or otherwise destroy any vessel

    Ways to commit
    • conspires · combines · confederates
    Federal basis
    • On the high seas, or within the United States (the other person may be within or without the United States)
    Mental state
    • Willfully and corruptly
    • With intent to injure any person that may have underwritten or may thereafter underwrite any policy of insurance on the vessel or on goods on board, or with intent to injure any person that has lent or advanced, or may lend or advance, any money on the vessel on bottomry or respondentia

  2. second paragraph Builds, or fits out any vessel to be cast away or destroyed

    Ways to commit
    • builds · fits out
    Federal basis
    • Within the United States
    Mental state
    • With intent to injure any person that may have underwritten or may thereafter underwrite any policy of insurance on the vessel or on goods on board, or with intent to injure any person that has lent or advanced, or may lend or advance, any money on the vessel on bottomry or respondentia ("like intent")
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to conspire (agree with someone) to sink or destroy a ship in order to cheat its insurers or lenders. It is also a crime to build or equip a ship within the United States for that purpose.
