§ 2385

Advocating overthrow of Government

Up to 20 years Espionage & National Security None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 115 · Treason, Sedition, and Subversive Activities

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever knowingly or advocates, abets, advises, or teaches the duty, necessity, desirability, or propriety of overthrowing or destroying the government of the United States or the government of any State, Territory, District or Possession thereof, or the government of any political subdivision therein, by force or violence, or by the assassination of any officer of any such government; or

Whoever, with intent to cause the overthrow or destruction of any such government, prints, publishes, edits, issues, circulates, sells, distributes, or publicly displays any written or printed matter advocating, advising, or teaching the duty, necessity, desirability, or propriety of overthrowing or destroying any government in the United States by force or violence, or to do so; or

Whoever organizes or helps or to organize any society, group, or assembly of persons who teach, advocate, or encourage the overthrow or destruction of any such government by force or violence; or becomes or is a member of, or affiliates with, any such society, group, or assembly of persons, knowing the purposes thereof—

Shall be or imprisoned not more than twenty years, or both, and shall be ineligible for employment by the United States or any department or agency thereof, for the five years next following his conviction.

If two or more persons conspire to commit any offense named in this section, each shall be or imprisoned not more than twenty years, or both, and shall be ineligible for employment by the United States or any department or agency thereof, for the five years next following his conviction.

As used in this section, the terms "organizes" and "organize", with respect to any society, group, or assembly of persons, include the recruiting of new members, the forming of new units, and the regrouping or expansion of existing clubs, classes, and other units of such society, group, or assembly of persons.

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly or willfully urge or teach that any government in the United States should be overthrown by force, violence, or assassination. It is also a crime to publish or distribute such material with intent to cause an overthrow, or to organize or knowingly join a group that pushes for violent overthrow. Conspiring to do any of these is also a crime.
