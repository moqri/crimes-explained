§ 756

Internee of belligerent nation

Up to 5 years Courts & Justice None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 35 · Escape and Rescue

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, within the jurisdiction of the United States, aids or entices any person belonging to the armed forces of a belligerent nation or faction who is interned in the United States in accordance with the law of nations, to escape or attempt to escape from the jurisdiction of the United States or from the limits of internment prescribed, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 735 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB); Pub. L. 104–132, title VII, §705(a)(3), Apr. 24, 1996, 110 Stat. 1295.)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime, within U.S. jurisdiction, to help or lure a member of a foreign country's armed forces who is being held (interned) in the U.S. during a war to escape. This covers escaping the country or leaving the area where they must stay.
