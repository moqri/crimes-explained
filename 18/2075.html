§ 2075

Officer failing to make returns or reports

Fine only Government & Corruption None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 101 · Records and Reports

Every officer who neglects or refuses to make any return or report which he is required to make at stated times by any Act of Congress or regulation of the Department of the Treasury, other than his accounts, within the time prescribed by such Act or regulation, shall be .

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 796 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 107–273, div. B, title IV, §4002(d)(1)(C)(ii), Nov. 2, 2002, 116 Stat. 1809.)

1 crime in this section

  1. Neglects or refuses to make any return or report which he is required to make at stated times by any Act of Congress or regulation of the Department of the Treasury

    Ways to commit
    • neglects · refuses
    Who
    • Every officer
    Federal basis
    • The return or report is required at stated times by an Act of Congress or regulation of the Department of the Treasury
    Conditions
    • The return or report is not made within the time prescribed by such Act or regulation
    Exceptions
    • Does not apply to his accounts
    Penalty
    • A fine

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a federal officer to neglect or refuse to file a required return or report on time when a law or Treasury regulation requires it at stated times. This does not cover the officer's accounts.
