§ 2381

Treason

Death penalty possible Espionage & National Security None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 115 · Treason, Sedition, and Subversive Activities

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, owing allegiance to the United States, levies war against them or adheres to their enemies, giving them aid and comfort within the United States or elsewhere, is guilty of treason and shall suffer death, or shall be imprisoned not less than five years and but not less than $10,000; and shall be incapable of holding any office under the United States.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 807 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(2)(J), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2148 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

    Ways to commit
    • levies war against them · adheres to their enemies, giving them aid and comfort
    Who
    • A person owing allegiance to the United States
    Penalty
    • Death, or imprisonment of not less than five years and a fine under this title of not less than $10,000mandatory minimum
    Other consequences
    • Incapable of holding any office under the United States

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime, called treason, for a person who owes loyalty to the United States to wage war against the country or to side with its enemies by giving them help, whether inside the U.S. or elsewhere.
