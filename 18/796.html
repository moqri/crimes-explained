§ 796

Use of aircraft for photographing defense installations

Up to 1 year Espionage & National Security Ch. 37 · Espionage and Censorship

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever uses or permits the use of an aircraft or any contrivance used, or designed for navigation or flight in the air, for the purpose of making a photograph, sketch, picture, drawing, map, or graphical representation of vital military or naval installations or equipment, in violation of section 795 of this title, shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 738 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

The crime in this section

  1. Uses or permits the use of an aircraft or any contrivance used, or designed for navigation or flight in the air

    Ways to commit
    • uses · permits the use of
    Federal basis
    • The vital military or naval installations or equipment are defined by the President under section 795
    Intent
    • For the purpose of making a photograph, sketch, picture, drawing, map, or graphical representation of vital military or naval installations or equipment
    Conditions
    • The making of the photograph or representation is in violation of section 795
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both
