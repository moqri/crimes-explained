§ 1517

Obstructing examination of financial institution

Up to 5 years Courts & Justice None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 73 · Obstruction of Justice

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever obstructs or to obstruct any examination of a financial institution by an agency of the United States with jurisdiction to conduct an examination of such financial institution shall be , imprisoned not more than 5 years, or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 101–647, title XXV, §2503(a), Nov. 29, 1990, 104 Stat. 4861 (PDF, 27 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Obstructs any examination of a financial institution

    Federal basis
    • The examination is of a financial institution by an agency of the United States with jurisdiction to conduct it
    Intent
    • Corruptly
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both
    Also covers
    • + attempt

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to corruptly block or try to block a federal agency's examination of a financial institution, such as a bank.
