§ 1991

Entering train to commit crime

Up to 20 years Transportation & Aviation None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 97 · Railroad Carriers and Mass Transportation Systems On Land, On Water, Or Through The Air

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, in any Territory or District, or within or upon any place within the exclusive jurisdiction of the United States, and trespasses upon or enters upon any railroad train, railroad car, or railroad locomotive, with the intent to commit murder or robbery, shall be or imprisoned not more than twenty years, or both.

Whoever, within such jurisdiction, and trespasses upon or enters upon any railroad train, railroad car, or railroad locomotive, with intent to commit any unlawful violence upon or against any passenger on said train, or car, or upon or against any engineer, conductor, fireman, brakeman, or any officer or employee connected with said locomotive, train, or car, or upon or against any express messenger or mail agent on said train or in any car thereof, or to commit any crime or offense against any person or property thereon, shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

Upon the trial of any person charged with any offense set forth in this section, it shall not be necessary to set forth or prove the particular person against whom it was intended to commit the offense, or that it was intended to commit such offense against any particular person.

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to willfully and maliciously trespass on or enter a train, rail car, or locomotive intending to commit murder, robbery, violence against passengers or crew, or another crime against people or property on board. This applies in U.S. territories, districts, and places under exclusive federal jurisdiction.
