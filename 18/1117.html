§ 1117

Conspiracy to murder

Up to life Violence & Threats 10 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 51 · Homicide

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

If two or more persons conspire to violate section 1111, 1114, 1116, or 1119 of this title, and one or more of such persons do any overt act to effect the object of the , each shall be punished by imprisonment for any term of years or for life.

History: (Added Pub. L. 92–539, title I, §101, Oct. 24, 1972, 86 Stat. 1071; amended Pub. L. 103–322, title VI, §60009(b)(1), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 1972 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Conspires to violate section 1111, 1114, 1116, or 1119

    Federal basis
    • The object is a violation of section 1111, 1114, 1116, or 1119
    Conditions
    • Two or more persons conspire
    • One or more of the conspirators do any overt act to effect the object of the conspiracy
    Penalty
    • Imprisonment for any term of years or for life
    Also covers
    • + conspiracy

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for two or more people to agree to commit a murder covered by sections 1111, 1114, 1116, or 1119, if at least one of them takes a step to carry out the plan.
