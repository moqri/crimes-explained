§ 1363

Buildings or property within special maritime and territorial jurisdiction

Up to 20 years Property Crimes 7 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 65 · Malicious Mischief

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, within the , and destroys or injures any structure, conveyance, or other real or personal property, or or to do such an act, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both, and if the building be a dwelling, or the life of any person be placed in jeopardy, shall be or imprisoned not more than twenty years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 764 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), (K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB); Pub. L. 104–132, title VII, §703, Apr. 24, 1996, 110 Stat. 1294; Pub. L. 107–56, title VIII, §811(d), Oct. 26, 2001, 115 Stat. 381.)
