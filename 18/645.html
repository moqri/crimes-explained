§ 645

Court officers generally

Up to 10 years Property Crimes None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 31 · Embezzlement and Theft

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being a United States marshal, clerk, receiver, referee, trustee, or other officer of a United States court, or any deputy, assistant, or employee of any such officer, retains or converts to his own use or to the use of another or after demand by the party entitled thereto, unlawfully retains any money coming into his hands by virtue of his official relation, position or employment, is guilty of embezzlement and shall, where the offense is not otherwise punishable by enactment of Congress, be or not more than double the value of the money so embezzled, whichever is greater, or imprisoned not more than ten years, or both; but if the amount embezzled does not exceed $1,000, he shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

It shall not be a defense that the accused person had any interest in such moneys or fund.

    Ways to commit
    • retains · converts to his own use or to the use of another · after demand by the party entitled thereto, unlawfully retains

    • A United States marshal, clerk, receiver, referee, trustee, or other officer of a United States court, or any deputy, assistant, or employee of any such officer
    Federal basis
    • The offender is an officer of a United States court, or a deputy, assistant, or employee of such an officer

    • It shall not be a defense that the accused person had any interest in such moneys or fund
    Exceptions
    • Applies where the offense is not otherwise punishable by enactment of Congress
    Penalty
    • A fine under this title or not more than double the value of the money so embezzled, whichever is greater, up to 10 years in prison, or both
    • If the amount embezzled does not exceed $1,000: A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both
