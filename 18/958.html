§ 958

Commission to serve against friendly nation

Up to 3 years Espionage & National Security None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 45 · Foreign Relations

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Any citizen of the United States who, within the jurisdiction thereof, accepts and exercises a commission to serve a foreign prince, state, colony, district, or people, in war, against any prince, state, colony, district, or people, with whom the United States is at peace, shall be or imprisoned not more than three years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 745 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(I), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Accepts and exercises a commission to serve a foreign prince, state, colony, district, or people, in war, against any prince, state, colony, district, or people, with whom the United States is at peace

    Who
    • A citizen of the United States
    Federal basis
    • Within the jurisdiction of the United States
    Conditions
    • The United States is at peace with the prince, state, colony, district, or people against whom the service is directed
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 3 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a U.S. citizen, while in the United States, to accept and act on a commission (official appointment) to serve a foreign power in war against a country the U.S. is at peace with.
