§ 2422

Coercion and enticement

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly persuade, lure, or force someone to travel across state or national lines to engage in prostitution or other illegal sexual activity. It is also a crime to use the mail, internet, phone, or similar means to knowingly persuade, lure, or force anyone under 18 into prostitution or illegal sexual activity. Attempts are also crimes.
