§ 1301

Importing or transporting lottery tickets

Up to 2 years Gambling & Obscenity None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 61 · Lotteries

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever brings into the United States for the purpose of disposing of the same, or knowingly deposits with any express company or other common carrier for carriage, or carries in any paper, certificate, or instrument purporting to be or to represent a ticket, chance, share, or interest in or dependent upon the event of a lottery, gift enterprise, or similar scheme, offering prizes dependent in whole or in part upon lot or chance, or any advertisement of, or list of the prizes drawn or awarded by means of, any such lottery, gift enterprise, or similar scheme; or, being engaged in the business of procuring for a person in 1 State such a ticket, chance, share, or interest in a lottery, gift,[1] enterprise or similar scheme conducted by another State (unless that business is permitted under an agreement between the States in question or appropriate authorities of those States), knowingly transmits in information to be used for the purpose of procuring such a ticket, chance, share, or interest; or knowingly takes or receives any such paper, certificate, instrument, advertisement, or list so brought, deposited, or transported, shall be or imprisoned not more than two years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 762 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXII, §320905, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2126 (PDF, 52 MB), 2147.)

1 So in original. The comma probably should not appear.

5 crimes in this section

  1. Brings into the United States any paper, certificate, or instrument purporting to be or to represent a ticket, chance, share, or interest in or dependent upon the event of a lottery, gift enterprise, or similar scheme offering prizes dependent in whole or in part upon lot or chance, or any advertisement of, or list of the prizes drawn or awarded by means of, any such lottery, gift enterprise, or similar scheme

    Federal basis
    • Importation into the United States
    Intent
    • For the purpose of disposing of the same

  2. Deposits with any express company or other common carrier for carriage any paper, certificate, or instrument purporting to be or to represent a ticket, chance, share, or interest in or dependent upon the event of a lottery, gift enterprise, or similar scheme offering prizes dependent in whole or in part upon lot or chance, or any advertisement of, or list of the prizes drawn or awarded by means of, any such lottery, gift enterprise, or similar scheme

    Federal basis
    • Carriage by an express company or other common carrier
    Knowledge
    • Knowingly

  3. Carries in interstate or foreign commerce any paper, certificate, or instrument purporting to be or to represent a ticket, chance, share, or interest in or dependent upon the event of a lottery, gift enterprise, or similar scheme offering prizes dependent in whole or in part upon lot or chance, or any advertisement of, or list of the prizes drawn or awarded by means of, any such lottery, gift enterprise, or similar scheme

    Federal basis
    • In interstate or foreign commerce

  4. Transmits in interstate or foreign commerce information to be used for the purpose of procuring such a ticket, chance, share, or interest

    Who
    • A person engaged in the business of procuring for a person in 1 State a ticket, chance, share, or interest in a lottery, gift enterprise, or similar scheme conducted by another State
    Federal basis
    • In interstate or foreign commerce
    Knowledge
    • Knowingly
    Exceptions
    • Unless that business is permitted under an agreement between the States in question or appropriate authorities of those States

  5. Takes or receives any such paper, certificate, instrument, advertisement, or list so brought, deposited, or transported

    Ways to commit
    • takes · receives
    Federal basis
    • The item was brought into the United States, deposited with a carrier, or transported in interstate or foreign commerce
    Knowledge
    • Knowingly
Applies to all 5 crimes above
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 2 years in prison, or both
