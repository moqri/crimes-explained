§ 1731

Vehicles falsely labeled as carriers

Up to 6 months Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

It shall be unlawful to paint, print, or in any manner to place upon or attach to any steamboat or other vessel, or any car, stagecoach, vehicle, or other conveyance, not actually used in carrying the mail, the words "United States Mail", or any words, letters, or characters of like import; or to give notice, by publishing in any newspaper or otherwise, that any steamboat or other vessel, or any car, stagecoach, vehicle, or other conveyance, is used in carrying the mail, when the same is not actually so used.

Whoever violates, and every owner, receiver, lessee, or managing operator who suffers, or permits the violation of, any provision of this section, shall be or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 785 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(G), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

Federal basis
  • Falsely represents a conveyance as carrying the United States mail
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 6 months in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to put the words "United States Mail" (or similar words) on a ship, car, or other vehicle that does not actually carry mail. It is also a crime to falsely advertise that a vehicle carries mail.
