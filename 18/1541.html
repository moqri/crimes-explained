§ 1541

Issuance without authority

Up to 25 years Immigration & Citizenship None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 75 · Passports and Visas

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, acting or claiming to act in any office or capacity under the United States, or a State, without lawful authority grants, issues, or verifies any passport or other instrument in the nature of a passport to or for any person whomsoever; or

Whoever, being a consular officer authorized to grant, issue, or verify passports, knowingly and grants, issues, or verifies any such passport to or for any person not owing allegiance, to the United States, whether a citizen or not—

Shall be , imprisoned not more than 25 years (if the offense was committed to facilitate an act of international terrorism (as defined in section 2331 of this title)), 20 years (if the offense was committed to facilitate a drug trafficking crime (as defined in section 929(a) of this title)), 10 years (in the case of the first or second such offense, if the offense was not committed to facilitate such an act of international terrorism or a drug trafficking crime), or 15 years (in the case of any other offense), or both.

For purposes of this section, the term "State" means a State of the United States, the District of Columbia, and any commonwealth, territory, or possession of the United States.

Applies to all 2 crimes above
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both (first or second such offense, not committed to facilitate international terrorism or a drug trafficking crime)
  • If the offense was committed to facilitate an act of international terrorism (as defined in section 2331): A fine, up to 25 years in prison, or both
  • If the offense was committed to facilitate a drug trafficking crime (as defined in section 929(a)): A fine, up to 20 years in prison, or both
  • If the offense is not a first or second such offense (any other offense): A fine, up to 15 years in prison, or both
Key terms
  • "International terrorism" (section 2331): activities that involve violent acts or acts dangerous to human life that violate the criminal laws of the United States or any State; appear to be intended to intimidate or coerce a civilian population, influence the policy of a government by intimidation or coercion, or affect the conduct of a government by mass destruction, assassination, or kidnapping; and occur primarily outside the territorial jurisdiction of the United States or transcend national boundaries
  • "Drug trafficking crime" (section 929(a)): any felony punishable under the Controlled Substances Act, the Controlled Substances Import and Export Act, or chapter 705 of title 46
  • "State": a State of the United States, the District of Columbia, and any commonwealth, territory, or possession of the United States
