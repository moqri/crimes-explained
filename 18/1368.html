§ 1368

Harming animals used in law enforcement

Up to 10 years Animals & Wildlife 1 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 65 · Malicious Mischief

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever and harms any police animal, or or to do so, shall be and imprisoned not more than 1 year. If the offense permanently disables or disfigures the animal, or causes to or the death of the animal, the maximum term of imprisonment shall be 10 years.

(b)

In this section, the term "police animal" means a dog or horse employed by a Federal agency (whether in the executive, legislative, or judicial branch) for the principal purpose of aiding in the detection of criminal activity, enforcement of laws, or apprehension of criminal offenders.

History: (Added Pub. L. 106–254, §2(a), Aug. 2, 2000, 114 Stat. 638; amended Pub. L. 107–273, div. B, title IV, §4003(a)(4), Nov. 2, 2002, 116 Stat. 1811.)

    Mental state

    Penalty
    • A fine and up to 1 year in prison
    • If the offense permanently disables or disfigures the animal, or causes serious bodily injury to or the death of the animal: A fine and up to 10 years in prison
    Key terms
    • "Police animal": a dog or horse employed by a Federal agency (executive, legislative, or judicial branch) for the principal purpose of aiding in the detection of criminal activity, enforcement of laws, or apprehension of criminal offenders
    Also covers
    • + attempt+ conspiracy

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to willfully and maliciously harm a police dog or horse used by a federal agency. Trying or planning with others to do so is also a crime.
