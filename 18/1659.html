§ 1659

Attack to plunder vessel

Up to 10 years Transportation & Aviation None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 81 · Piracy and Privateering

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, upon the high seas or other waters within the admiralty and maritime jurisdiction of the United States, by surprise or open force, attacks or sets upon any vessel belonging to another, with an intent unlawfully to plunder the same, or to despoil any owner thereof of any moneys, goods, or merchandise laden on board thereof, shall be or imprisoned not more than ten years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 775 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to attack another person's ship by surprise or open force, on the high seas or U.S. maritime waters, intending to rob the ship or its cargo.
