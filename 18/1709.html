§ 1709

Theft of mail matter by officer or employee

Up to 5 years Mail & Postal 76 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being a Postal Service officer or employee, any letter, postal card, package, bag, or mail, or any article or thing contained therein entrusted to him or which comes into his possession intended to be conveyed by mail, or carried or delivered by any carrier, messenger, agent, or other person employed in any department of the Postal Service, or forwarded through or delivered from any post office or station thereof established by authority of the Postmaster General or of the Postal Service; or steals, abstracts, or removes from any such letter, package, bag, or mail, any article or thing contained therein, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 780 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 91–375, §6(j)(19)(A), Aug. 12, 1970, 84 Stat. 778 (PDF, 12 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(I), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

2 crimes in this section

  1. Embezzles any letter, postal card, package, bag, or mail, or any article or thing contained therein entrusted to him or which comes into his possession

    Conditions
    • The item is intended to be conveyed by mail, or carried or delivered by any carrier, messenger, agent, or other person employed in any department of the Postal Service, or forwarded through or delivered from any post office or station thereof

  2. Steals, abstracts, or removes from any such letter, package, bag, or mail, any article or thing contained therein

    Ways to commit
    • steals · abstracts · removes
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Who
  • A Postal Service officer or employee
Federal basis
  • Involves the mail of the United States
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a Postal Service worker to embezzle mail entrusted to them, or to steal or remove anything from mail.
