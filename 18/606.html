§ 606

Intimidation to secure political contributions

Up to 3 years Elections & Voting None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 29 · Elections and Political Activities

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being one of the officers or employees of the United States mentioned in section 602 of this title, discharges, or promotes, or degrades, or in any manner changes the official rank or compensation of any other officer or employee, or promises or threatens so to do, for giving or withholding or neglecting to make any contribution of money or other valuable thing for any political purpose, shall be or imprisoned not more than three years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 722 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

    Ways to commit
    • discharges · promotes · degrades · in any manner changes the official rank or compensation · promises so to do · threatens so to do
    Who
    • One of the officers or employees of the United States mentioned in section 602
    Federal basis
    • The offender is an officer or employee of the United States mentioned in section 602
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 3 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for certain federal officials or employees to fire, demote, promote, or change the pay of another federal worker, or threaten or promise to, because that worker gave or refused to give a political contribution.
