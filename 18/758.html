§ 758

High speed flight from immigration checkpoint

Up to 5 years Immigration & Citizenship 14 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 35 · Escape and Rescue

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever flees or evades a checkpoint operated by the Immigration and Naturalization Service, or any other Federal law enforcement agency, in a motor vehicle and flees Federal, State, or local law enforcement agents in excess of the legal speed limit shall be , imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 104–208, div. C, title I, §108(b)(1), Sept. 30, 1996, 110 Stat. 3009–557.)
