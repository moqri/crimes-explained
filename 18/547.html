§ 547

Depositing goods in buildings on boundaries

Up to 2 years Fraud & Money None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 27 · Customs

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever receives or deposits any merchandise in any building upon the boundary line between the United States and any foreign country, or carries any merchandise through the same, in violation of law, shall be or imprisoned not more than two years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 717 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)
