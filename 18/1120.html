§ 1120

Murder by escaped prisoners

See penalty details Violence & Threats None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 51 · Homicide

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Definition. In this section, "Federal correctional institution" and "term of life imprisonment" have the meanings stated in section 1118.

(b) Offense and Penalty. A person, having escaped from a Federal correctional institution where the person was confined under a sentence for a term of life imprisonment, kills another shall be punished as provided in sections 1111 and 1112.

History: (Added Pub. L. 103–322, title VI, §60012(a), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 1973 (PDF, 52 MB); amended Pub. L. 104–294, title VI, §601(c)(2), Oct. 11, 1996, 110 Stat. 3499.)

1 crime in this section

  1. (b) Kills another

    Who
    • A person who has escaped from a Federal correctional institution where the person was confined under a sentence for a term of life imprisonment
    Federal basis
    • The person escaped from a Federal correctional institution
    Penalty
    • Set in section 1111(b); section 1112
    Key terms
    • "Federal correctional institution" and "term of life imprisonment": as defined in section 1118 (any Federal prison, correctional facility, community program center, or halfway house; a sentence for natural life, commuted to natural life, an indeterminate term of at least 15 years to life, or an unexecuted death sentence)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a person who escaped from a federal prison while serving a life sentence to kill someone.
