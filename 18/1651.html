§ 1651

Piracy under law of nations

Up to life Transportation & Aviation None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 81 · Piracy and Privateering

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, on the high seas, commits the crime of piracy as defined by the law of nations, and is afterwards brought into or found in the United States, shall be imprisoned for life.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 774 (PDF, 39 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Commits the crime of piracy as defined by the law of nations

    Federal basis
    • Committed on the high seas
    • The offender is afterwards brought into or found in the United States
    Penalty
    • Imprisonment for lifemandatory minimum

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to commit piracy on the high seas, as defined by international law, if the person is later brought into or found in the United States.
