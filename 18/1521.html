§ 1521

Retaliating against a Federal judge or Federal law enforcement officer by false claim or slander of title

Up to 10 years Courts & Justice None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 73 · Obstruction of Justice

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever files, to file, or to file, in any public record or in any private record which is generally available to the public, any false lien or encumbrance against the real or personal property of an individual described in section 1114, on account of the performance of official duties by that individual, knowing or that such lien or encumbrance is false or contains any materially false, fictitious, or fraudulent statement or representation, shall be or imprisoned for not more than 10 years, or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 110–177, title II, §201(a), Jan. 7, 2008, 121 Stat. 2535.)

1 crime in this section

  1. Files, in any public record or in any private record which is generally available to the public, any false lien or encumbrance against the real or personal property of an individual described in section 1114

    Federal basis
    • The property belongs to an individual described in section 1114 (an officer or employee of the United States or of any agency in any branch of the United States Government, including any member of the uniformed services, or any person assisting such an officer or employee in the performance of such duties)
    Knowledge
    • Knowing or having reason to know that such lien or encumbrance is false or contains any materially false, fictitious, or fraudulent statement or representation
    Conditions
    • The filing is on account of the performance of official duties by that individual
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both
    Also covers
    • + attempt+ conspiracy
