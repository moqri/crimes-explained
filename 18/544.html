§ 544

Relanding of goods

Up to 2 years Fraud & Money 1 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 27 · Customs

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

If any merchandise entered or withdrawn for exportation without payment of the duties thereon, or with intent to obtain a drawback of the duties paid, or of any other allowances given by law on the exportation thereof, is relanded at any place in the United States without entry having been made, such merchandise shall be considered as having been imported into the United States contrary to law, and each person concerned shall be or imprisoned not more than two years, or both; and such merchandise shall be .

The term "any place in the United States", as used in this section, shall not include the Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Wake Island, Midway Islands, Kingman Reef, Johnston Island, or Guam.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 716 (PDF, 39 MB); June 30, 1955, ch. 258, §2(c), 69 Stat. 242; Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §§330004(18), 330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2142 (PDF, 52 MB), 2147; Pub. L. 104–294, title VI, §604(b)(23), Oct. 11, 1996, 110 Stat. 3508.)

1 crime in this section

  1. Relands merchandise at any place in the United States without entry having been made

    Who
    • Each person concerned
    Federal basis
    • Relanded at any place in the United States
    Conditions
    • The merchandise was entered or withdrawn for exportation without payment of the duties thereon, or with intent to obtain a drawback of the duties paid, or of any other allowances given by law on its exportation
    • No entry has been made
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 2 years in prison, or both
    Other consequences
    • The merchandise is considered imported into the United States contrary to law
    • The merchandise shall be forfeited
    Key terms
    • "Any place in the United States": does not include the Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Wake Island, Midway Islands, Kingman Reef, Johnston Island, or Guam

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to bring back and land in the United States, without properly declaring them, goods that were sent out for export without paying import taxes or to get a tax refund. Each person involved can be charged.
