§ 1863

Trespass on national forest lands

Up to 6 months Property Crimes Ch. 91 · Public Lands

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, without lawful authority or permission, goes upon any national-forest land while it is closed to the public pursuant to lawful regulation of the Secretary of Agriculture, shall be or imprisoned not more than six months, or both.

History: (Added May 24, 1949, ch. 139, §43, 63 Stat. 95 (PDF, 5.2 MB); amended Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(G), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

The crime in this section

  1. Goes upon any national-forest land

    Federal basis
    • National-forest land closed to the public pursuant to lawful regulation of the Secretary of Agriculture
    Conditions
    • Without lawful authority or permission
    • The land is closed to the public pursuant to lawful regulation of the Secretary of Agriculture
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 6 months in prison, or both
