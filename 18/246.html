§ 246

Deprivation of relief benefits

Up to 1 year Civil Rights None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 13 · Civil Rights

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever directly or indirectly deprives, to deprive, or threatens to deprive any person of any employment, position, work, compensation, or other benefit provided for or made possible in whole or in part by any Act of Congress appropriating funds for work relief or relief purposes, on account of political affiliation, race, color, sex, religion, or national origin, shall be , or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 94–453, §4(a), Oct. 2, 1976, 90 Stat. 1517; amended Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(L), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Deprives or threatens to deprive any person of any employment, position, work, compensation, or other benefit, directly or indirectly

    Ways to commit
    • deprives · threatens to deprive
    Federal basis
    • The benefit is provided for or made possible in whole or in part by any Act of Congress appropriating funds for work relief or relief purposes
    Conditions
    • The deprivation is on account of political affiliation, race, color, sex, religion, or national origin
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both
    Also covers
    • + attempt

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to take away, try to take away, or threaten to take away a person's job, pay, or other benefit funded by federal relief or work-relief money because of their political affiliation, race, color, sex, religion, or national origin.
