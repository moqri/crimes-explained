§ 1584

Sale into involuntary servitude

Up to life Human Trafficking & Slavery None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 77 · Peonage, Slavery, and Trafficking in Persons

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever knowingly and holds to involuntary servitude or sells into any condition of involuntary servitude, any other person for any term, or brings within the United States any person so held, shall be or imprisoned not more than 20 years, or both. If death results from the violation of this section, or if the violation includes kidnapping or an attempt to kidnap, aggravated sexual abuse or the attempt to commit aggravated sexual abuse, or an attempt to kill, the defendant shall be or imprisoned for any term of years or life, or both.

(b) Whoever obstructs, to obstruct, or in any way interferes with or prevents the enforcement of this section, shall be subject to the penalties described in subsection (a).

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly hold someone in involuntary servitude (forced labor) or sell them into it, or to bring such a person into the United States. It is also a crime to interfere with enforcement of this law.
