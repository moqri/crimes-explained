§ 2722

Additional unlawful acts

See penalty details Cyber, Privacy & Communications None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 123 · Prohibition On Release and Use of Certain Personal Information From State Motor Vehicle Records

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Procurement for Unlawful Purpose. It shall be unlawful for any person knowingly to obtain or disclose personal information, from a motor vehicle record, for any use not permitted under section 2721(b) of this title.

(b) False Representation. It shall be unlawful for any person to make false representation to obtain any personal information from an individual's motor vehicle record.

Simplified explanation

It is illegal to knowingly get or share personal information from a motor vehicle record for a use the law does not allow. It is also illegal to lie to get personal information from someone's motor vehicle record. This applies to any person.
