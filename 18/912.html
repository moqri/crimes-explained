§ 912

Officer or employee of the United States

Up to 3 years Government & Corruption 15 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 43 · False Personation

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever falsely assumes or pretends to be an officer or employee acting under the authority of the United States or any department, agency or officer thereof, and acts as such, or in such pretended character demands or obtains any money, paper, document, or , shall be or imprisoned not more than three years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 742 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

2 crimes in this section

  1. Falsely assumes or pretends to be an officer or employee acting under the authority of the United States, and acts as such

    Ways to commit
    • assumes · pretends

  2. In such pretended character demands or obtains any money, paper, document, or thing of value

    Ways to commit
    • demands · obtains
    Conditions
    • The person falsely assumes or pretends to be an officer or employee acting under the authority of the United States
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Federal basis
  • The impersonation is of an officer or employee acting under the authority of the United States or any department, agency or officer thereof
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 3 years in prison, or both
