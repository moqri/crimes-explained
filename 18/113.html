§ 113

Assaults within maritime and territorial jurisdiction

(7) Assault resulting in substantial to a spouse or intimate partner, a dating partner, or an individual who has not attained the age of 16 years, by a or imprisonment for not more than 5 years, or both.

(8) Assault of a spouse, intimate partner, or dating partner by strangling, suffocating, or attempting to strangle or suffocate, by a , imprisonment for not more than 10 years, or both.

(b) Definitions. In this section—

(1) the term "substantial " means which involves—

(A) a temporary but substantial disfigurement; or

(B) a temporary but substantial loss or impairment of the function of any bodily member, organ, or mental faculty;

(2) the term "" has the meaning given that term in section 1365 of this title;

(3) the terms "dating partner" and "spouse or intimate partner" have the meanings [1] given those terms in section 2266;

(4) the term "strangling" means intentionally, knowingly, or impeding the normal breathing or circulation of the blood of a person by applying pressure to the throat or neck, regardless of whether that conduct results in any visible injury or whether there is any intent to kill or protractedly injure the victim; and

(5) the term "suffocating" means intentionally, knowingly, or impeding the normal breathing of a person by covering the mouth of the person, the nose of the person, or both, regardless of whether that conduct results in any visible injury or whether there is any intent to kill or protractedly injure the victim.

8 crimes in this section

  1. (a)(1) Assault

    Intent
    • With intent to commit murder or a violation of section 2241 or 2242
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 20 years in prison, or both

  2. (a)(2) Assault

    Intent
    • With intent to commit any felony
    Exceptions
    • Murder or a violation of section 2241 or 2242 (covered by (a)(1))
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both

  3. (a)(3) Assault with a dangerous weapon

    Intent
    • With intent to do bodily harm
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both

  4. (a)(4) Assault by striking, beating, or wounding

    Ways to commit
    • striking · beating · wounding
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both

  5. (a)(5) Simple assault

    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 6 months in prison, or both
    • If the victim of the assault is an individual who has not attained the age of 16 years: A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both

  6. (a)(6) Assault

    Conditions
    • The assault results in serious bodily injury
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both

  7. (a)(7) Assault

    Conditions

    • The victim is a spouse or intimate partner, a dating partner, or an individual who has not attained the age of 16 years
    Penalty

  8. (a)(8) Assault by strangling, suffocating, or attempting to strangle or suffocate

    Ways to commit
    • strangling · suffocating · attempting to strangle · attempting to suffocate
    Conditions
    • The victim is a spouse, intimate partner, or dating partner
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both
Applies to all 8 crimes above
Federal basis
  • Within the special maritime and territorial jurisdiction of the United States
Key terms
  • "substantial bodily injury": bodily injury involving a temporary but substantial disfigurement, or a temporary but substantial loss or impairment of the function of any bodily member, organ, or mental faculty
  • "serious bodily injury": as defined in section 1365
  • "dating partner" and "spouse or intimate partner": as defined in section 2266
  • "strangling": intentionally, knowingly, or recklessly impeding the normal breathing or circulation of the blood of a person by applying pressure to the throat or neck, regardless of visible injury or intent to kill or protractedly injure
  • "suffocating": intentionally, knowingly, or recklessly impeding the normal breathing of a person by covering the mouth, the nose, or both, regardless of visible injury or intent to kill or protractedly injure

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to commit an assault within special federal jurisdiction (such as federal lands, military bases, or U.S. ships). The punishment depends on the type of assault, from simple assault to assault with intent to murder.
