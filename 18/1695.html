§ 1695

Carriage of matter out of mail on vessels

Up to 29 days Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever carries any letter or packet on board any vessel which carries the mail, otherwise than in such mail, shall, except as otherwise provided by law, be or imprisoned not more than thirty days, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 777 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(A), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2146 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Carries any letter or packet on board any vessel which carries the mail, otherwise than in such mail

    Federal basis
    • The vessel carries the mail
    Exceptions
    • The penalty applies except as otherwise provided by law
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 30 days in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to carry a letter or package on a ship that carries the mail, other than in the mail itself, unless another law allows it.
