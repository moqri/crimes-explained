§ 2111

Special maritime and territorial jurisdiction

Up to 15 years Violence & Threats Ch. 103 · Robbery and Burglary

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, within the , by force and violence, or by intimidation, takes or to take from the person or presence of another anything of value, shall be imprisoned not more than fifteen years.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 796 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXII, §320903(a)(1), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2124 (PDF, 52 MB).)
