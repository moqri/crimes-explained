§ 592

Troops at polls

Up to 5 years Elections & Voting None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 29 · Elections and Political Activities

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being an officer of the Army or Navy, or other person in the civil, military, or naval service of the United States, orders, brings, keeps, or has under his authority or control any troops or armed men at any place where a general or special election is held, unless such force be necessary to repel armed enemies of the United States, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both; and be disqualified from holding any office of honor, profit, or trust under the United States.

This section shall not prevent any officer or member of the armed forces of the United States from exercising the right of suffrage in any election district to which he may belong, if otherwise qualified according to the laws of the State in which he offers to vote.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 719 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Orders, brings, keeps, or has under his authority or control any troops or armed men at any place where a general or special election is held

    Ways to commit
    • orders · brings · keeps · has under his authority or control
    Who
    • An officer of the Army or Navy, or other person in the civil, military, or naval service of the United States
    Federal basis
    • The offender is an officer or other person in the civil, military, or naval service of the United States
    Exceptions
    • Such force is necessary to repel armed enemies of the United States
    • Does not prevent any officer or member of the armed forces from exercising the right of suffrage in any election district to which he may belong, if otherwise qualified under State law
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both
    Other consequences
    • Disqualified from holding any office of honor, profit, or trust under the United States

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a military officer or other federal employee to order, bring, or keep troops or armed men at a place where an election is being held. The only exception is when force is needed to fight off armed enemies of the United States.
