§ 832

Participation in nuclear and weapons of mass destruction threats to the United States

Up to life Weapons & Terrorism None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 39 · Explosives and Other Dangerous Articles

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever, within the United States or subject to the jurisdiction of the United States, participates in or knowingly provides material support or resources (as defined in section 2339A) to a nuclear weapons program or other weapons of mass destruction program of a foreign terrorist power, or or to do so, shall be imprisoned for not more than 20 years.

(b) There is extraterritorial Federal jurisdiction over an offense under this section.

(c) Whoever without lawful authority develops, possesses, or or to develop or possess a radiological weapon, or threatens to use or uses a radiological weapon against any person within the United States, or a national of the United States while such national is outside of the United States or against any property that is owned, leased, funded, or used by the United States, whether that property is within or outside of the United States, shall be imprisoned for any term of years or for life.

(d)

As used in this section—

(1) "nuclear weapons program" means a program or plan for the development, acquisition, or production of any nuclear weapon or weapons;

(2) "weapons of mass destruction program" means a program or plan for the development, acquisition, or production of any weapon or weapons of mass destruction (as defined in section 2332a(c));

(3) "foreign terrorist power" means a terrorist organization designated under section 219 of the Immigration and Nationality Act, or a state sponsor of terrorism designated under section 6(j) [1] of the Export Administration Act of 1979 or section 620A of the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961; and

(4) "nuclear weapon" means any weapon that contains or uses nuclear material as defined in section 831(f)(1).[1]

History: (Added Pub. L. 108–458, title VI, §6803(c)(2), Dec. 17, 2004, 118 Stat. 3768.)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to take part in, or knowingly give material support (such as money, equipment, or help) to, a nuclear or other weapons of mass destruction program run by a foreign terrorist group or state sponsor of terrorism. It is also a crime, without lawful authority, to develop, possess, use, or threaten to use a radiological weapon (a "dirty bomb") against people in the U.S., U.S. citizens abroad, or U.S. property. Attempts and conspiracies are covered.
