§ 1716C

Forged agricultural certifications

Up to 1 year Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever forges or counterfeits any certification authorized under any rules or regulations prescribed under section 3014(c) of title 39 with intent to make it appear that such is a genuine certification, or makes or knowingly uses or sells, or possesses with intent to use or sell, any forged or counterfeited certification so authorized, or device for imprinting any such certification, shall be , or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (Added Pub. L. 100–574, §2(a), Oct. 31, 1988, 102 Stat. 2893.)

3 crimes in this section

  1. Forges or counterfeits any certification authorized under rules or regulations prescribed under section 3014(c) of title 39

  3. Possesses any forged or counterfeited certification so authorized, or device for imprinting any such certification

Applies to all 3 crimes above
Federal basis
  • The certification is one authorized under rules or regulations prescribed under section 3014(c) of title 39
Penalty
  • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to forge or fake the agricultural certifications used for mailing plants under Postal Service rules. It is also a crime to make or knowingly use or sell fakes or devices for printing them, or to possess them intending to use or sell them.
