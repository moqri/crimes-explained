§ 611

Voting by aliens

Up to 1 year Elections & Voting None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 29 · Elections and Political Activities

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) It shall be unlawful for any alien to vote in any election held solely or in part for the purpose of electing a candidate for the office of President, Vice President, Presidential elector, Member of the Senate, Member of the House of Representatives, Delegate from the District of Columbia, or Resident Commissioner, unless—

(1) the election is held partly for some other purpose;

(2) aliens are authorized to vote for such other purpose under a State constitution or statute or a local ordinance; and

(3) voting for such other purpose is conducted independently of voting for a candidate for such Federal offices, in such a manner that an alien has the opportunity to vote for such other purpose, but not an opportunity to vote for a candidate for any one or more of such Federal offices.

(b) Any person who violates this section shall be , imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

(c) Subsection (a) does not apply to an alien if—

(1) each natural parent of the alien (or, in the case of an adopted alien, each adoptive parent of the alien) is or was a citizen (whether by birth or naturalization);

(2) the alien permanently resided in the United States prior to attaining the age of 16; and

(3) the alien reasonably believed at the time of voting in violation of such subsection that he or she was a citizen of the United States.

History: (Added Pub. L. 104–208, div. C, title II, §216(a), Sept. 30, 1996, 110 Stat. 3009–572; amended Pub. L. 106–395, title II, §201(d)(1), Oct. 30, 2000, 114 Stat. 1635.)

1 crime in this section

  1. (a) Votes in any election held solely or in part for the purpose of electing a candidate for the office of President, Vice President, Presidential elector, Member of the Senate, Member of the House of Representatives, Delegate from the District of Columbia, or Resident Commissioner

    Who
    • An alien
    Federal basis
    • The election is held solely or in part for the purpose of electing a candidate for the office of President, Vice President, Presidential elector, Member of the Senate, Member of the House of Representatives, Delegate from the District of Columbia, or Resident Commissioner
    Exceptions
    • (a)(1)-(3): the election is held partly for some other purpose, aliens are authorized to vote for that purpose under a State constitution or statute or a local ordinance, and voting for that purpose is conducted independently so that the alien has no opportunity to vote for a candidate for such Federal offices
    • (c): does not apply to an alien if each natural parent (or, if adopted, each adoptive parent) is or was a citizen, the alien permanently resided in the United States prior to attaining the age of 16, and the alien reasonably believed at the time of voting that he or she was a citizen of the United States
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 1 year in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a non-citizen to vote in an election for a federal office, such as President or Congress. Non-citizens may vote on local matters on the same ballot day only if State or local law allows it and the voting is kept separate from federal races.
