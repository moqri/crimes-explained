§ 2245

Offenses resulting in death

Death penalty possible Violence & Threats None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 109A · Sexual Abuse

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) [1] In General.—A person who, in the course of an offense under this chapter, or section 1591, 2251, 2251A, 2260, 2421, 2422, 2423, or 2425, murders an individual, shall be punished by death or imprisoned for any term of years or for life.

History: (Added Pub. L. 103–322, title VI, §60010(a)(2), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 1972 (PDF, 52 MB); amended Pub. L. 109–248, title II, §206(a)(3), July 27, 2006, 120 Stat. 613.)

1 So in original. No subsec. (b) has been enacted.

1 crime in this section

  1. (a) Murders an individual

    Federal basis
    • In the course of an offense under chapter 109A, or section 1591, 2251, 2251A, 2260, 2421, 2422, 2423, or 2425
    Conditions
    • In the course of an offense under this chapter, or section 1591, 2251, 2251A, 2260, 2421, 2422, 2423, or 2425
    Penalty
    • Death or imprisonment for any term of years or for life

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to murder someone while committing a federal sexual abuse crime or certain related crimes, such as sex trafficking, sexual exploitation of children, or transporting people for illegal sexual activity.
