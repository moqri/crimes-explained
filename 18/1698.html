§ 1698

Prompt delivery of mail from vessel

Fine only Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, having charge or control of any vessel passing between ports or places in the United States, and arriving at any such port or place where there is a post office, fails to deliver to the postmaster or at the post office, within three hours after his arrival, if in the daytime, and if at night, within two hours after the next sunrise, all letters and packages brought by him or within his power or control and not relating to the cargo, addressed to or destined for such port or place, shall be .

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 777 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §§330004(10), 330016(1)(C), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2141 (PDF, 52 MB), 2146.)

1 crime in this section

  1. Fails to deliver to the postmaster or at the post office all letters and packages brought by him or within his power or control and not relating to the cargo, addressed to or destined for such port or place

    Who
    • A person having charge or control of any vessel passing between ports or places in the United States
    Federal basis
    • The vessel passes between ports or places in the United States and arrives at a port or place where there is a post office
    Conditions
    • The vessel arrives at a port or place where there is a post office
    • Delivery is not made within three hours after his arrival, if in the daytime, and if at night, within two hours after the next sunrise
    Penalty
    • A fine

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for the person in charge of a ship traveling between U.S. ports to fail to deliver letters and packages for that port to the post office within 3 hours of daytime arrival (or 2 hours after the next sunrise if arriving at night).
