§ 1112

Manslaughter

Up to 15 years Violence & Threats 87 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 51 · Homicide

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Manslaughter is the unlawful killing of a human being without malice. It is of two kinds:

Voluntary—Upon a sudden quarrel or heat of passion.

Involuntary—In the commission of an unlawful act not amounting to a , or in the commission in an unlawful manner, or without due caution and circumspection, of a lawful act which might produce death.

(b) Within the ,

Whoever is guilty of voluntary manslaughter, shall be or imprisoned not more than 15 years, or both;

Whoever is guilty of involuntary manslaughter, shall be or imprisoned not more than 8 years, or both.

2 crimes in this section

  1. (a) Unlawfully kills a human being without malice (voluntary manslaughter)

    Conditions
    • Upon a sudden quarrel or heat of passion
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 15 years in prison, or both

  2. (a) Unlawfully kills a human being without malice (involuntary manslaughter)

    Ways to commit
    • in the commission of an unlawful act not amounting to a felony · in the commission in an unlawful manner of a lawful act which might produce death · in the commission without due caution and circumspection of a lawful act which might produce death
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 8 years in prison, or both
Applies to all 2 crimes above
Federal basis
  • Within the special maritime and territorial jurisdiction of the United States
