§ 2319

Criminal infringement of a copyright

Up to 10 years Property Crimes 5 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 113 · Stolen Property

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Any person who violates section 506(a) (relating to criminal offenses) of title 17 shall be punished as provided in subsections (b), (c), and (d) and such penalties shall be in addition to any other provisions of title 17 or any other law.

(b) Any person who commits an offense under section 506(a)(1)(A) of title 17—

(1) shall be imprisoned not more than 5 years, or fined in the amount set forth in this title, or both, if the offense consists of the reproduction or distribution, including by electronic means, during any 180-day period, of at least 10 copies or phonorecords, of 1 or more copyrighted works, which have a total retail value of more than $2,500;

(2) shall be imprisoned not more than 10 years, or fined in the amount set forth in this title, or both, if the offense is a and is a second or subsequent offense under subsection (a); and

(3) shall be imprisoned not more than 1 year, or fined in the amount set forth in this title, or both, in any other case.

(c) Any person who commits an offense under section 506(a)(1)(B) of title 17—

(1) shall be imprisoned not more than 3 years, or fined in the amount set forth in this title, or both, if the offense consists of the reproduction or distribution of 10 or more copies or phonorecords of 1 or more copyrighted works, which have a total retail value of $2,500 or more;

(2) shall be imprisoned not more than 6 years, or fined in the amount set forth in this title, or both, if the offense is a and is a second or subsequent offense under subsection (a); and

(3) shall be imprisoned not more than 1 year, or fined in the amount set forth in this title, or both, if the offense consists of the reproduction or distribution of 1 or more copies or phonorecords of 1 or more copyrighted works, which have a total retail value of more than $1,000.

(d) Any person who commits an offense under section 506(a)(1)(C) of title 17—

(1) shall be imprisoned not more than 3 years, , or both;

(2) shall be imprisoned not more than 5 years, , or both, if the offense was committed for purposes of commercial advantage or private financial gain;

(3) shall be imprisoned not more than 6 years, , or both, if the offense is a and is a second or subsequent offense under subsection (a); and

(4) shall be imprisoned not more than 10 years, , or both, if the offense is a and is a second or subsequent offense under paragraph (2).

(e)

(1) During preparation of the presentence report pursuant to Rule 32(c) of the Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure, victims of the offense shall be permitted to submit, and the probation officer shall receive, a victim impact statement that identifies the victim of the offense and the extent and scope of the injury and loss suffered by the victim, including the estimated economic impact of the offense on that victim.

(2) Persons permitted to submit victim impact statements shall include—

(A) producers and sellers of legitimate works affected by conduct involved in the offense;

(B) holders of intellectual property rights in such works; and

(C) the legal representatives of such producers, sellers, and holders.

(f)

As used in this section—

(1) the terms "phonorecord" and "copies" have, respectively, the meanings set forth in section 101 (relating to definitions) of title 17;

(2) the terms "reproduction" and "distribution" refer to the exclusive rights of a copyright owner under clauses (1) and (3) respectively of section 106 (relating to exclusive rights in copyrighted works), as limited by sections 107 through 122, of title 17;

(3) the term "financial gain" has the meaning given the term in section 101 of title 17; and

(4) the term "work being prepared for commercial distribution" has the meaning given the term in section 506(a) of title 17.

3 crimes in this section

  1. (b) Commits an offense under section 506(a)(1)(A) of title 17

    Penalty
    • Up to 1 year in prison, a fine in the amount set forth in this title, or both (in any other case)
    • If the offense consists of the reproduction or distribution, including by electronic means, during any 180-day period, of at least 10 copies or phonorecords, of 1 or more copyrighted works, which have a total retail value of more than $2,500: Up to 5 years in prison, a fine in the amount set forth in this title, or both
    • If the offense is a felony and is a second or subsequent offense under subsection (a): Up to 10 years in prison, a fine in the amount set forth in this title, or both

  2. (c) Commits an offense under section 506(a)(1)(B) of title 17

    Penalty
    • Up to 1 year in prison, a fine in the amount set forth in this title, or both (where the offense consists of the reproduction or distribution of 1 or more copies or phonorecords of 1 or more copyrighted works with a total retail value of more than $1,000)
    • If the offense consists of the reproduction or distribution of 10 or more copies or phonorecords of 1 or more copyrighted works, which have a total retail value of $2,500 or more: Up to 3 years in prison, a fine in the amount set forth in this title, or both
    • If the offense is a felony and is a second or subsequent offense under subsection (a): Up to 6 years in prison, a fine in the amount set forth in this title, or both

  3. (d) Commits an offense under section 506(a)(1)(C) of title 17

    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 3 years in prison, or both
    • If the offense was committed for purposes of commercial advantage or private financial gain: A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both
    • If the offense is a felony and is a second or subsequent offense under subsection (a): A fine, up to 6 years in prison, or both
    • If the offense is a felony and is a second or subsequent offense under paragraph (2) (commercial advantage or private financial gain): A fine, up to 10 years in prison, or both
Applies to all 3 crimes above
Federal basis
  • The offense is a violation of section 506(a) of title 17
Other consequences
  • These penalties are in addition to any other provisions of title 17 or any other law
Key terms
  • "Phonorecord" and "copies": meanings in section 101 of title 17
  • "Reproduction" and "distribution": the copyright owner's exclusive rights under section 106(1) and (3) of title 17, as limited by sections 107 through 122
  • "Financial gain": meaning in section 101 of title 17
  • "Work being prepared for commercial distribution": meaning in section 506(a) of title 17

Simplified explanation

This section sets the criminal punishments for willful copyright infringement, which is defined in section 506(a) of title 17 (the copyright law). The punishment depends on how many copies were made or shared, their value, whether it was done for profit, and whether it was a repeat offense.
