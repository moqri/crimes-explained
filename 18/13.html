§ 13

Laws of States adopted for areas within Federal jurisdiction

See penalty details Courts & Justice 638 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 1 · General Provisions

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever within or upon any of the places now existing or hereafter reserved or acquired as provided in section 7 of this title, or on, above, or below any portion of the territorial sea of the United States not within the jurisdiction of any State, Commonwealth, territory, possession, or district is guilty of any act or omission which, although not made punishable by any enactment of Congress, would be punishable if committed or omitted within the jurisdiction of the State, Territory, Possession, or District in which such place is situated, by the laws thereof in force at the time of such act or omission, shall be guilty of a like offense and subject to a like punishment.

(b)

(1) Subject to paragraph (2) and for purposes of subsection (a) of this section, that which may or shall be imposed through judicial or administrative action under the law of a State, territory, possession, or district, for a conviction for operating a motor vehicle under the influence of a drug or alcohol, shall be considered to be a punishment provided by that law. Any limitation on the right or privilege to operate a motor vehicle imposed under this subsection shall apply only to the .

(2)

(A) In addition to any term of imprisonment provided for operating a motor vehicle under the influence of a drug or alcohol imposed under the law of a State, territory, possession, or district, the punishment for such an offense under this section shall include an additional term of imprisonment of not more than 1 year, or if of a minor is caused, not more than 5 years, or if death of a minor is caused, not more than 10 years, and an additional , or both, if—

(i) a minor (other than the offender) was present in the motor vehicle when the offense was committed; and

(ii) the law of the State, territory, possession, or district in which the offense occurred does not provide an additional term of imprisonment under the circumstances described in clause (i).

(B) For the purposes of subparagraph (A), the term "minor" means a person less than 18 years of age.

(c) Whenever any waters of the territorial sea of the United States lie outside the territory of any State, Commonwealth, territory, possession, or district, such waters (including the airspace above and the seabed and subsoil below, and artificial islands and fixed structures erected thereon) shall be deemed, for purposes of subsection (a), to lie within the area of the State, Commonwealth, territory, possession, or district that it would lie within if the boundaries of such State, Commonwealth, territory, possession, or district were extended seaward to the outer limit of the territorial sea of the United States.

1 crime in this section

  1. (a) Is guilty of any act or omission which would be punishable if committed or omitted within the jurisdiction of the State, Territory, Possession, or District in which such place is situated, by the laws thereof in force at the time

    Federal basis
    • Within or upon any place reserved or acquired as provided in section 7 (special maritime and territorial jurisdiction), or on, above, or below any portion of the territorial sea of the United States not within the jurisdiction of any State, Commonwealth, territory, possession, or district
    Conditions
    • The act or omission is not made punishable by any enactment of Congress
    Penalty
    • A like punishment to that under the laws of the State, Territory, Possession, or District in which the place is situated, in force at the time of the act or omission
    • If the offense is operating a motor vehicle under the influence of a drug or alcohol, a minor (other than the offender) was present in the vehicle, and the State law does not provide an additional term of imprisonment for that circumstance: An additional term of up to 1 year in prison and an additional fine, or both
    • If in that drunk/drugged-driving case, serious bodily injury of a minor is caused: An additional term of up to 5 years in prison and an additional fine, or both
    • If in that drunk/drugged-driving case, death of a minor is caused: An additional term of up to 10 years in prison and an additional fine, or both
    Other consequences
    • For operating a motor vehicle under the influence, whatever may or shall be imposed through judicial or administrative action under State law counts as punishment; any limitation on the right or privilege to operate a motor vehicle applies only to the special maritime and territorial jurisdiction of the United States
    Key terms
    • "minor": a person less than 18 years of age
    • Territorial sea waters outside any State are deemed to lie within the State, Commonwealth, territory, possession, or district they would lie within if its boundaries were extended seaward to the outer limit of the territorial sea

Simplified explanation

On federal land and certain U.S. coastal waters, an act that is not a federal crime but is a crime under the law of the surrounding state becomes a federal crime with the same punishment. This section also adds extra punishment for drunk or drugged driving when a child is in the vehicle.
