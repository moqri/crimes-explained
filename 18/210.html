§ 210

Offer to procure appointive public office

Up to 1 year Government & Corruption None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 11 · Bribery, Graft, and Conflicts of Interest

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever pays or offers or promises any money or , to any person, firm, or corporation in consideration of the use or promise to use any influence to procure any appointive office or place under the United States for any person, shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 694 (PDF, 39 MB), §210, formerly §214; renumbered §210, Pub. L. 87–849, §1(b), Oct. 23, 1962, 76 Stat. 1125 (PDF, 1.6 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(H), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)
