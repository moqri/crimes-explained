§ 482

Foreign bank notes

Up to 20 years Fraud & Money None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 25 · Counterfeiting and Forgery

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, within the United States, with intent to defraud, falsely makes, alters, forges, or counterfeits any bank note or bill issued by a bank or corporation of any foreign country, and intended by the law or usage of such foreign country to circulate as money, such bank or corporation being authorized by the laws of such country, shall be or imprisoned not more than 20 years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 708 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(I), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB); Pub. L. 107–56, title III, §375(e), Oct. 26, 2001, 115 Stat. 342.)

1 crime in this section

  1. Falsely makes, alters, forges, or counterfeits any bank note or bill issued by a bank or corporation of any foreign country

    Ways to commit
    • falsely makes · alters · forges · counterfeits
    Federal basis
    • Within the United States
    Intent
    • With intent to defraud
    Conditions
    • The note or bill is intended by the law or usage of the foreign country to circulate as money
    • The bank or corporation is authorized by the laws of that country
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 20 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime, within the United States, to make, alter, or forge bank notes issued by a foreign bank that are meant to be used as money, with intent to defraud.
