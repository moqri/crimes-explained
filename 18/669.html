§ 669

Theft or embezzlement in connection with health care

Up to 10 years Property Crimes 12 sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 31 · Embezzlement and Theft

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

(a) Whoever knowingly and , steals, or otherwise without authority converts to the use of any person other than the rightful owner, or intentionally misapplies any of the moneys, funds, securities, premiums, credits, property, or other assets of a health care benefit program, shall be or imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both; but if the value of such property does not exceed the sum of $100 the defendant shall be or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.

(b)

As used in this section, the term "health care benefit program" has the meaning given such term in section 24(b) of this title.

History: (Added Pub. L. 104–191, title II, §243(a), Aug. 21, 1996, 110 Stat. 2017 (PDF, 25 MB).)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to knowingly and willfully embezzle, steal, or misuse money or property of a health care benefit program (such as a health insurance plan).
