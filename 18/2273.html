§ 2273

Destruction of vessel by nonowner

Up to 10 years Transportation & Aviation None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 111 · Shipping

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, not being an owner, upon the high seas or on any other waters within the admiralty and maritime jurisdiction of the United States, and casts away or otherwise destroys any vessel of the United States to which he belongs, or the destruction thereof, shall be imprisoned not more than ten years.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 804 (PDF, 39 MB).)

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for someone who is not an owner to deliberately sink or destroy a U.S. ship they belong to (such as a crew member), on the high seas or other waters under U.S. maritime authority. Attempting to do so is also a crime.
