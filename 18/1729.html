§ 1729

Post office conducted without authority

Fine only Mail & Postal None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 83 · Postal Service

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime to set up or claim to run a business or office using the sign, name, or title "post office" without Postal Service permission.
