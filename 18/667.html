§ 667

Theft of livestock

Up to 5 years Property Crimes Ch. 31 · Embezzlement and Theft

Official text prohibited actknowledgeintentif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever obtains or uses the property of another which has a value of $10,000 or more in connection with the marketing of livestock in with intent to deprive the other of a right to the property or a benefit of the property or to appropriate the property to his own use or the use of another shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both. The term "livestock" has the meaning set forth in section 2311 of this title.

History: (Added Pub. L. 98–473, title II, §1111, Oct. 12, 1984, 98 Stat. 2149 (PDF, 62 MB); amended Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §§330009(b), 330016(1)(L), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2143 (PDF, 52 MB), 2147.)
