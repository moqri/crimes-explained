§ 1421

Accounts of court officers

Up to 5 years Immigration & Citizenship None sentenced (FY2025) Ch. 69 · Nationality and Citizenship

Official text prohibited actmental stateif conditionpenaltylegal term

Whoever, being a clerk or assistant clerk of a court, or other person charged by law with a duty to render true accounts of moneys received in any proceeding relating to citizenship, naturalization, or registration of aliens or to pay over any balance of such moneys due to the United States, neglects to do so within thirty days after said payment shall become due and demand therefor has been made, shall be or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.

History: (June 25, 1948, ch. 645, 62 Stat. 766 (PDF, 39 MB); Pub. L. 103–322, title XXXIII, §330016(1)(K), Sept. 13, 1994, 108 Stat. 2147 (PDF, 52 MB).)

1 crime in this section

  1. Neglects to render true accounts of such moneys or to pay over any balance of such moneys due to the United States

    Who
    • A clerk or assistant clerk of a court, or other person charged by law with a duty to render true accounts of moneys received in any proceeding relating to citizenship, naturalization, or registration of aliens or to pay over any balance of such moneys due to the United States
    Federal basis
    • Moneys received in proceedings relating to citizenship, naturalization, or registration of aliens, and balances due to the United States
    Mental state
    • Willfully
    Conditions
    • Within thirty days after the payment becomes due and demand therefor has been made
    Penalty
    • A fine, up to 5 years in prison, or both

Simplified explanation

It is a federal crime for a court clerk or other official responsible for money collected in citizenship, naturalization, or alien registration proceedings to willfully fail to account for or pay that money to the United States. The failure must last more than 30 days after the money is due and has been demanded.
